## Meter
The meter above the note is the first thing anyone sees, so players pick one of six looks (setting `meterLook`), in the owner's order: *Classic* (default), *Light arc*, *Spirit level*, *Eclipse*, *Arrows*, *Light bars*. Every look shows the note its own way (the approved mock-up [docs/design/meter-looks-round2-preview.html](../design/meter-looks-round2-preview.html)); only Classic has a numbered scale. A saved look that no longer exists (*Rolling scale*, *Comet*, *Studio meter*) reads as Classic.
- **One frame** (`:feature:tuner` `components/MeterFrame.kt`): every look sits in `MeterFrame`, so changing looks moves nothing else. It owns the 2:1 box (`METER_ASPECT_RATIO`; each look draws its ♭ and ♯ inside it, at its scale's ends), the ±50-cent range (beyond it the marker pins at the edge, `meterCents`; without a reading it rests in the centre at 0.3 opacity), the look's spring (`meterFeel(look)`), the in-tune amount `tuned` (smoothstep, 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out), the in-tune `glow`, the reception fade and blur, and `clearAndSetSemantics {}` (`NoteDisplay` speaks the reading). It hands the look one `MeterReading`: cents, `velocity` (from the spring, without boxing), `presence`, `tuned`, `glow` and `applyMarkerLook(layer)`, read only in layer and draw phases.
- **Art in the design system** (`:core:designsystem` `meter/`): `MeterLookArt` draws each look, so the tuner and the settings cards draw exactly the same thing and `:feature:settings` needs no `:feature:tuner`. A part that only turns or slides gets its own `graphicsLayer` and only changes its transform; a part that changes shape is redrawn in its `Canvas` from paths, brushes and strokes made once. The art is not clipped: the calm field and the glow fade out past the box (up to ~110u). The settings cards clip their preview to its box so the names stay clean.
- **Shared pieces** (`meter/MeterArt.kt`, named like the mock-up's helpers): `MeterRoles` (the colour roles and number face), `FieldArt` (`meterField`, the same calm field under every look, so switching looks changes nothing round the box), `ContactShadowArt`, `GlassArt`, `ScaleMarks` (with `numberFit`, which keeps neighbouring numbers 8u apart by setting them up to 30 % smaller), the style's finish (chosen in `meter/MeterFinish.kt`, drawn by `NeedleArt`, `HubArt` and `TrackArt`), `SignsArt` (♭ and ♯), `markerShadowPaint`, `GlowArt`, `StaticArt`, plus `mix` and `blurRadius`. Everything is built in `drawWithCache`, so only a size or colour change rebuilds it; drawing allocates nothing. Shadows need hardware-accelerated `BlurMaskFilter` (Android 9+); on Android 8 the meter has no shadows. `meter/MeterLooks.kt` holds Classic and its dial (`DialGeometry`, `DialUnder`, `DialCentre`).
- **The looks:**
  - *Classic:* the dial with the style's needle; needle and shadow drawn once and only rotated by their layers, the style's hub over the pivot in a layer that doesn't turn (it redraws only while it fades to "in tune"). Slightly underdamped, so it swings ~9 % past a new string and settles within a second, like a mechanical needle. The unit "CENTS" (`meter_cents_label`, translated) sits 42u above the pivot under the needle, 7.5u in the number face with 0.3 em letter spacing, muted at 90 % (`CentsLabelArt`).
  - *Light arc* (`LightArc.kt`): a fan of little lights like an LED pedal's, no dial and no numbers. 17 rays (one every 6.25 cents, ±50 at ±1.2 rad) from a pivot 6u under the box, each of 5 lights 78u to 148u out (3.4u to 5.6u across), unlit in the track colour (75 % dark, 70 % light) like dark LEDs. The rays from the centre out to the reading light as a wedge (`lightArcGoal`): the reading's ray all five lights at full, rays nearer the centre fewer (down to 1.6) and dimmer (to 40 %); the ray just past the reading lights part of the way, so the wedge grows smoothly. A lit light is the accent (a little whiter in dark), 8 % larger, with a halo 3.4× its radius. The middle ray is the centre mark in its own layer: its two inner lights at 45 % and the rest at 14 % off the note, all five in the centre colour as it's reached, and the rays beside it light half way. `LightArcHeat` (one preallocated array of 85) gives a 0.22 s fading trail; its frame loop (`withFrameNanos`) runs only while a light is still changing, then waits on `snapshotFlow`, so a steady reading moves nothing and UI tests settle. ♭ and ♯ in the box's lower corners.
  - *Spirit level* (`SpiritLevel.kt`): a big straight vial (86 % of the box wide, 38 % high, round ends, its middle 45 % down) of liquid tinted by the accent, with no scale or numbers. The bubble stays at the vial's middle height and slides 92u for 50 cents (pinned at ±52); it is drawn once and its layer only moves and stretches it (up to 25 % while moving fast, from `velocity`). In tune it rests between two lines etched 3u clear of its ends, so a cent or two off it touches one; the lines fade to "in tune". A highlight streak along the upper inside; ♭ and ♯ under the vial's ends. No drop shadow (air in liquid).
  - *Eclipse* (`Eclipse.kt`): the target is the sun (44u, the accent lit from its upper left, with a halo 2.4× its radius) in the middle, 47 % down; the note is the moon (45.5u, a disc of the background colour with a faint ink rim) sliding along a dotted path, 118u for 50 cents (pinned at ±52). They start to overlap within about ±38 cents; in tune the moon covers the sun whole. At rest the moon fades out with `presence` and the sun is the muted colour dimmed 45 % toward the background (a third set of brushes), so a meter with no note never shows a total eclipse. The moon is drawn once and only moved by its layer. As the note is reached the sun blends 60 % to "in tune" (two sets of brushes made once, cross-faded), its halo dims and the moon's rim turns "in tune"; the in-tune glow is a corona round the moon (a ring from its edge to 2.1× its radius at 1.9× the usual glow strength, in its own layer moved with the moon, alpha from `glow`, larger and stronger in Bold). ♭ and ♯ under the path's ends.
  - *Arrows* (`Arrows.kt`, after a pedal tuner's converging wedges): four chevrons each side (tips 26u + 30u·k from the middle, 7 % smaller each, 7.5u round strokes) point at a 9u × 100u centre bar. On the side the note is off they light one after another, the inner one first, each over 10.5 cents from 2 cents off (`arrowLight`), in the accent with a halo of themselves 2.2× as wide; the other side and unlit chevrons are the track colour. In tune the inner pair lights at 90 % in the centre colour and the bar (outlined in the centre colour, filled 18 %) fills in. No shimmer (the mock-up had one): a steady reading must draw nothing new, and UI tests must settle.
  - *Light bars* (`LightBars.kt`, after a pedal tuner's display): 41 bars (one every 2.5 cents, ±150u, 3.6u round), mirrored about the middle line, 5u each way when dark (the centre bar 12u, part way to the accent). A Gaussian peak (σ 4.5 cents, widening to 6.5 as the note is reached; `lightBarPeak`) round the reading grows the bars by up to 62u each way, lit in the accent at 1.25× their share, fading to 30 % toward their tips (one vertical brush per colour made once), with a soft accent halo (72u) under the peak that gives way to the in-tune glow; near the middle the lit bars blend to "in tune" as the note is reached. The peak is pinned at ±51.5 cents, so the end bar stays lit past the range.
- **Feel per look** (`meter/MeterFeel.kt`, frequency / damping ratio, stiffness = (2π f)²): Classic 1.6 Hz / 0.6, Light arc 2.2 / 0.9, Spirit level 1.0 / 0.5, Eclipse 1.2 / 0.8, Arrows 2.0 / 0.9, Light bars 1.8 / 0.8.
- **The in-tune moment** (owner: smooth, not gimmicky): a soft glow eases in with the haptic tick, holds while the note rings and fades when it leaves.
  - *When:* `InTuneGlowRule` (pure) lights it on the update that raises `Detected.inTune`, never restarts it while the note holds, and bridges a dropout of the same note for 600 ms (shorter than the tick's 1.5 s, so a bridged dropout never ticks twice). Another note or drifting out of tune puts it out at once, and so does the same note coming back while the hint asks to tune it (a re-pluck often starts sharp), so the glow never lights with "Tune down" on screen. `rememberInTuneGlow` runs it in `TunerScreen` (saved over rotation) → `TunerContent(meterGlow)` → `MeterFrame(glow)`, eased 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out.
  - *Remove animations:* `rememberAnimationsOff()` (`:core:designsystem`, `AnimationsOff.kt`, shared with Settings' look cards) reads `ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE` and follows changes; at 0 `tuned` and `glow` snap.
  - *Drawing:* the shared `TuneGlow`, a `GlowArt` in the "in tune" colour drawn once in its own layer under the marker; `glow` only sets the layer's alpha (`CompositingStrategy.ModulateAlpha`), so easing it redraws and allocates nothing. Never clipped: it fades out past the box, behind the controls around the meter.
  - *Calm or Bold* (`InTuneEffect`): Bold is the same glow and timing at 1.7 × the alpha, capped at 52 % (`BOLD_GLOW_MAX_ALPHA`) so the scale stays readable, and 1.35 × the radius (`BOLD_GLOW_SIZE`). It travels into `meterRoles(effect)`, so changing it rebuilds each look's cached art once and is never read per frame.
- **Picker** (setting "Meter", above the display choice): `MeterLookPicker` draws each look's `MeterLookArt` in a 3 × 2 grid of cards (two to a row from 1.3× font). It shares `LookCardGrid` with `WaveLookPicker`: the ring on the picked card, names on one line shrinking to 6 sp (French "Barres lumineuses" in Classic at 2× text), one selectable radio group for TalkBack, test tags, and `LookDemo`, an infinite animation (UI tests skip it). With "Remove animations" the cards hold still on the loop's first second (`holdStill`: the meter 18 cents flat, the wave drawn, the style strips in place), or where they were when it is switched on, and move again when it is off. The cards play one loop (`MeterDemo`): 18 cents flat, tuned up slowing like a peg, in tune, drifting 14 sharp, tuned down. Each card previews the chosen in-tune effect.
- **Tests:** JVM tests for each look's maths (`MeterReadingTest`, `MeterFeelTest`, `LightArcHeatTest`, `SpiritLevelTest`, `EclipseTest`, `ArrowsTest`, `LightBarsTest`, `TuneGlowTest`, `InTuneGlowRuleTest`, `MeterDemoTest`, `NumberFitTest`, `MeterFinishTest`). UI: `MeterLooksTest` (every look × style × light/dark: clearly drawn in its box, fading out around it with no edge at the box and nothing left at 0.3 box widths, skipped by TalkBack, moves without recomposing, glows in Calm and more in Bold with Bold's glow reaching past the box, snaps with animations off), `CompactLandscapeStylesTest`, `TunerContentTest`, `SettingsContentTest`.
- **Pictures for the owner:** `scripts/meter-look-screens.sh` (~2 min) runs `MeterLookScreenshots` with `-a meterScreenshots=true` (without it the class has no cases): every look in every style, light above dark, 20 cents flat and in tune with Bold, as PNGs plus an `index.html` contact sheet in `build/owner-screens/meter-looks/`.

## Meter art language
The looks are drawn "as if by the same artist" (owner, after two rounds of moving mock-ups, October 2026). The approved mock-ups are the reference: [docs/design/meter-looks-preview.html](../design/meter-looks-preview.html) and, for the open face (no plate), [docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html](../design/theme-styles-preview.html) (their top comments list the same rules). *u* = box width / 340, about 1 dp on a phone.
- **Colours:** roles only, from the current style: background (stage), surface (plate), onSurface (ink), onSurfaceVariant (muted), outlineVariant (track), primary (accent) and the "in tune" colour. No ornaments beyond the style's finish (below). Minor ticks are ink at 50 % and numbers ink at 80 % (`MINOR_TICK_INK`, `SCALE_NUMBER_INK`), so they keep 3:1 and 4.5:1 on every style's background.
- **Font:** only the style's number face (`numberFontFor(style)`), with lining figures (`LINING_FIGURES`); wide faces (Michroma, Orbitron) at 82 %.
- **Plate:** none. No plate, frame or drop shadow: the meter sits straight on the screen's background. The dial (Classic) has a 146u radius around a pivot 17u above the box's bottom.
- **Field:** instead, a calm field: a soft oval wash of the background colour, 190u × 150u each way round a point 47u above the box's bottom middle, 75 % in its middle, 56 % at 62 % of the way out, 0 at its edge, no edge line. The same for every look.
- **Glass:** dial looks have none. A window that is an object lying on the screen (the spirit level's tube) is recessed: sunk toward the background, 1u rim in ink at 14 %, inner shadow under the top edge (7u blur), one sheen from the top (white 28 % light, 7 % dark, gone by 45 % of its height), inner corners 10u, and a soft contact shadow under it (10u blur, 4u down, black 50 % dark, 16 % light).
- **Scale** (the open face's larger one): a tick every 5 cents, none at 0. Major (every 10) 13u × 2.1u in ink; minor 7u × 1.3u at 50 %; the style's caps. Numbers 10–50 without sign, 12.5u, ink at 80 %, centred 27u from the tick's base, at least 8u apart (`numberFit` sets close numbers up to 30 % smaller; only Classic has a scale). The dial's ticks start 4u inside its arc.
- **♭ and ♯:** inside the box, in muted, at the ends of each look's scale (`SignsArt`, 20u in the music-sign face): under the dial's end ticks (fitted between them and the box's bottom), under the vial's and the eclipse's path's ends, under the outer arrows, in the light arc's and the light bars' lower corners. No row of signs under the box, so the meter takes 2:1 and nothing more.
- **Centre:** one 3.5u rounded bar in the accent.
- **Marker** (needle, bubble, moon, lights or bars): a small soft shadow (3u blur, 1.5u right, 2.5u down); the beam glows instead (a 6u halo of itself at 18 %).
- **Finish** (`MeterFinish.kt`, the mock-up's `fin`; one of each per style, same proportions, same art): needle (Ancient spear, Classic watch hand with a ring near its tip, Retro VU line with an accent tip, Contemporary plain bar, Futuristic beam with a chevron, Space Age ball tip), hub over the pivot (stone, cap in the centre colour, ink disc with a dot, ink dot, ring, ringed planet), arc (dots in the accent, railway of two ink hairlines, broad band in the track colour, line, dashed accent line, dots), tick caps (round, butt, butt, round, square, round) and, for Ancient only, ticks drawn by hand (±12 % length, ±20 % width, ±0.05 rad, from a fixed hash so they never change). Classic wears the needle and the arc; the scale and the spirit level's lines the caps. The arcs are decoration (the ticks carry the scale), so the dashed and dotted accent arcs (55–60 %) need no contrast minimum.
- **Light:** one glow only, radial, in the "in tune" colour (32 % dark, 16 % light at its middle, fading to 0), never clipped by the box.
- **At rest:** every look's centre (Classic's centre bar and hub, Light arc's middle ray, Arrows' bar) is the muted colour without a note and fades to the accent with `presence` (`MeterRoles.centre(tuned, presence)`, `RestingCentreTest`): with wallpaper colours the accent can be green, and a resting meter must never look in tune.
- **In tune:** one eased amount (smoothstep, 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out) blends centre mark and marker to "in tune"; the glow eases the same way. Both follow the steady hint's decision; with "Remove animations" both switch at once. Nothing flashes, bursts, flies, pulses or repeats.
- **Motion:** springs with a damping ratio of 0.5 or more.

## Wave view
Most people don't know what a cent is, so the wave display (`WaveView`, 240 × 40 dp, low and frameless so it doesn't compete with the meter) shows how far off the note is as a moving shape under the note. It borrows the beats you hear between two close notes: `WaveMotion` (`dial/WaveMotion.kt`, plain Kotlin) pinches the ribbon into pulses.
- **Drift follows the strobe rule exactly:** `WaveMotion` runs its own `StrobeMotion` ([Notes, tunings and audio](notes-and-audio.md), *Strobe model*), so it stands dead still within half a cent and drifts right when sharp, left when flat, faster the further off (up to 4 pulses/s from 10.5 cents). It uses the exact cents, not `inTune`, so the last few cents still show.
- **Shape shows distance from afar, up to 50 cents:** the pinch deepens (`depthFor`) and more pulses fit across (`beatsFor`, 1.5 to 4.5) the further off; within half a cent the band is perfectly even. Shape eases towards the reading at 5/s, so a new string never makes it snap.
- **Breathing:** off pitch the band breathes slowly (one breath every 2.5 s, 10 % of its height). On the note it fades out (`WaveMotion.stir` → 0 in about a second), so nothing moves at all, like a strobe disc. The owner picked this over breathing on gently (October 2026); an old saved `wave_breathes_in_tune` is ignored.
- **Colour:** off pitch a tint of `primary` at 80 % (60 % was too dim on dark backgrounds); in tune the "in tune" colour with a soft halo; without a reading a faint line.
- **No words for the distance:** during a note the pill stays empty and uncoloured (it keeps its place); in tune a small check mark shows in its middle in the "in tune" colour, or at its start while a tip or the tune hint fills it, and fades out where it was, so colour-blind players see in tune too; and the frequency line is left out; in tune shows in the note's colour, glow and pop. With the tune hint on, the pill shows "Tune up ↑" / "Tune down ↓". TalkBack still hears the cents.
- **Drawing:** the frame loop runs only while there is a reading, with `withInfiniteAnimationFrameNanos` (UI tests skip infinite animations), and redraws only the view's own layer, without recomposing. `WaveView` is `clearAndSetSemantics {}` (test tag `TunerTestTags.WAVE` only); `NoteDisplay`'s live region says the reading, and the sentence never changes between frames.
- **Layout:** 240 × 40 dp at any font size (30 dp in the compact note on a phone on its side). In two-pane on a short screen the note and wave get smaller so the readout fits.

## Strobe look
The first wave look: two rows of stripes that drift right when sharp, left when flat, and stand still within half a cent, for the last cent of fine tuning. `drawWave` passes `WaveMotion.phase` to `drawStrobe` (`dial/Strobe.kt`) in the current style's own marks (see *Visual styles*), the lower row at twice the speed (`phase · 2`), like the rings of a strobe disc. The marks fade over the outer fifth at each end (`strobeFade`, by position only, so the wrap stays seamless). Without a reading the rows shrink to half height in the faint resting colour. On the note it freezes, as every look does. Every look shares one view and one set of layout tests.

## Wave looks
Setting "Wave look", shown while the display is the wave: six looks (`WaveLook`: strobe, ribbon, dots, fireflies, loop, ripples, in that grid order), picked from `WaveLookPicker` cards with live previews (`LookCardGrid`, as the meter picker).
- **One cue, each in its own way:** off pitch something moves one step of the look per strobe period (`WaveMotion.phase`), right or outwards when sharp, left or inwards when flat; within half a cent everything stands still. `unrest(depth)` (0 on the note, ~0.4 a cent off, 1 at 50 cents) shows distance from afar and eases to nothing, so every look settles into one calm shape on the note.
- *Strobe:* see above. *Ribbon:* pulses drift and smooth out (`dial/SoundRibbon.kt`, in each style's marks). *Dots:* a dot-matrix strip scrolling one dot per period (`dotColumnAt`, `edgeFade`). *Fireflies:* eight specks per pulse riding the pulses, scattered by `unrest`, gliding into an even line on the note (`fireflyAt`). *Loop:* a hoop spinning like a coin at the beat rate, its brighter near half sweeping the way the note is off, settling face-on into a still ring (`loopAt`, `loopWidth`, `loopNearSide`). *Ripples:* five rings spreading out when sharp and drawing in when flat, swelling unevenly off pitch, smooth ovals on the note (`rippleAt`, `rippleWobble`).
- Strobe and ribbon use each style's own marks; the other four have one shape in the style's colours.
- Every position is a continuous periodic function of `phase`, so nothing jumps when the direction flips or the phase wraps. JVM tests check each function for direction, stillness on the note and no jumps.
- Drawing allocates nothing: one `Ribbon` refilled, the caller's `Path` reused, strokes from `roundStroke` / `buttStroke`, cached per quarter-pixel width. `drawWave` (`dial/WaveLooks.kt`) draws them; the settings previews use the same `drawWave` through one shared `WaveMotion`.
- **Tests:** `WaveViewTest` (every look in every style drawn inside its box, at most a 4 dp glow outside; motion without recomposing), `TunerContentTest` (TalkBack skips the wave and hears the reading once), `SettingsContentTest` (radio buttons, names on one line at 1.3× and 2× font). The wave's size is the same for every look, so the layout tests cover all six.

## Visual styles
Six styles, in the owner's order: Ancient, Classic, Retro, Contemporary (the default, and the only one that can follow the wallpaper), Futuristic and Space Age. A saved Prehistoric or Middle Ages (styles of older versions) opens as Ancient (see [Lifecycle](lifecycle.md), *Saved settings*). Colours, faces, shapes and backgrounds follow the approved mock-up [docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html](../design/theme-styles-preview.html) (its data block is the source). Each style owns one accent hue round the colour wheel (Ancient terracotta, Retro avocado, Futuristic cyan, Contemporary blue, Space Age violet, Classic burgundy); greens are kept for "in tune". Each style sets:
- **Palette** (`StylePalettes.kt`): a light and a dark `Palette` of the mock-up's roles (background, surface, ink, muted, track, accent + onAccent, inTune + onInTune, and `second`/`third` for background art only, never text), copied exactly, turned into a Material 3 `ColorScheme`. **Soft grounds** (October 2026, `docs/design/background-tones-preview.html`): light mode was too white and dark mode too black, so the backgrounds sit at CIELAB lightness ≈ 91.5 (light) and ≈ 11 (dark; Futuristic 8 for its neon) instead of 95–97 and 2–8, surfaces 4 (light) or 5 (dark) above, each in the style's own hue with its tint raised (never plain grey); Ancient's light stone was already there. Four colours went a shade darker to keep their contrast: Contemporary light's and Futuristic light's in tune and Futuristic light's accent (4.5:1), and Retro light's ink (its meter ticks, 3:1). In the scheme, primary = accent; primary and secondary containers (selected segments, chips, the slider's track) = surface mixed 22 % (light) / 32 % (dark) with the accent; secondary/tertiary = second/third (the background reads them there, so Contemporary's wallpaper colours reach it); outline = muted, outlineVariant = track. `StylePaletteTest` holds every palette, light and dark, to WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for ink, muted, accent and inTune on the background, surface and surface container, for onAccent/onInTune on their fills and for text on every container; the meter's numbers (ink at 80 %) 4.5:1 and its minor ticks (ink at 50 %), centre mark and outlines 3:1. It also keeps the accents at least 30 degrees apart in hue and out of the greens.
- **Lettering** (`StyleTypography.kt`): a display face for the note, headlines and titles, a number face for readouts and the meter (`numberFontFor`), plain reading text. Ancient: Forum (antique Roman letters; the owner found Caesar Dressing too gimmicky and picked Forum from six calmer faces in `docs/design/ancient-font-preview.html`), numbers in Alegreya Sans Medium (kept from the approved readouts). Classic: Playfair Display 400, numbers in Bodoni Moda 500 (optical size 14), serif reading text. Retro: Righteous. Contemporary: the phone's font, light. Futuristic: Orbitron 500 (+0.06 em on titles), numbers Orbitron 500. Space Age: Michroma (+0.02 em). Per language (`facesFor(style, language)`, `TwangleTheme` passes the app's language): Orbitron lacks Polish and Turkish letters (ą ć ę ł ń ś ź ż; ğ İ ş), so in Polish and Turkish Futuristic's display and titles take Michroma (+0.02 em), and no word mixes two faces (Forum has them all, and Russian too); in a script no display face has (Arabic, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Cyrillic outside Classic and Ancient) the phone's font draws whole words, and the face's extra letter spacing is left out, so Chinese and Japanese characters don't stand apart. `StyleFontCoverageTest` (JVM) reads the bundled fonts' character maps and every language's texts: in every style each run of letters of one script is wholly in the face or wholly not, and no extra spacing where the face lacks the script. Every text style, and the meter's numbers, ask for lining figures (`fontFeatureSettings = "lnum"`, `LINING_FIGURES`), so a 0 never looks like an o (Playfair Display's own figures are old-style; faces without the feature ignore it). Bundled under the SIL OFL (`core/designsystem/src/main/res/font`, licences in `assets/licenses`, credited on the About page via `bundledFontCredits`): Forum, Alegreya Sans (one weight), Playfair Display, Bodoni Moda (variable, upright only), Righteous, Orbitron, Michroma. About 1.15 MB of font files (Forum is bundled whole, 300 KB: `scripts/subset-font.py` would drop its kerning), nothing downloaded.
- **Music signs** (`MusicSigns.kt`): none of those faces has ♯ or ♭, and the fallback drew them at a different size in each, so every ♯ ♭ ♮ ♩ ♪ comes from `music_signs.ttf` (Noto Sans Symbols cut to those glyphs, 2.6 KB, `scripts/subset-font.py`). `musicText()` sets a sign after a letter like a chord symbol (0.7 em and raised; ♭ 0.8 em). The text itself is unchanged, so UI tests see "C♯"; TalkBack hears words via `NoteNameSpeech`.
- **Shapes** (`shapesFor`, mock-up corners: `small` = the cents pill, `medium` = look cards and tips): uneven pebbles for Ancient, fine 4 dp corners for Classic, fat 18 to 22 dp curves for Retro, Contemporary's round ones, corners cut on every side for Futuristic, capsules for Space Age. String buttons (`StringChipShape.kt`: `stringChipShape(style, index)` and `stringChipSize(style)`, shared by the tuner and the mood cards) are pebbles (six, no two alike), ivory keys (38 x 50 dp, the name low, a shaded front edge: `stringChipShading`), soft hi-fi buttons (lit from above, shaded below), circles, bevelled neon tiles (an accent outline at 55 % on a solid tint, the accent at 10 % on the background colour so the name reads over the picture; the picked one glowing: `neonGlow`; Futuristic's mode switch is outlined the same way) and capsules, at the mock-up's proportions; in the tuner each sits in a touch target of at least 48 dp each way (`minimumInteractiveComponentSize`), and the compact row's small ones shrink to fit 32 dp. Rows of 7 or 8 strings that don't fit shrink every button alike, keeping its proportions (see [Tuner screen](ui-tuner.md), *String row*).
- **Background** (`StyleBackdrop`): drawn once by `MainActivity` behind every see-through screen (`Scaffold(containerColor = Transparent, contentColor = onBackground)`), in its own offscreen `graphicsLayer` (`CompositingStrategy.Offscreen`), so the tuner's readings at up to 120 Hz only reuse a cached texture; seeded random numbers make it the same every time. **Abstract backgrounds** (October 2026, picked by the owner from [docs/design/style-backgrounds-preview.html](../design/style-backgrounds-preview.html) after a first round of drawn objects — bison, lyre, drapes, daisies, Sputnik — was turned down as not impressive): patterns and textures instead of pictures, at 1 unit = 1 dp. Ancient stone (grit, soft patches) with wavy bands of earth colours at the top and bottom, like rock layers; Classic velvet folds, a spotlight and the gold curves of a concert-hall shell; Retro a 70s supergraphic, three avocado, mustard and orange stripes turning a rounded corner at the top right (the mock-up's second turn, low on the left, ran through the string buttons and the style cards on the phone, so it went); Contemporary fine flowing silk lines at the top and lower down, every sixth in the accent; Futuristic a glowing perspective grid in the lower part that fades away upwards with **no horizon line** (the owner's wish after rounds 3 and 4), a faint magenta haze, sparks rising at the sides and scan lines by night (the mock-up's canvas blur is stood in for by two wide faint strokes under each line); Space Age 1960s halftone dots, big in the top right corner (orange) and low on the left (violet), shrinking to nothing, over a violet gradient and faint stars by night. Motifs sized for the upright phone shrink with the height (`fit`: on its side, or on a small card). Every screen but the tuner (metronome, settings, About, the tuning editor) gets the quiet version (`quiet = true`: every motif softer, Futuristic's grid at half strength and without sparks). Bright motifs stay in the outer fifths (`sideX`), away from the note and meter; on its side, where the note column takes the right and the meter starts a tenth in, they keep to the strip left of the meter (Retro's stripes turn two corners there, Space Age's dots come from the two left corners). Nothing is fixed to the screen's edges; the settings list ends 24 dp above the bottom inset.
- **Mood card** (`StyleMood.kt`): the style's picture on its card in Settings (4:5, from the approved mock-up `docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html`): its background (`StyleBackdropPreview`: the motifs at the mock-up's card scale, 2.2 times the card's share of a 393 dp phone, plus a soft wash of the accent from below), a big "A" in its display face, a strip of its ribbon marks in its accent colour (never "in tune", so each card keeps its own colour) drifting gently 3 cents sharp, and two string buttons (E, A picked) in its shape and the mock-up's card sizes (keys and capsules taller). - **Sound ribbon** (`dial/SoundRibbon.kt`) and **strobe** (`dial/Strobe.kt`): the ribbon and strobe looks in each style's own marks, as the mock-up's `mark` and `period`: Ancient fingertip dabs with a smudge (13 dp apart), Classic an engraved band (fill 32 %, 1.7 dp outline, a hairline through it; strobe bars between two hairlines), Retro a glowing phosphor band (strobe: backlit bars), Contemporary rounded voice-memo bars, Futuristic thin neon bars that always glow softly, Space Age pairs of stars (strobe: twinkles over glowing dots); `stripePeriod` 8–13 dp. - **Press effect everywhere** (owner's request 2026-10-15: "all buttons should have the feedback effect when tapped"): every button, row and item of the phone app lights up on touch-down in the style's own finish, stays lit while held and fades over 280 ms, the metronome's `pressFeedback` ([Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md), *Metronome screen*), instead of Material's ripple. `TwangleTheme` provides `LocalIndication = PressIndication.Row` and `LocalRippleConfiguration = null`, so:
  - **Rows, list and menu items** (Settings' switch, choice and link rows, About's credits, the language list, the tuning panel's tuning rows and New tuning, the strum panel, the ⋮ menu's items through `TwangleMenuItem`) take the theme's light from a plain `clickable`/`selectable`/`toggleable`: `PressIndication`, an `IndicationNodeFactory` that reuses `pressFeedback`'s node with no sink and `face = false` (only the tint: no Futuristic neon frame or glow over a long row), across the row's bounds and clipped by the row's own clip. The navigation bar's and rail's items take it in the style's `medium` shape, no sink.
  - **Material's buttons** draw no ripple once `LocalRippleConfiguration` is null, and give no other way in, so the screens use `TwangleTextButton`, `TwangleButton`, `TwangleIconButton`, `TwangleFilledTonalIconButton` (`PressButtons.kt`): Material's own button, look and TalkBack, with `pressFeedback` on the same interaction source (and `pressFlash()` in its click, for TalkBack's double tap). Material sets its 48 dp touch area *outside* the modifier it is given, which would light the touch area rather than the 40 dp button, so these turn Material's off (`OwnTouchArea`: `LocalMinimumInteractiveComponentSize` 0 inside) and set the same area round the light themselves (`Modifier.touchArea`, Compose still widens touches to it); nothing moves. A text or icon button's light lies under its text; a filled button (Next, Allow microphone, Retry, the editor's − / +) draws its face as content, so its light lies over it (`above`) in a flat tone that never hides the text (`ACCENT_FILL`: on-accent at 20 %; the tour balloon's Skip `IN_ACCENT`, Next `IN_ACCENT_FILLED`). The tuner's mode switch (Material's `SegmentedButton`) does the same in place.
  - **Custom faces with a shape** (the string buttons, the tuning panel's instrument chips, the strum check switch, Settings' style, meter and wave cards; and the tuning's name above the strings, which as a quiet label lights with the tint only) sink as well: `Modifier.pressSink(source)` before the face (the whole button, fill and outline included, drawn smaller about its middle) and `PressIndication(shape, face = true)` on its clickable or after its last border, so the light lies over the fill and under the name (over the preview for the look cards, `above`). The signal bars light a 48 dp circle round them like an icon button, without a sink (the bars are drawn beside their touch area).
  - Not changed: the A4 slider (a drag control; its thumb has Material's own pressed look, without the ripple), the switches and radio buttons inside rows (the row takes the touch), the watch app (`:wear`, Wear Compose with its own press look, no shared theme) and the home-screen widget (Android views, no Compose). `PressFeedbackTest` (JVM) checks the touch area and the lights' equality; `PressEffectTest` (`:feature:settings`, UI, every style light and dark in a thorough run) holds each kind of button down, checks it lights and fades back, clicks once by touch and by TalkBack, keeps 48 dp, and a text button's light stays on its pill.

`TwangleTheme(style, darkTheme, dynamicColor)` provides the `ColorScheme`, `Typography`, `Shapes` and `LocalStyleTokens` (`StyleTokens`: the style, its "in tune" pair, the unfaded scheme), remembered per style and brightness. A style or brightness change fades every colour over 450 ms (one `Animatable`) and cross-fades the background. Each mood card wraps its own `TwangleTheme`, so it previews that style whatever the current one is.
