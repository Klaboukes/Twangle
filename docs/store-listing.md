# Twangle on Google Play: the store listing

The plan for how Twangle is presented on Google Play: the texts, the pictures, and how people find it. Phase 23 of the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) builds it. The English texts below are the source; every language in `twangle.languages` gets its own translation.

## What makes Twangle stand out
Most tuners in the store are free to download but show ads, sell a subscription, ask for an account or track their users. Twangle's strong points, in the order the listing tells them:

1. **It tunes well:** a clear needle, within ±1 cent, from a 5-string bass's low B to the top frets, with "Tune up" / "Tune down", strum check and a reference tone to tune by ear.
2. **Many instruments:** guitar (6, 7, 8 strings), bass, ukulele, violin, viola, cello, mandolin, chromatic for everything else, and your own tunings.
3. **A metronome** in the same app.
4. **Made for everyone:** TalkBack speaks the note and how far off it is, every control works at 2× text, a vibration when in tune, 16 languages including Arabic (right to left). Few tuners can say this, and blind and low-vision musicians look for exactly this.
5. **Nothing to pay, nothing to sign, nothing leaves the phone:** no ads, no account, no purchases, no tracking, and no internet permission at all. Play's *Data safety* section will show "No data collected" and "No data shared", which backs this up.
6. **Yours to style:** six styles, six meter looks, light and dark.
7. **Widget and Wear OS watch app** (the watch only if it launches now, **decision 3**).

## Google's rules that shape the listing
Checked against the Play Console when entering the listing, since Google changes them now and then.
- **No price or promotion words in the title, icon, developer name, feature graphic or screenshots:** no "free", "no ads", "best", "#1", "download now". Those claims go in the short and full description only, stated as plain facts.
- **No other apps named** anywhere in the listing, and no keyword lists: search words appear in normal sentences.
- **Lengths:** title 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000.
- **Screenshots:** 2 to 8 per device type, the long side at most twice the short side. The Xiaomi 12X's screen is 1080 × 2400 (2.2 : 1), too long as it is, so screenshots are placed on a 1080 × 1920 canvas with a caption above (see below). At least 4 screenshots of 1080 px or more make the app eligible for Play's recommendations.
- **Icon** 512 × 512 PNG; **feature graphic** 1024 × 500 PNG or JPEG, shown behind the promo video or at the top of the listing on some devices, so nothing important near its edges.

## Title and texts

**App name in the store (30):** `Twangle: Guitar Tuner` (21). "Guitar tuner" is by far the most searched phrase in this category and the title weighs most in Play search. The launcher label stays "Twangle". In other languages the part after the colon is translated ("Twangle: Gitaarstemmer", "Twangle: Gitarrenstimmgerät"), as long as it fits in 30.

**Short description (80):** shown under the title, and the second strongest place for search words.
> Accurate tuner for guitar, bass, ukulele and violin, with a metronome. No ads.

(78 characters.) Alternative if the accessibility point should lead:
> Guitar tuner and metronome that speaks with TalkBack. No ads, no account.

(73 characters.)

**Full description (4000):** plain text, short paragraphs, a few bullet lines; no emoji rows.

> Twangle is a guitar tuner that is simple to use and precise to a single cent. Play a string, and Twangle shows the note and how far it is from being in tune, on a big clear meter. "Tune up" or "Tune down" tells you which way to turn the peg.
>
> No ads, no account, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Twangle doesn't even have permission to use the internet: the sound from your microphone is analysed live on your phone and never recorded or sent anywhere.
>
> TUNE ANY INSTRUMENT
> • Guitar (6, 7 or 8 strings), bass (4 or 5 strings), ukulele, violin, viola, cello and mandolin
> • Guitar tunings: Standard, Drop D, half step down, full step down, DADGAD, Open G, Open D and Open E
> • Make your own tunings for any instrument
> • Chromatic tuner for any other instrument or your voice
> • Strum check: strum all strings at once and see which ones are flat, sharp or in tune
> • Hold a string to hear its note and tune by ear
> • Set A4 from 415 to 466 Hz, for example 442 Hz to play with an orchestra
>
> METRONOME
> • 30 to 250 beats per minute on a round tempo dial, with tap tempo
> • 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8 and 7/8, with an accent on the first beat and lights to follow along
> • Clicks over your own music or backing track, and can vibrate on the beat
>
> MADE FOR EVERYONE
> • Works with TalkBack: Twangle says the note and how far off it is ("E 2, 12 cents flat, tune up"), every button has a clear name, and it never floods you with speech
> • Every screen works with large text, up to twice the normal size
> • A short vibration when a string is in tune, so you can feel it too
> • In 16 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish
> • Note names the way you learned them: C D E, with H for B, or Do Re Mi
>
> MAKE IT YOURS
> • Six styles, from Ancient to Space Age, each with its own colours, lettering and background
> • Six meter looks and six wave looks, a strobe among them
> • Light, dark, or following your phone, with your wallpaper's colours on Android 12 and newer
> • Focus mode: one big meter on a phone on its side, easy to read from a distance
>
> ALSO
> • A home-screen widget that opens the tuner with one tap
> • A Wear OS app that tunes on your watch by itself, no phone needed
> • Keeps the screen on while you tune
>
> Twangle works fully offline and will stay free: no ads, no account, no purchases, ever.

(About 2,300 characters, so each translation has room to grow.) The watch line comes out if the watch app follows in an update. The words people search for (guitar tuner, bass tuner, ukulele tuner, violin tuner, cello tuner, chromatic tuner, metronome, Drop D, strobe) each appear in a real sentence; none is repeated just for search.

## Pictures

### Icon and feature graphic
- **Icon:** the launcher icon (*twang to tune*, PR #206) at 512 × 512, full square: Google adds the round corners and shadow itself.
- **Feature graphic:** the Twangle icon and name on the left, on the right one style's meter showing a note in tune with its calm glow, on that style's background. No other text. One version for every language (it has no words to translate besides the name).

### Watch screenshots (only if the watch launches now)
Two or three square screenshots (at least 384 × 384), the watch's round screen only, no watch frame: a note in tune with the glowing ring, a note flat, and the microphone prompt.

### Promo video
None for v1.0. Maybe later: a 30-second screen recording of tuning a real guitar, with the sound.

## Being found
- **Category:** Music & Audio.
- **Tags:** in the Play Console (*Store settings*), up to five of Google's tags: the ones closest to tuner, metronome and musical instruments.
- **Contact details:** the public contact email (**decision 5**) and the privacy-policy page as website.
- **Ratings:** good ratings early help search more than anything else. Ask the closed-test testers to rate it once it is public. In Twangle, a calm "Rate Twangle" row in About that opens the Play page; no pop-up asking for a rating.
- **Translations:** every language's title, short and full description and captions, translated by Claude and checked by a native speaker where possible (the Phase 22 owner check covers this).
- **Accessibility communities:** once live, submit Twangle to the app directories and forums of blind and low-vision Android users (for example *Accessible Android*), where a tuner that speaks is rare. Ask the closed-test testers whether one of them uses TalkBack.
- **After launch:** Play's *store listing experiments* can compare the two short descriptions or the first screenshot; *custom store listings* can give e.g. bass players their own listing later.
- **"What's new":** short release notes in every language with each update, in the same plain style.
