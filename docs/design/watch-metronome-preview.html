A metronome on the watch

Rough mock-up of Phase 80 in Ink, on a Galaxy Watch 5-sized screen. The beat is a tap on the wrist; the click is optional. Only the tempo and start/stop, four beats to the bar.

Decided (owner, 2026-11-05):
  • A tap on the wrist on every beat, the first beat of the bar stronger; the watch's click optional.
  • Tempo and start/stop only: four beats to the bar, no meter choice, no tap tempo.
  • Reached by a swipe from the tuner and by the tile's Metronome button.
