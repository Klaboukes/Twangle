Background tones: now and softer

Each style in light and dark mode, as it is now and with a softer background: light mode a little less white, dark mode a little less black. The colours of the text, the needle and the "in tune" green stay the same.

Tap a phone to see it big, then tap it (or press the space bar) to flip between now and softer in the same spot. That shows the difference best.

The sliders show how light the background is, from 0 (black) to 100 (white). Now: light mode sits at 91 to 97, dark mode at 2 to 8. Suggested: light 91.5, dark 11 (Futuristic 3 lower, to keep its neon look). A computer screen is not the phone's screen: the phone's dark mode looks darker still.
