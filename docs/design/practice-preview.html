The Practice tab, chord charts and scales

Owner picked A, D, G, I on 2026-10-16.

The Metronome tab becomes Practice: the Metronome, Chords and the Capo helper, with a small switch at the top. Three questions, each with its own letters. Pick one letter per question (for example "A, D, G, I").

Shown on a guitar in standard tuning; with another instrument or your own tuning the charts follow it.

1 · The switch at the top

The bar under it reads Tuner · Instrument · Practice · Settings. Practice opens on the tool you used last.

2 · A chord chart

C major as an example. First how one shape looks (D, E, F), then how several shapes of the same chord are shown (G, H).

3 · A scale on the fretboard

A minor pentatonic as an example. The root (A) is filled in the accent colour.

Not shown yet: how you pick the chord (root and type, such as "C" and "major") or the scale. That comes with the screens, with buttons of the same steady size as the rest of the app.

TalkBack reads every chart in words, string by string: "A string, fret 3, finger 3".
