What stays the same

  • ♭ left, ♯ right, by position; the rays stand on the stretched scale, closer together near the middle.
  • The wedge from the middle out to the reading, in the reading's tone (amber far off, green in tune), each light fading as the reading leaves it; the middle ray muted off the note and green in tune, its neighbours half lit; the in-tune glow behind it (that glow, for being in tune, is left as it is).
  • The fan's size: the pointer runs along the marks' row and needs a little room at the ends, so with it the fan is about 6 % smaller.
  • High contrast as today: an unlit light an empty ring in ink, no halo.
