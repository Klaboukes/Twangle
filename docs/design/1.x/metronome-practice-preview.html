Where do subdivisions and the speed trainer go?

The metronome screen keeps its dial and its row of three buttons (Sound · Tap tempo · Listen). So both new things need room somewhere else. Pick one place for the subdivision (A, B or C) and one for the speed trainer (1, 2 or 3).

My pick: C and 3, two small buttons at the empty end of the "Metronome ⌄" row (drawn together at the bottom). They take no height from the dial and leave the meter picker and the three buttons alone. Each shows its state at a glance: the note value picked, and the trainer button lit while it is on.

Subdivisions: softer clicks between the beats

Four choices: beats only, eighths, triplets, sixteenths. The beat lights stay on the beats.

In 6/8 and 7/8, all three

The click is already an eighth there, so triplets and sixteenths rest at 38 % and take no taps. They keep their places, so nothing moves when the meter changes.

Speed trainer: start slower, climb to the goal

The trainer's panel

The same panel whichever way it opens. Real size (1 px per dp). Start and Goal step by 5 BPM; Step and Bars are short lists.

While it climbs (C and 3 together)

Left out on purpose: a fourth button in the row (the owner's rule), anything inside the dial (− + and Start fill it), and a tap on the beat lights (nobody would find it).

On its side, the "Metronome ⌄" row stands over the dial's half, so C and 3 would sit at its end there too. With the Practice tab switched off there is no tool button: the two buttons then stand alone at the top end, in the same place.
