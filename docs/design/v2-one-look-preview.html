One look, four candidates

The same screen, the ruler, the lens and the colour with the trail inside, in four characters. Everything that works stays the same in all four: left flat, right sharp, amber far off, green in tune, light and dark. What differs is what the app feels like: its colours, its lettering, the shape of its lens and buttons. Pick the one you would want to open every day.

Where the picker went

The chip at the top, Guitar · Standard, is the one picker. Tap it and the sheet asks what you are tuning: instrument, tuning and Any note live there and nowhere else, so the mode switch is gone. The pegs stay what they are, the strings of that tuning: tap one to tune only it, hold it to hear it. In Any note the pegs go and the chip says so.

The same in all four

  • The lens contract: left flat, right sharp, green in tune, quiet when the string dies; the trail, the Light arc or the strobe inside.
  • The two colours are the app's own: amber far off, green in tune. They never come from the wallpaper, so a resting meter never looks in tune.
  • Light and dark, following the phone, with the switch in Settings to force one.
  • The phone's colours as the only personalisation: on Android 12 and up, the surfaces and the accent take the wallpaper's palette when the switch is on (it is today, in Contemporary); off, the look's own palette.
  • The top row: the gear top left, the picker chip in the middle, focus mode top right; the three tabs below.
