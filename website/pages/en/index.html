A calm, precise tuner. Free, with nothing to sign up for.

Play a string and Twangle shows the note and how far it is from being in tune, to a single cent. Then it helps you play: chord names, chord charts, scales, the capo and a metronome.

Tune any instrument

Guitar (6, 7, 8 or 12 strings), bass (4 or 5 strings), ukulele, 5-string banjo, violin, viola, cello and mandolin, in the common tunings or your own. The chromatic tuner does any other instrument, or your voice. All instruments and tunings

Twangle speaks with TalkBack, works with large text, and comes in 16 languages.
