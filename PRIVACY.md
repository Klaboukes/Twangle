# Twangle privacy policy

*Effective date: 11 October 2026* · [Nederlands](PRIVACY.nl.md)

Twangle is a guitar tuner for Android, made by the developer Klaboukes. In short: **Twangle collects no data about you.** Nothing you play and nothing about you leaves your phone.

## The microphone
- Twangle needs the microphone to hear the note you play. Android asks your permission first, and you can take it back at any time in the phone's settings.
- The microphone is only on **while the tuner screen is open**. When you go to the settings screen, switch apps, lock the phone or turn the screen off, Twangle lets go of the microphone within about 5 seconds. Android's microphone indicator shows when it is on.
- The sound is analysed live, in the phone's memory, to find the pitch, and thrown away straight after. Twangle **never records, saves or sends** any sound.

## No internet, no tracking
- Twangle has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- No ads, no account or sign-up, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.
- Twangle does not share data with anyone, because it has none to share.

## Settings stay on your phone
Twangle remembers your choices: the A4 reference pitch, the tuning, how note names are written, the visual style, light or dark mode, wallpaper colours, the display (needle or wave) and the wave's look, keep screen on and vibrate when in tune. These are stored only on your phone.

If you have turned on Android's own backup (for example Google backup), Android may include these settings in your phone's backup or copy them to a new phone, so they come back after a reinstall. That backup is run by Android and your backup provider, not by Twangle, and you control it in the phone's settings. It never contains sound.

## Vibration
Twangle can vibrate briefly when a string is in tune. This needs Android's vibrate permission, which is granted on install and gives no access to any data.

## Children
Twangle collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
