One button for instrument and tuning

The two menus under the note become one button that says what you are tuning, such as Guitar · Standard. Tap it and a panel slides up with the instruments along the top and that instrument's tunings below. Your own tunings will join that list in Phase 16. Try it on the middle phone.

Language
  • One line, alwaysThe button names the instrument first, then the tuning. Even the longest pair, "8-snarige gitaar · Standaard", stays on one line. Only the widest letters (Futuristic, Space Age) set it a little smaller, and nothing splits over two lines any more.
  • Instrument, then tuningTapping an instrument switches the tuner to its standard tuning right away. The panel stays open so you can pick another tuning. Tapping a tuning picks it and closes the panel.
  • Room to growThe instrument row scrolls sideways, so all ten fit and more can come. The tuning list scrolls too. In Phase 16 your own tunings go under the presets, with "New tuning" at the bottom (shown greyed out here).
  • Same look as the restThe button and instrument chips take each style's own shape, like the string buttons. The panel uses the style's surface colour. The meter here is only a sketch; the real one stays as it is.
