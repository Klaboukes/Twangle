## Tuner screen
`TunerScreen` wires the ViewModel; `TunerContent` is the stateless body used by previews and UI tests. Upright (`SingleColumnLayout`, as the approved mock-up `docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html`) the column fills the screen's height: mode switch at the top, the meter, the note, and the guitar controls at the bottom; the free height goes 1 : 2 above the meter and above the strings (`TOP_GAP_WEIGHT`), each gap at least 24 dp. With a very large font the gaps close and it scrolls. The meter spans the screen less 8 dp each side (`METER_MARGIN`); everything else keeps a 16 dp text margin (`COLUMN_PADDING`). From top to bottom:
- **Top corners:** empty. Settings and the metronome are reached from the navigation bar ([UI basics](ui.md), *Navigation bar*).
- **Mode switch:** `ModeSwitch`, the app's `SegmentedSwitch` (`:core:designsystem`, also the Practice tab's tool switch), Material 3 segmented buttons "Strings" / "Chromatic" ("Snaren" / "Chromatisch"), the selected segment in `primaryContainer` (Material's secondary put Futuristic's purple beside its cyan). Centred at the top, 8 dp down. No "A4 = … Hz" line. A label that doesn't fit its half of the row (Dutch "Chromatisch", Russian "Хроматический" at 2× text on a 360 dp phone) shrinks as far as it must, down to 8 sp, and one line keeps no line spacing above and below it, which at 2× text was taller than the button. The picked label is measured in the room Material leaves beside the check mark (Material measures it in the whole segment and lays the mark beside it, which cut the end off a picked label that just fitted); every label's whole one-line width is its minimum.
- **Meter:** `MeterFrame` in the picked look (see [Meter, wave and styles](ui-meter-and-styles.md), *Meter*), with `zIndex(-1)` so its calm field and in-tune glow, which reach past its box, lie behind the mode switch and the note. The scroll areas fill the screen (`heightIn(min = …)`), so the glow fades out on the screen rather than at a scroll area's edge.
- **Note:** `NoteDisplay`: the big note name and octave in the style's display face (shrinking to fit wide faces and solfège), then the cents in a pill ("12 cents flat", whole cents) and the smoothed frequency, or the wave (see *Display*). Pill numbers use `numberFontFor(style)` (Alegreya Sans for Ancient, whose Caesar Dressing 0 looks like a diamond; Bodoni Moda for Classic; otherwise the title face). While listening the meter rests faded and the text asks for a string (Auto) or a note (Chromatic); the waiting note is a faint ♪.
- **Guitar controls** (`GuitarControls`): the strum check's switch at the end of a row of its own, in line with the last string, above `StringSelector`. The start of that row names the instrument and tuning ("Guitar · Drop D", `tuner_tuning_name`; a custom tuning by its own name): quiet text in `titleSmall` and the muted colour, no outline or ⌄, so it reads as a label rather than a button and the tuner stays calm. It starts in line with the first string; a long name (a large font, a long language) takes up to two lines and widens the row rather than run under the switch, 12 dp at least from it. A tap on it (48 dp high at least) opens the instrument and tuning panel, as the bar's Instrument item does; TalkBack: "Guitar, Drop D, button, double-tap to change instrument or tuning". Only in guitar mode, where the strings show (E₂ A₂ D₃ G₃ B₃ E₄, target highlighted and `selected`; each string a button, "Lock to this string" / "Pick strings automatically"; a locked string gets an outer ring, "Locked", and the hint "Play the A2 string"). Chips take the style's outline shape and a see-through backing.
- **Instrument and tuning** (`InstrumentTuningPanel.kt`, as the approved mock-up `docs/design/combined-picker-preview.html`): opened from the navigation bar's Instrument item ([UI basics](ui.md), *Navigation bar*), which keeps the tuner calmer than a button above the strings did (owner, 2026-10-15). `TunerContent` shows the panel while `pickerOpen` and the state has a tuning (not over an error, which closes it). Without the microphone `TunerScreen` shows the same panel over the permission prompt, from `TunerViewModel.panelTuning` (the saved tuning and its notation, known without listening). Picking an instrument or a tuning in chromatic mode switches to guitar mode, where the strings show. The panel is a Material 3 modal bottom sheet over the dimmed tuner (at most 74 % of the window with its handle, top corners from the style's cards, Futuristic's cut): the title "Instrument and tuning", every instrument in one row that scrolls sideways (the picked one in `primaryContainer` with a 2 dp accent outline, scrolled into view on opening; Ancient's chips alternate pebbles, Retro's are shaded push buttons), a divider, then that instrument's presets (`TuningRow`: name over its open strings, the current one in `primaryContainer` and ticked), in a column that scrolls and opens scrolled to the ticked tuning (a `BringIntoViewRequester` on its row, preset or own, asked one frame after the sheet's content is composed, from inside it: the sheet composes its content in a window of its own, later than the code round it, so asked from outside, the rows are often not there yet and the panel opens at the top), so a bass's or a long list of own tunings never hides it below the fold. Picking an instrument switches the tuner to its standard tuning and keeps the panel open, so the strings change behind it; picking a tuning closes it, as do a tap outside, a swipe down or Back. The player's own tunings for that instrument follow the presets (`MyTunings.kt`) under the small muted heading "My tunings" (in capitals with wide letter spacing as the mock-up; TalkBack hears it as written, a heading), which also stands alone over "New tuning" while there are none. Each is a `TuningRow` (picked and ticked like a preset) with a ⋮ button at its end, outside the part that picks it (TalkBack: "Options for Drop D loose"); it opens a small menu, Edit and Delete. Edit closes the panel and opens the tuning editor for that tuning. Delete first asks in a dialog ("Delete this tuning?" / "“Drop D loose” will be gone for good.", Delete in the error colour, Cancel; Back or a tap outside also keep it), so a stray tap deletes nothing; confirmed, the tuning goes (`TunerViewModel.deleteCustomTuning`; a picked one gives way to the instrument's standard tuning) and the panel stays open. The name is in the dialog's text, not its title, and the title is set at 115 % of `titleMedium`: in the wider title faces (Space Age) the dialog's own headline size left the question mark alone on a line at 2× text. A confirmation was chosen over undo: it needs no restore in the repository and no snackbar over the tuner. The list ends in "New tuning" (a + in the accent), which closes the panel and opens the tuning editor ([Lifecycle](lifecycle.md), *Custom tunings*). `StringRowStylesTest` checks the strum check's switch for every instrument, upright and on its side, at 1× and 2× text (wholly shown, in the window, above the strings and ending in line with them). `MyTuningsStylesTest` checks the own-tunings part in every style in Dutch at 2× text (heading, a 40-character name and its ⋮ button wholly shown, the button beside the name); it is drawn outside the panel there, because the panel, the menu and the dialog are windows of their own that take the phone's text size and language rather than a test's override, so those were checked on the phone.
- **String row, 4 to 8 strings** (`StringRowFit.kt`, JVM-tested): while every button fits at full size in its 48 dp touch target, the row spreads them evenly over its width (`SpaceEvenly`), so a bass's four sit wider apart than a guitar's six. Where they don't fit (7 or 8 strings upright on a narrow phone, 8 in the controls pane on its side), each button gets an equal share of the row and shrinks with its lock-ring gap (at least 2 dp) so neighbours stay 4 dp apart: 41 dp shares for 8 strings on a 360 dp phone. Its touch area is its share by 48 dp tall; Compose widens a touch just beside a button to the nearest one. The compact layout's small row packs its 32 dp chips 4 dp apart and shrinks the same way only if its column can't hold them. Every string's name keeps its size where it fits and otherwise shrinks to fit its button (`TextAutoSize`, down to 8 sp; the octave and line height are relative, so they shrink with it): squeezed buttons, and wide faces such as Space Age's "Mi" at 2× text. Strings stay in instrument order (a re-entrant ukulele's G4 first). `StringRowStylesTest` checks 4, 5, 7 and 8 strings and the ukulele upright, on its side and compact, at 1× and (solfège) 2× text.
- **Mode slot** (`ModeSlot`): measures both modes' content all the time, takes the taller height (at least 104 dp) and places only the current mode's, so a mode switch moves nothing, also at 2× font. In chromatic mode with the wave it shows `ChromaticReadout` (cents and frequency, hidden from TalkBack since the note's live region says them); with the needle the slot stays empty.
- **In tune:** meter marker, centre mark, note, cents text and highlighted string fade to the style's "in tune" colour (`StyleTokens.inTune`, read through `InTuneColor.kt`; Material 3 has no success role). A check mark appears beside the cents, so it doesn't depend on seeing green (in the wave view too, see [Meter, wave and styles](ui-meter-and-styles.md), *Wave view*); the pill fills, a soft glow lights behind the note and the note pops (grows 8 % and springs back). Each runs once per change, read in the draw phase.
- **Pill turns green gradually** (2026-10-15, owner: the jump from grey to green was abrupt): before the note is in tune the pill already takes some of the "in tune" colour as it comes close (`pillTint`, JVM-tested): none from 25 cents off, rising with a smoothstep to 40 % at the target, the same flat or sharp, so it says "getting close" without repeating the meter's direction. Only in tune fills it fully (with the check mark), so a full pill still means in tune. The fill glides on a very slow spring (`StiffnessVeryLow`), so it follows the note, not every jitter of the cents; past 40 % the text switches (150 ms fade) to whichever of `onSurface` and `onInTune` has more contrast on the fill at that moment, so it stays readable all the way (a blend of the two was grey on mid-green in the phone check); below 40 % it never changes colour.
- **Bloom and sheen** (2026-10-15, owner, as the mock-up shown in chat): the fill is drawn as a bloom (`drawPillBloom` in `PillTint.kt`), a soft oval in the pill's proportions that grows from the middle and deepens with the fill (`bloomReach`, `bloomCentre`, JVM-tested), the same both sides; past 70 % a flat layer fades in, so in tune the pill is even to its ends. As the note gets in tune one soft white band (32 %) crosses the pill once, 250 ms after, over 900 ms (`drawPillSheen`), then all is still. A real string drifts a few cents and drops in and out of tune while it rings, so the sheen follows `InTuneTickRule` (like the haptic tick) with a 6 s quiet time: the same note sheens again only after 6 s out of tune; another note at once. The sheen runs in its own coroutine, so the note leaving tune never freezes it half way; none in the wave view or with "Remove animations". Phone check (Xiaomi, Futuristic): a tone sliding into tune, wobbling ±3 cents, drifting 11 cents flat for a second and back sheened once.

## Strum check view
Strum once and every string of the tuning shows flat, in tune or sharp at once (the detector: [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md), *Strum check (polyphonic)*).
- **Entry point:** a switch above the strings, at the end of a row of its own, its outline ending where the last string button's face does (`StrumCheckToggle`: strings with a sweep across them, in the style's button outline and shape, filled with `primaryContainer` while on), so it lives with guitar mode's controls and leaves the top corners empty. The string row spreads its buttons evenly, so its edges are not the buttons': `GuitarControls` takes the faces' inset from the same fit rules the row uses (`stringFaceInset`, JVM-tested; the row is symmetric, so one inset serves both ends) and lines the switch's outline and the tuning's name up with them (at the row's edge the switch sat up to 22 dp past a bass's last string; reading the buttons' positions as alignment lines during layout left the row at stale positions when the tuning changed). Not saved: the app always opens on the tuner. Switching to chromatic turns it off; switching it on from chromatic goes to guitar mode. In the compact layout on its side it is reached like the other controls (tap the screen).
- **Audio** (hard rules 2–4 unchanged): one `AudioRecorder` collection serves both. `PitchPipeline.listen(range, strumStrings)` asks for every chunk whether strum check runs (`TunerOptions.strumChecking`, and no reference tone or its 0.3 s tail); while it does, chunks go to a `StrumListener` + `StrumDetector` (`StrumAnalysis`, made the first time and again only for another range; the frame follows the tuning's lowest string like the tuner's window) instead of YIN. It emits a `StrumReading` when strum check starts or starts over (other strings or A4), when a strum is heard or stops ringing, and with each reading (every 0.1 s). Back on the tuner, its window and smoothing start afresh. The switch never reopens the mic.
- **State:** `StrumCheckInterpreter` (pure, JVM-tested) makes `TunerUiState.StrumCheck`: verdicts from `StrumVerdict` at the tuner's own tolerance (5 cents Relaxed, 2 Precise; +2.5 for shadowed strings), the previous reading of the same strum holding "in tune" 1 cent longer. Each string shows **all readings of the strum so far together**, weighted by their frame's power (`StrumReading.levelDbfs`), so the loud start counts most and the noisy readings as the strings die away barely count (the phone check in [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md)): heard when heard in more than half the weight, its cents the weighted median of the readings that heard it, less sure when more than half of those were (up to 100 readings, 10 s). A strum's first reading must hear at least half the strings, or it is taken for another sound (a voice, a knock) and what was shown stays. A new strum keeps the last strum's strings (faded) until its own first reading. A reference tone starts the strum check over.
- **Panel** (`StrumPanel`, in the meter's place at its 2:1 shape, so every layout places it like the meter): one column per string, in the string row's order: its name (octave low, as on the string buttons), a short upright scale (±50 cents, sharp up, flat down; ♯ and ♭ once at the panel's start; the in-tune band round the centre mark, faint and in the muted colour until the string reads in tune, then in the style's "in tune" colour, and the centre mark muted until the string is heard, so a panel waiting for its strum never looks like six strings in tune (green only ever means in tune)), a marker at the reading (the "in tune" colour, else the accent) and under it a check mark, the cents ("−12", "+8", up to ±99) or "–" for a string not heard (its scale faded). A **less sure** (shadowed) string has a hollow marker and paler figures. On a calm field like the meter's, in the style's colours, display face and number face, sized by the panel's width (u = width / 340, like the meter), not the font size. Markers glide for 250 ms.
- **Line under it** (`StrumStatus`, in the note's place): "Strum all the open strings", "Listening…", then "2 strings to tune", "Not every string was heard" or "All strings in tune" (in the "in tune" colour), with a smaller line: what the strings show, "Tap a string to tune it", "Strum again, or tap a string to tune it". Each line takes the room of its longest text (`TallestOf`), so nothing moves between phases. A polite live region.
- **Strings:** no target is highlighted. Tapping one, on the string row or its column in the panel, leaves strum check and locks the tuner to that string, to tune it (both call `onStringClick`, the ViewModel's `toggleString`; while a reference tone sounds, a tap stops it, as anywhere). The columns are buttons once the strum has given readings (in every phase but the first wait, which has nothing to tap): they tile the panel, so a tap anywhere on it reaches a string, at least 48 dp each way on a phone (the panel's own size, nothing added round it, so nothing moves), and press with the style's ripple clipped to its button shape (`pickerButtonShape`: Ancient's pebble, else `shapes.medium`), drawn over the art while the whole column takes the touch. TalkBack: a button with the action "Tune A 2" ("A 2 stemmen"; "Stop the tone" while one sounds), its spoken reading unchanged.
- **TalkBack:** each string is one item: "A 2, 12 cents flat, tune up", "B 3, not heard", "E 4, in tune, less sure", "D 3, not strummed yet"; the switch is "Strum check, switch, on/off". Its icon has no words beside it (the row's start holds the tuning's name), so a long-press shows "Strum check" in a Material plain tooltip above it, which goes by itself after a moment; the tour's strum step names it too (`aLongPressOnTheStrumCheckSwitchShowsItsName`). Dutch: "Strumcheck", "12 cent te laag, hoger stemmen", "niet gehoord", "minder zeker".
- **Tests:** `StrumCheckInterpreterTest` (also: a strum's readings weighed together, a new strum starting its own, a sound hearing fewer than half the strings is no strum); `StrumCheckFlowTest` (synthetic `PluckSynth` strums from `SyntheticStrum` through the pipeline and the ViewModel: A2 −20 and G3 +15 read flat and sharp, B3 and E4 less sure, the mic opened once; chromatic, tapping a string, a reference tone). UI: `StrumCheckStylesTest` (every style: upright on a narrow phone and on the Xiaomi at 1.25×, light and dark, all four phases in view and unmoved; on its side, full and compact; Dutch at 2× wholly shown; TalkBack; markers in the style's colours; tapping each column tunes its string in every phase with readings, nothing before; every column ≥ 48 dp upright and on its side; each column a TalkBack button "Tune …" or "Stop the tone", none before the first reading). `TunerStylesTest`, `StringRowStylesTest`, `CompactLandscapeStylesTest` and `TunerContentTest` include the switch.
- **Measured on the Xiaomi 12X:** a synthetic strum (A2 −20, G3 +15, the rest in tune, `PluckSynth`-like harmonics) played from the computer's speaker read A2 −21 and G3 +15, the others in tune, B3 and E4 less sure; the reading stayed after the strings died away, and tapping A2 went to the tuner locked to it. The phone check in [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md) (`scripts/strum-check.sh`) plays more strums, guitar and bass.

## More tools

They stay in the tuner rather than in a Tools tab in the bar (owner, 2026-10-16): they share its microphone, instrument and string row ([ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md), *Where they go in the app*). Phase 35 made the button easier to find.
- **Icon (Phase 35):** owner's pick, 2026-10-16 (round 2 of [more-tools-preview.html](../design/more-tools-preview.html)): a hammer and screwdriver, Material Symbols Outlined `handyman` (weight 400, Apache 2.0, `ic_more_tools.xml`, 960-unit viewport), icon only, no word in any language. It replaced a small ^ (Material's `expand_less`), which didn't say "there are tools in here". 24 dp in the 44 dp button (the ^ was 28 dp; the filled glyph looks the same size at 24).
- **Which tools** (`tunerToolsFor(ToolSwitches, Instrument)`, JVM-tested): the strum check always; the 12th-fret check where its switch (Phase 28, `ToolSwitches.intonationCheck`) is on and the instrument has frets and a saddle (`IntonationInstruments.offers`: not the violin, viola or cello); the chord name where its switch (`ToolSwitches.chordNaming`) is on and the instrument is strummed in chords (`ChordInstruments.offers`: the guitars, the ukulele and the mandolin, not the basses or the violin family). The pitch trail (Phase 31) joins the list when it is built.
- **One tool: as v1.0.** With the strum check alone (every release build until the tools go live, Phase 34, and a violin in debug builds) `GuitarControls` shows the strum check's own switch exactly as before; there is no more-tools button, so a hidden tool leaves no trace (`ToolSwitchesOffTest` checks it on the real app).
- **More than one:** `MoreToolsButton` takes the switch's place, with the same 44 dp outline in a 48 dp touch target and the same alignment with the last string. It always shows the hammer and screwdriver, outlined while no tool is on and filled with `primaryContainer` (accent outline) while one is, so it says "a tool is on" without changing its icon. A tap opens a Material `DropdownMenu` that lifts softly off the screen (`shapes.medium`, `surfaceContainerHigh`, 8 dp shadow, no outline): one row per tool with its icon and name ("Strum check", "12th-fret check"), the one that is on in the accent with a check mark at its end (the check's room is kept on every row, so the menu doesn't change width). Picking a tool turns it on and the other off; picking the ticked one turns it off. A long-press shows "More tools" in a tooltip. TalkBack: "More tools, button", with the tool that is on as its state ("12th-fret check"); each menu row is `selected` when on.
- **Tour:** the step keeps its place (`TourPart.STRUM`, the button's outline); where the button stands in the switch's place its text is "More tools, like strum check" (`tourTextOf`, passed to `TourHost` from `MainActivity`).
- **Phone check of the icon** (Xiaomi 12X, 2026-10-16): the button off and on, light and dark, and its menu, on the installed debug app; the UI tests cover the styles, on its side, 2× text and TalkBack.

## 12th-fret check view
Phase 29, behind `ToolSwitches.intonationCheck` (hidden in release builds until it goes live, Phase 34). The comparison and its numbers: [Notes, tunings and audio](notes-and-audio.md), *Intonation check*. Built from the owner's pick, option A of `docs/design/intonation-preview.html`, without a second mock-up.
- **Like the strum check:** turned on from the menu, guitar mode only (chromatic turns it off; turning it on from chromatic goes to guitar mode), one tool at a time, not saved, the microphone kept open. `TunerViewModel` holds the chosen string and an attempt count (`TunerOptions.intonationString` / `intonationAttempt`); a new tuning goes back to choosing; an instrument without frets turns it off.
- **Strings:** a tap on a string checks it (TalkBack's action "Check A 2"; while a reference tone sounds, a tap stops it, as anywhere). The string being checked is highlighted (no lock ring). Each string with a result carries a small badge on its button's top end corner, drawn over it and outside its box so the row doesn't move: a check mark on the "in tune" colour (fine), or an arrow on the accent pointing as in the panel's drawing, where the neck is on the left (→ back, ← forward); TalkBack says it as the button's state ("move saddle back"). The mock-up had the marks under the strings, but the row sits at the bottom of its slot with no room under it.
- **Panel** (`IntonationPanel`, in the meter's place at its 2:1 shape, on the calm field, sized by its width like the strum panel): at the top "● Open — ○ 12th fret" (the current and done steps' dots filled in the accent). Choosing with no result yet: "Which string?". The open string and the 12th fret: a ring round the note asked for (A₂, then A₃) that fills while the steady note is taken (`progress`, in 5 % steps, so a second of collecting sends 20 states); TalkBack hears a progress bar named after the step. The verdict: the string seen from the side, the neck at the left and the saddle (a small triangle) at the right, labelled "neck" and "saddle"; under the saddle an arrow for the move or a check mark; under it how far the 12th fret is from the octave ("8 cents sharp", green when fine, else the accent; never red). Once any string has a result, choosing shows one column per string instead (its name, its mark on a disc, its cents "+8" / "−6", or an empty ring and "–" when not checked yet), each a button that checks that string, as on the string row; TalkBack: "A 2, move saddle back, 8 cents sharp" or "B 3, not checked yet".
- **Line under it** (`IntonationStatus`, in the note's place): "Choose a string" / "Tune it first, then check it"; "Play the open A2 string" / "Let it ring"; "Tune the A2 string first" / "23 cents flat"; "Now press it at the 12th fret" / "Press it down, not a light touch" (a light touch gives the harmonic, which always sounds the exact octave); "That wasn't the octave" / "Press it at the 12th fret and play again"; the verdict "Move the saddle back" (accent) / "Away from the neck, about 3 mm", "Move the saddle forward" / "Towards the neck, about 2 mm", "Fine" (in-tune colour) / "Leave the saddle where it is"; after the strings, "2 saddles to move" or "Every string checked is fine" / "Tap a string to check it". Millimetres only on guitars, to the nearest half millimetre ("less than 0.5 mm" below that), formatted in the app's language; elsewhere the direction only. Each line takes the room of its longest text (`TallestOf`), so nothing moves between steps; the lines are one polite live region whose spoken text names the string as words ("Play the open A 2 string"). A message changes only when a step's collection ends (about a second), so nothing flickers.
- **Buttons:** under the lines, only with the verdict, "Check again" (outlined) and "Next string" (filled): a fixed 148 dp each whatever the label, the same height (`SameSizePair`: a label that needs two lines at 2× text makes both taller), 12 dp apart. Their room is kept in every step (`Kept`), so they appear without moving anything. "Next string" goes to the next string of the tuning; after the last, back to choosing, where every string's result shows.
- **Compact layout on its side:** the panel and the line beside each other like the meter and the note, the small string row under it with the string being checked.
- **Tests:** `IntonationInterpreterTest` and `IntonationFlowTest` (JVM: every step, the open string ringing on, an octave misread, tune first, not the octave, results kept, one tool at a time, the violin, the switch off, a new tuning; synthetic plucks through the pipeline and the ViewModel: A2, then A3 8 cents sharp, says back). UI: `IntonationCheckStylesTest` (every style in a thorough run: upright on a narrow phone and on the Xiaomi at 1.25×, every step in view and the line not moving; Dutch at 2× upright and on its side wholly shown, the buttons too; every language at 2× overnight; the buttons the same size; the menu; the v1.0 switch with one tool; TalkBack; and the real `TunerScreen` and ViewModel with a fake microphone playing synthetic plucks: menu, A string, open, fret, verdict "about 3 mm" and the A string's badge; `-a intonationScreens=true` saves a picture of each step).
- **Phone check** (Xiaomi 12X, 2026-10-16): the more-tools button (then a ^), its menu, choosing a string and the open step on the installed debug app; the verdict through the fake microphone (synthetic plucks, `IntonationCheckStylesTest`). The laptop's speaker didn't reach the phone that day (the tuner itself heard nothing either), so a real guitar is the owner's check (ROADMAP *Owner checks*).

## Chord name view
Phase 30, behind `ToolSwitches.chordNaming` (hidden in release builds until it goes live, Phase 34). Built from the owner's pick of 2026-10-16, option B of `docs/design/chord-mode-preview.html`. The detector: [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md), *Chord naming*.
- **Entry:** the third tool in the more-tools menu, "Chord name" / "Akkoordnaam" (a chord box icon), like the strum check and the 12th-fret check: guitar mode only (chromatic turns it off; turning it on from chromatic goes to guitar mode), one tool at a time, not saved, the microphone kept open. A tap on a string leaves it and tunes that string (locked), as from the strum check; an instrument it isn't offered for turns it off.
- **Listening:** `PitchPipeline.listen(chordA4Hz = …)` sends the microphone's chunks to `ChordAnalysis` (a `StrumListener` and a `ChordDetector`, made the first time) while the tool runs and no reference tone (or its echo) sounds: a `ChordReading` with each reading of a strum (its `Chord?` and bass, named at the tuner's A4), when a strum starts or stops ringing, and every 0.1 s of audio between, so time passes while nothing rings. A reference tone starts it over.
- **Settling** (`ChordSettler`, `:core:dsp`, JVM-tested; `ChordNameInterpreter` makes the state): each reading is named on its own; a name shows once it wins 4 of the strum's last 6 readings (about a second after the strum), stays at least 1.5 s, and is replaced only when another chord wins 4 of 6; the bass joins it only when it agrees in 4 of 6 too. Readings without a chord don't erase it: it stays, faded, once the strum stops ringing and while a new strum hasn't named anything yet. A strum that names nothing for 1.5 s shows "Not a clear chord" (at least 2 s), never a guess; a sound too short for a reading (a knock) changes nothing. The last three chords shown are kept.
- **Panel** (`ChordPanel`, in the meter's place at its 2:1 shape, on the calm field, sized by its width like the strum panel; every style's colours and faces): the symbol big ("Am"; a slash chord's "/E" smaller and muted: "C/E"), its name in words under it ("A minor" / "A mineur", "C major, E in the bass" / "C majeur, E in de bas"), then the chord's notes as small circles in the accent's tint (A · C · E, the 7th chords four) with the bass filled in the accent and labelled "bass", and the last three chords along the bottom, the current one in the ink colour (left out while the current one is the only chord so far). Faded: 38 % (the words a little less), eased over 0.3 s. Waiting: a faint ♪; a strum heard but not named yet: the ♪ with three soft dots; not a clear chord: a faint "?". TalkBack hears the panel as one item in words: "A minor, notes A, C, E, before that G major, C major" (note names spoken: "B flat major").
- **Spelling** (`ChordSpelling`, `:core:dsp`, JVM-tested): the symbol and the notes in the player's note-name system (letters, German H and B: "Hm", B♭ written "B"; solfège: "Lam", "Do/Mi"), with the usual chord spelling for sharps and flats whatever the sharps/flats setting: the sign of the key the chord is named after (the major key for major, 7th, maj7 and sus chords, the minor key for minor and m7): B♭, E♭, A♭, D♭ but F♯; F♯m, C♯m, G♯m but E♭m, B♭m; F7 has an E♭, C7 a B♭, A7 a C♯. Endings: "", m, 7, maj7, m7, sus2, sus4. A tone with no name of its own here (E♯ in F♯maj7) takes the same note's (F). Words per type are translated (`tuner_chord_major` …; Dutch majeur, mineur, septiem, groot septiem, mineur septiem, sus 2, sus 4).
- **Line under it** (`ChordStatus`, in the note's place): "Strum a chord" / "All the strings of it" while waiting; "Listening…" / "Let it ring"; once named "Strum a chord" / "Strum again for the next"; "Not a clear chord" / "Strum all the strings of it". Each line takes the room of its longest text (`TallestOf`), so nothing moves; the lines are one polite live region, which once a chord is named says it in words ("A minor, Strum again for the next").
- **Compact layout on its side:** the panel and the line beside each other like the meter and the note; no string row under them.
- **Tests:** `ChordSettlerTest` and `ChordSpellingTest` (`:core:dsp`); `ChordNameFlowTest` (JVM: synthetic `PluckSynth` strums through the pipeline and the ViewModel: Am named and faded as it dies away, never another name; G, C and C/E one after the other; a single note is not a clear chord; one tool at a time, chromatic, a tapped string, basses, the switch off). UI: `ChordNameStylesTest` (every style in a thorough run, light and dark: upright on a narrow phone and on the Xiaomi at 1.25×, every phase in view and the line not moving; Dutch at 2× upright and on its side wholly shown; every language at 2× overnight; the menu; TalkBack in English and Dutch, solfège; and the real `TunerScreen` and ViewModel with a fake microphone playing a synthetic Am and C/E strum; `-a chordScreens=true` saves a picture of each). `ToolSwitchesOffTest` checks the release app shows none of it.
- **Phone check** (Xiaomi 12X, 2026-10-16): every phase light and dark, and the real screen naming the synthetic Am and C/E strums through the fake microphone. Real chords on a real guitar are the owner's check (ROADMAP *Owner checks*).

## Pitch trail view

To be built as follows:
- **Entry:** a tool in the more-tools menu, "Pitch trail", one tool at a time like the others.
- **Panel** in the meter's place at its 2:1 shape (the needle gives way to it, so every layout places it like the meter), on the calm field like the strum panel, sized by its width, no frame or hard edge. The note name, the cents pill and the string row stay as they are.
- **The line:** time runs from right to left, the newest reading at the right (a little in from the edge) with a dot and a soft glow round it; **4 s** shown. Up is sharp, down is flat, ±50 cents over the panel's height (clamped there), with faint hairlines at ±25 and small ♯ / ♭ marks at the right end. The oldest third fades out to the left.
- **In tune:** the centre line is in tune, drawn as a soft band of ±5 cents (the tuner's tolerance) in the "in tune" colour, faint, glowing a little brighter while the note is in tune now (eased, no flash). The parts of the line that were in tune take the "in tune" colour, the rest the accent; the head dot likewise.
- **Gaps** (`NO_NOTE`: silence, a reference tone, another tool) break the line, and the gap scrolls away with it.
- The mock-up's line is about 2.4 dp at 0.8 px per dp (≈ 3 dp on the phone); the exact sizes and the other styles' marks are decided when it is built.

## Tour
On first start a short tour points out the tuner's main parts, one at a time, as the approved mock-up [docs/design/tour-preview.html](../design/tour-preview.html) (its top comment is the design; owner decided 2026-10-14). Code: `:feature:tuner` `tour/` (`TourHost` and the overlay in `TourOverlay.kt`, the pure `TourGeometry.kt`, `TourPart`, `TourTargets`), wired in `MainActivity` (its rules in `FirstStartTour.kt`). Its strings step ("Tap to lock, hold to hear") is how people find the string lock.
- **Layer:** `TourHost` wraps the whole window (screen and navigation bar or rail) and lays the tour on top, never in the layout, so nothing moves when it comes or goes; under the dim layer the balloon may cover any part. Taps outside the balloon are swallowed (the lit part isn't live, so the tour never changes a setting); Back = Skip tour. While it runs the screen below is `clearAndSetSemantics {}`: TalkBack (and UI Automator) find only the balloon.
- **Parts:** each marks itself with `Modifier.tourTarget(part)` (a `Modifier.Node` that reports its unclipped bounds in the window to `TourTargets`, found through `LocalTourTargets`; the pieces of one part are joined): `MODE`, `METER` (the meter look, also with the wave display, or the strum panel in its place), `NOTE` (the note's block: its name with the cents pill and frequency, or with the wave), `PICKER` (the navigation bar's or rail's Instrument item), `STRUM` (the strum check switch's outline), `STRINGS`, `NAVIGATION` (the bar's or rail's Metronome and Settings tabs, not Tuner). The Tuner tab marks itself with `tourControl()`: no step's part, but a control the balloons keep clear of. The strum switch and strings mark themselves only in guitar mode (`inTour`), since chromatic mode keeps them composed but unplaced and their old bounds mustn't count. A part out of view in a scrolling column (very large fonts) is brought into view (`bringIntoView`). Steps (`TourPart.stepsFor`): all seven in guitar mode (strum check left out where it isn't offered; every instrument has it today); in chromatic mode (only when started from Settings) `MODE`, `METER`, `NOTE`, `PICKER`, `NAVIGATION`, "1 of 5".
- **Order** (`tourOrder`; owner, 2026-10-15: the balloons jumped around): the reading order of the parts in the current layout, the bar's or rail's parts always last (its Instrument item, then the Metronome and Settings pair). `readingOrder` is an XY cut on the parts' bounds: blocks side by side with a clear gap from top to bottom (the two panes) left to right, each on its own; inside a block, rows (boxes whose heights overlap) top to bottom; in a row, left to right. Side by side goes first so a pane scrolled on its own (large fonts) never reorders the tour; upright the full-width meter keeps everything one block. Upright: mode switch, meter, note, strum check, strings, then the bar's Instrument item and tabs. On a phone on its side (the compact tuner shows the two panes during the tour) and in the two-pane layout: meter, note, then mode switch, strum check, strings, then the rail's (or bar's) Instrument item and tabs. The overlay derives the order from the live bounds; parts not laid out yet go after the others, before the bar. The step index is saved, so a turned phone carries on at the same step number in its own order. In a right-to-left language (Arabic) the screen is mirrored and `readingOrder(rightToLeft = true)` reads side-by-side blocks right to left, so upright the order stays mode switch, meter, note, strum check (now at the strings' left end), strings, bar; the balloons are placed in window coordinates (`place`, not `placeRelative`), so they follow the mirrored parts, while Skip tour and Next swap sides with the layout.
- **Dim and spotlight:** on a layer of its own, dark themes the style's background mixed 50 % with black at 80 % (it keeps the style's hue), light themes the background mixed 60 % with ink at 80 % (a clear grey in the hue; 40 % at 72 % measured only 2× on the Xiaomi); with a lighter dim the owner found the lit part hardly stood out. The target, checked in phone screenshots: the lit part at least 3× as bright as the same kind of part under the dim. The spotlight is cut out (`DST_OUT`): the part plus 8 dp, kept in the window, its corner the part's (the mock-up's button, string-button or card radius per style, `tourCornerDp`) plus 8 dp, at most half the hole; Futuristic's corners cut (at most 12 dp); a soft 14 dp edge (`BlurMaskFilter`, Android 9+; before that a sharp edge). A soft halo hugs the edge outside: the style's accent, 28 % at the edge in dark themes and 20 % in light, fading to nothing over 12 dp (`BlurMaskFilter.Blur.OUTER` on the hole's outline, so it follows Futuristic's cut corners; Android 9+, none before). It is steady, glides with the spotlight and fades with the dim; no outline, no pulse.
- **Balloon:** no outline; filled with the accent (`primary`), text in on-accent; above the text the style's mark (`NavMark`, as under the open tab) and the step count ("2 of 5", 12 sp, at 78 %); the text 16 sp; Skip tour (a text button) and Next / Done (filled on-accent with accent text; Futuristic a neon outline) side by side, or on two rows where they don't fit (`TourButtonsPolicy`); the last step has nothing left to skip, so Done stands alone at the end. `shapes.medium` corners; Next the style's button shape with at most 20 dp corners. A soft two-layer shadow (Futuristic: an accent glow) under the body and the 14 × 8 dp pointer, drawn with `setShadowLayer` (Android 9+). Its width hugs the text (`IntrinsicSize.Max`), at most min(window less its side margins, 260 dp × font scale); it grows with the font without a cap. The buttons keep 48 dp touch targets, so the bottom padding is 4 dp instead of the mock-up's 8.
- **Placement** (`placeBalloon`, JVM-tested): measured from the part's own element, not the spotlight: the pointer's tip ends 2 dp from the element (the balloon may lie over the spotlight's 8 dp pad and soft edge, never over the element). The step's preferred side (mode switch, meter, note and strings below; instrument button, strum check and bar above) if the balloon fits between 40 dp from the top and 24 dp from the bottom (or the system bars, if more); else the other side. Where the preferred side would cover another interactive control (`TourTargets.controlsBesides`: the mode switch, instrument button, strum switch, string row and the bar's tabs) and the other side fits without covering one, the other side (upright on the Xiaomi the note's balloon goes above, over the meter, instead of onto the instrument button). Where neither fits: beside the part (right, then left, again a clear one first: the rail on a phone on its side, an addition to the mock-up); where it fits on no side at its full width, it narrows to the room beside the part (at least 200 dp, `MIN_BESIDE_WIDTH`), taller, if it then fits there (the strings' step at 2× text on a phone on its side); else over the part on the side with more room, without a pointer. Under the dim it may still cover the meter and the note. Where no side is clear it takes the one that fits: upright the strings' and the bar's balloons go above over the instrument button and the strings, since below there is no room. Centred on the part, at least 16 dp (or the cutout) from the sides; the pointer at the element's middle (the bar step: the middle of the Metronome and Settings pair), at least 27 dp from the balloon's ends; the strum switch at the row's end clamps the balloon, the pointer stays over the switch. `TourTargets.shown` (`TourShown`) says which step is on screen and where its balloon and pointer tip are, for tests.
- **Motion:** the dim fades in (300 ms), then the balloon (200 ms); Next fades the balloon out (150 ms) while the spotlight glides (300 ms, cubic ease in-out: position, size and corners together), then the next balloon fades in; Skip tour, Done and Back fade it all out (250 ms). With "remove animations" every change is instant (`rememberAnimationsOff`). The balloon fades with `CompositingStrategy.ModulateAlpha`, so its pointer and shadow, outside its box, aren't clipped.
- **Once:** `tourDue` = asked for from Settings, or not `tourSeen` and not ended in this session; `tourShown` = due, on the tuner, and the tuner itself shows (the microphone allowed: `TunerScreen(onMicGrantedChange)`), so it comes after the microphone question. Skip tour, Back and Done end it for good: `tourSeen` is saved. Updates never show it: `decideTourOnce()` runs at every start and, while `tour_seen` is missing, saves it as seen if any other setting is saved (an install from before the tour, or one restored from a backup; an old key such as `string_lock_tip_seen` counts too) and as not seen on an empty file, so a fresh user who changes a setting before allowing the microphone still gets the tour. The step is saved (`rememberSaveable`), so a turned phone carries on where it was; after it ended it starts again at step 1. "Show me around" in Settings opens the tuner and starts it again.
- **Beside the tuner:** the compact layout on its side shows its controls and the rail while the tour runs (`TunerContent(controlsAlwaysShown)`). The Baseline Profile journeys skip the tour by its `tour_skip` tag.
- **Texts** (`tour_*` in `:feature:tuner`, Dutch beside them): "Tune strings or any note" / "Stem snaren of elke noot", "How far off you are" / "Hoe ver je ernaast zit", "The note you play" / "De noot die je speelt", "Pick instrument and tuning" / "Kies instrument en stemming", "Strum to check every string" / "Sla aan en check alle snaren", "Tap to lock, hold to hear" / "Kies om vast te zetten, houd vast om te horen" (it names the reference tone's long-press too), "Metronome and settings" / "Metronoom en instellingen"; Skip tour / Overslaan, Next / Volgende, Done / Klaar. TalkBack: a pane titled "Show me around" ("Rondleiding") reading "Step 6 of 7. Tap to lock, hold to hear." (a polite live region, so Next reads the new step), then the two buttons.
- **Tests:** `TourGeometryTest` (JVM: the spotlight, every placement case, the pointer's tip 2 dp from its own element and over it upright and on its side, the bar pair's middle, balloons off other controls where the other side is clear, the order upright, on its side, scrolled, in chromatic mode and with the strum switch on its own row, the glide, the steps per mode); `FirstStartTourTest` (JVM, `:app`: when it shows); `DataStoreSettingsRepositoryTest` (a fresh install is due, an update has seen it, a decided fresh install stays due); `TourStylesTest` (`:app`, every style in a thorough run: each step's balloon, text and both buttons wholly shown inside the window in Dutch at 2× on a 360 dp phone, on a phone on its side with the compact tuner's controls, in the two-pane layout and (thorough) on the Xiaomi and a tablet, in the layout's order, each pointer's tip over its own part and 2 dp from it, the balloon never over its part; chromatic's four steps, upright and on its side; Skip tour and Back end it; started again it begins at step 1; TalkBack hears only the pane; taps outside do nothing; nothing in the tuner or bar moves); `TourFlowTest` (`:app`, UI Automator on the real app and its saved settings: due it shows and hides the tuner, Back ends it for good, also after a restart, and Settings, Show me around starts it again; a Compose test rule can't start `MainActivity`, whose opening screen waits for a frame its test clock never gives); `SettingsContentTest` (the row).

## Display
Setting "Display": `TunerDisplay` **needle** (cents in a pill and the frequency) or **wave** (the default, in the picked wave look). The meter is there with both. The wave takes the cents' place, so `NoteDisplay` and `TunerContent` lay it out differently: no pill colour, no frequency line, the check mark in the "in tune" colour, `ChromaticReadout` in chromatic mode, the compact note on a phone on its side.
