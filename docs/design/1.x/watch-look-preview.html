Mock-up · Phase 61 · the watch tuner's look

A finer look, a bigger marker

Today's watch tuner beside four options, same colours, same calm in-tune glow. Every option has a bigger marker; they differ in what else the ring shows. All five play the same string together.

NOW

Today

A thin ring, the ticks at the top, a 14 dp dot.

A

Bigger dot

Only the marker changes: half as big again, with a soft light round it. The smallest step.

B

Lit arc

A big dot, and the ring lit from the middle to it, so how far off you are shows as a length. ♭ and ♯ at the scale's ends.

C

Needle

A short needle pointing in from the ring, like the phone's meter, over a finer scale with a tick every 5 cents.

D

Deep glass

The fanciest: a faint deep-blue light behind the note, a scale band that brightens toward the middle, a large ring-shaped marker, and the note itself glowing softly in tune.
