# Twangle — Roadmap

Status legend: ☐ todo · ◐ in progress · ☑ done

Each phase ends in a state that builds and can be demoed. Phases are worked in the order they are listed here, not by number. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) and its topic files for design details.

Every finished phase is in [ROADMAP-ARCHIVE.md](ROADMAP-ARCHIVE.md), with the overview of the music tools that went into v1.0 and the finished part of Phase 23; only the open phases are below. Move a phase there once all its items are ☑, and finished owner items too. Ideas not planned yet live in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).

If that leaves the open phases' numbers out of order, renumber them so they count up again (finished phases keep theirs) and add the mapping to the archive's opening note, which lists the renumberings of 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-27; commits, PRs and branches from before a renumbering use the old numbers.

## Phase 58 — Open the code after v1.0
Goal: the Twangle repository goes public as open source, once v1.0 is live on Play. The licence is the **GPL version 3** (owner, 2026-10-17): anyone may copy, change and share the code, but what they build from it stays open under the same licence, so nobody can turn Twangle into a closed app with ads. The bundled fonts keep their own SIL Open Font License, and the AndroidX and Kotlin libraries' Apache 2.0 fits inside GPL 3.
- ☐ `LICENSE` with the full GPL 3 text, and a *Licence* section in README.md naming it
- ☐ README.md says the name "Twangle" and its icon are not covered by the licence, so copies must use their own name and icon
- ☐ Before opening: check the history for anything that shouldn't be public (the owner's personal email in old commits, `local.properties`, keys); the owner decides whether to keep the history or start the public repository fresh
- ☐ The owner makes the repository public; later commits use the GitHub no-reply email
- ☐ While the owner is the only author, the licence can still change; once others contribute, it can't without them, so settle any change before accepting outside code
- ☐ Google's rating window (Phase 50) comes from a library under Google's own terms, not open source: F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid flag such a library, and GPL 3 alone doesn't allow it in a copy someone else builds. Settle it before opening: a build without it for F-Droid (`RatingWindow.NONE`, e.g. a `foss` flavour), and either a short GPL exception for that one library in `LICENSE`, or the Play build only made by the owner

## Phase 59 — Chord charts and scales for the banjo (owner, 2026-10-27)
Goal: the 5-string banjo from Phase 57 gets the Practice tools the guitar has, so a beginner can tune and then learn the first chords (G, C, D7) without leaving Twangle. Today Chords, Scales and the Capo helper show a quiet line in their place for it (`ShortStringLine`, gated on `Tuning.hasShortString`), because the charts and the shape finder treat every string as starting at the nut, and on a guitar a string outside the chord is muted. The banjo's short fifth string starts at the 5th fret, and players let it ring as a drone. Visible changes land with their UI tests, every language where words change, both hands (Phase 57's left-handed switch), and the Practice doc. In order, Capo last:
- ☐ **Rough mock-up first** (Contemporary, one row per idea), for **the owner** to pick: a banjo chord chart (G, C, D7) with the short string drawn starting at the 5th fret, open as a drone or fretted above it; whether the open short string always rings, or only where its note belongs to the chord (and is otherwise fretted or marked unplayed); and the Scales neck with the short string
- ☐ **The shape finder knows the short string:** in `:core:dsp`, a tuning's strings can start at a fret (the banjo's fifth at 5), so no shape frets that string below its start; the drone rule the owner picked decides when it rings open. `ChordShapeFinderTest`'s every-tuning checks stop leaving out `hasShortString` tunings (every shape sounds the chord on open G, double C and double D, within the hand's reach), plus banjo cases: the common first shapes come out first (G open, C, D7)
- ☐ **Chord charts for the banjo:** `ChordChart` draws the short string from its own start fret (mirrored with the left-handed switch), Chords offers the banjo and its line goes; the chord's sound plays its five strings in the banjo voice. `ChordsScreenTest` with the banjo, both hands, TalkBack naming the short string; every language at 2× text
- ☐ **Scales for the banjo:** `ScaleNeck` (upright and on its side) draws the short string from the 5th fret, with no notes below it; Scales offers the banjo and its line goes. `ScalesScreenTest` with the banjo, both hands
- ☐ **Capo, only if players ask:** on a banjo the short string needs its own capo (often small spikes in the neck), so the helper's sums differ; until then Capo keeps its quiet line for the banjo, and this item moves to [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md) with that condition if Phase 59 is otherwise done

## Phase 60 — Meters that read to the cent (owner, 2026-10-29)
Goal: every meter look reads nearly as precisely as Classic, which the owner keeps going back to. Spirit level and Eclipse go (the owner's least favourite, and the least precise); **Pointers** and **Loupe** take their places; Light arc, Arrows and Light bars get sharper. Picked by the owner from two rounds of the moving mock-up [docs/design/meter-precision-preview.html](docs/design/meter-precision-preview.html) (its top comment has the numbers; turned down: *Horizon*, a tilting line, because which way to tune wasn't clear, and *Lift*, an up-and-down look). **Can be built before launch: `/roadmap-step 60`** (owner, 2026-10-29: "I will let my desktop build this").
Why Classic reads best, and so the rules for the others: its needle tip moves ~4.3u per cent, it has a tick every 5 cents to compare against, and a thin needle meets a thin centre bar (the eye lines up two thin lines very precisely). The others move 1.8–2.4u per cent, step 6–10 cents at a time or show a ~10-cent-wide soft peak, with nothing fixed to compare against. So each new or sharpened look uses: **the stretched middle** (`meterStretch`, in `:core:designsystem` `meter/`: position = sign · 50 · (1 + k)|x| / (1 + k|x|), x = cents / 50, k = 1.5; 2.5× the movement per cent at the centre, 0.4× at ±50, 50 stays 50; never on Classic, whose numbers would go uneven), **a few fixed marks without numbers**, **thin edges instead of soft blobs**, and **the in-tune window** shown where the mock-up shows it: ±5 cents, ±2 with the Precise setting (`MeterFrame` hands the window to the look, read like the other `MeterReading` values). Everything else follows the meter art language ([ui-meter-and-styles.md](docs/architecture/ui-meter-and-styles.md)): roles only, the calm field, ♭ and ♯ inside the box, one soft in-tune glow, drawn once and moved by layers, nothing allocated per frame, a resting centre never in tune. Each increment updates that doc (and `history.md` for what goes), keeps `MeterLooksTest` passing in every style light and dark, runs `scripts/meter-frames.sh` for its look, and gets a phone check. In order:
- ☑ **Pointers instead of Spirit level:** a fixed pointer above the scale's middle (tip 7u above the track, 18u wide, 24u tall, in the centre colour), the note's pointer below it pointing up (same size, ink, with the marker shadow), sliding along a straight track (±150u at ±50, stretched: ~7.5u per cent near in tune), and a band of light along the track from the middle to the note's pointer (8u high, round ends, the accent from 8 % at the middle to 80 % dark / 65 % light at the pointer). Ticks above the track at 1, 2, 3, 4 cents (6u, ink 40 %), 5 and 10 (12u, ink 80 %), 20 to 50 (9u, ink 50 %). In tune the two tips meet; the glow and colours as the other looks. Feel 1.9 Hz / 0.8. It takes Spirit level's place in the picker, and a saved Spirit level opens as Pointers. Its name "Pointers" in English and Dutch ("Wijzers"), with a translator note; Spirit level's name goes from every language. JVM tests for the pointer's place and the band (`PointersTest`, replacing `SpiritLevelTest`)
- ☐ **Loupe instead of Eclipse:** a straight scale (3u per cent, ±150u, ticks every 5 cents as on the open face) with a slim marker (3u × 44u, ink); over its middle a round magnifying glass (radius 54u) lying on the screen (the art language's glass: contact shadow, 1u rim in ink at 14 %, one sheen from the top). Through the glass everything is magnified 3× at its centre, easing to 1× at its rim (x' = R · h(x / R), h as the stretch with k = 2), so nothing hides under it: a tick per cent where they are at least 4u apart, ticks and marker up to 1.8× as long, the centre mark in the centre colour, the in-tune window as a quiet band (track colour, fading toward the glass's top and bottom; never green at rest). Under the glass ~9u per cent. Feel 1.8 Hz / 0.8. It takes Eclipse's place in the picker, and a saved Eclipse opens as Loupe. Its name "Loupe" in English and Dutch ("Loep"), with a translator note; Eclipse's name goes from every language. JVM tests for the glass's mapping (continuous at the rim, 3× at the centre) and the marker's place (`LoupeTest`, replacing `EclipseTest`)
- ☐ **Light arc, sharper:** the same 17 rays, evenly spaced, but in stretched cents, so the first ray lights at about 3 cents, then 6, 10, 14, 20, 27, 37 and 50; tiny ink dots just past the outer lights mark 5 cents (1.8u, 55 %) and 10 cents (1.3u, 32 %); the fading trail 0.12 s instead of 0.22. `LightArcHeatTest` and the ray tests follow
- ☐ **Arrows, sharper:** the chevrons light at 2, 5, 10 and 20 cents, each filling over its own step (full at 5, 10, 20 and 40), instead of one per 10.5 cents; one lit arrow means "almost there". `ArrowsTest` follows
- ☐ **Light bars, sharper, with a clear centre:** the 41 bars in stretched cents (one per cent near the middle); the peak narrower (σ 2.6 stretched units, 3.6 as the note is reached); the bars of the in-tune window a little taller (8u) and tinted 14 % toward ink, blending toward "in tune" as it is reached. The centre bar stands out: 5.6u wide, always taller than the peak ((16 + 64 · peak)u each way), 85 % of the way to the accent, with a 3.2u dot in the centre colour 89u above and below the middle; reaching the note, only the centre bar and its dots turn fully "in tune" (the lit bars beside it at most 35 %). `LightBarsTest` follows
- ☐ **What shows the looks:** README.md's list of meter looks; any store screenshot, website picture or Settings card test that shows Spirit level or Eclipse by name (the store screenshots are retaken at the next overnight run, so only their scripts change)
