Show me around: the first-start tour

The first time Twangle opens, the screen dims and lights up one part at a time, each with a short balloon: the note, the strings, the instrument button, the mode switch and the bar at the bottom. The balloons float on their own layer above the screen, so nothing in the tuner moves. Step through it on the phone below with Next and Skip tour, or let it play by itself. Experienced players lose nothing: one tap on Skip tour (or Back) ends the whole tour for good, and people updating from an earlier Twangle never see it.

    What to look at

    1. 1The dim and the light. Everything but one part fades back in the style's own background colour, and that part stays bright. Calm enough, light and dark? Try Futuristic and Ancient in the dark too.
    2. 2The balloon, round 2. No hard border any more: the balloon lifts off the dimmed screen with a soft shadow (in Futuristic, a glow), and carries the style's own mark, the one under the open tab in the bottom bar. Three fills to compare with the Balloon switch: Card (the style's plain card colour), Accent (the style's own colour, bold) and Glass (frosted, the screen faintly showing through). Which one, and does it suit every style, light and dark?
    3. 3The hardest case. Tap Hardest: Dutch, 2×, 360 dp. Balloons grow, flip above or below their part, or cover other parts. Under the dim layer that's allowed (your choice of 14 October); what matters is that the whole balloon and both buttons stay on the screen.
    4. 4The words. Short enough? Right in Dutch? The table beside the phone has every text.
    5. 5The movement. The light glides to the next part in a third of a second and the balloon fades in after it. Too slow, too fast, or just right?
    6. 6Out of the way. Skip tour sits in every balloon and ends everything in one tap, no "are you sure". Clear enough?
    7. 7The order. Note, strings, instrument button, mode switch, bar. Would you change it, or leave a step out?
