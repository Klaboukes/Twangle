Fewer buttons above the chart

Today's screen has about 22 framed buttons above the chart. Here are three calmer ways to pick a chord or a scale. In all three the chart or neck gets more room. The tool switch, the tuning line and the ‹ 1 of 6 › pager stay as they are.

A · The name is the button calmest

Only the chord's name shows ("C major ⌄"). Tap it and a sheet slides up with the 12 roots and the types. Tap a type and the sheet closes. Changing chord takes two taps instead of one.

B · Plain rows, no frames my pick

The roots become one line of plain letters, the picked one in a filled circle. The types become one line of plain words, the picked one underlined. Still one tap per change, but no boxes at all. The five scale names are longer, so their line slides sideways.

C · Two pickers side by side

One row with two equal pickers: the root and the type. Tap one and a small grid opens right under it. Two taps per change, like A, but you always see both parts. The pickers say the name, so the separate name line goes.
