Signal bars · rough mock-up

Where should the signal bars go?

Today they sit at the end of the line under the note, so they jump sideways each time the text changes. Every option below keeps them in one spot that never moves. Each phone shows the screen with no note and with a note, so you can see that nothing shifts.

With A, B and C the line under the note no longer carries the bars, so it simply stays centred. In all four, a tap on the bars still opens the panel with Volume, Background and Clarity.

Phone on its side and focus mode: the bars take the same kind of spot there (A: the top corner of the note's side, B: by the buttons, D: with the note).
