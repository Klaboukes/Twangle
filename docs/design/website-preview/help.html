Help

Short answers to what players run into. Tap a question.

The microphone

Twangle says the microphone is turned off

Twangle can't hear your strings without it. Tap Open settings in Twangle, then Permissions, then Microphone, and choose to allow it while using the app.

"Another app is using the microphone"

Only one app can listen at a time. Close the other app (a call, a voice recorder, a video chat), then go back to Twangle.

Does Twangle record me?

No. The sound is analysed live on your phone and thrown away. Twangle has no permission to use the internet, so it couldn't send anything even if it wanted to. The privacy policy explains it in plain words.

Tuning

The needle won't settle

Play one string at a time and let it ring; touch the other strings lightly so they stay quiet. Hold the phone close to the instrument, away from a fan, a TV or people talking.

Twangle shows the wrong string

Check that the instrument and tuning above the strings match yours. If a string is far out of tune, Twangle may take it for its neighbour: tap the string you are tuning so Twangle stays on it.

My band or orchestra tunes to 442 Hz

Set it in Settings → Reference pitch (A4). Every note then follows.

The app

The screen turns off while I tune

Turn on Settings → Keep screen on. The screen then stays on while the tuner is open.

Is Twangle really free?

Yes. Every feature, for good: no ads, no purchases, no account. Twangle is made by one person who wanted a tuner like this.
