# Translation glossary

Part of §6 of [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), next to [Languages](languages.md). The words Twangle already uses for its
recurring terms, per language, so that texts translated at night (the `/translate` skill) say "string", "fret" or
"in tune" the way the rest of the app does. They were taken from the app's own texts on 2026-10-28 (a few added by the
meaning review of 2026-10-29, [findings](../translation-review-2026-10-29.md)); the app's
texts win where this list and a text differ, and a translator who settles a new recurring term adds it here. A
language added with `/add-language` gets its section in the same change. The register (informal or formal you)
and the typography rules of each language are in [Languages](languages.md), *Languages and registers*.

Each line is English = the language's word, as shown in the app (`\'` is Android's escaped apostrophe).
