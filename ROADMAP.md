## Owner checks (live tests)
Done by the owner by hand, with a real guitar or headset. They are not roadmap increments: `/roadmap-step` skips them.

- ☐ **When you have the gear** (postponed 2026-10-16, no hurry):
  - Phase 5, a Bluetooth headset: with one connected, the tuner still listens through the phone's mic and reads a string correctly
  - Wired earbuds with a mic (3.5 mm plug, or USB-C), for a noisy room (2026-10-31): with them plugged in, Android listens through the earbud mic, which Twangle allows (only a Bluetooth headset is overruled), so the mic can lie close to the guitar. Lay the mic on the guitar's top a few centimetres from the soundhole (not in it), hands off the cable, and tune all six strings, first in a quiet room, then with loud music from the laptop: the note and cents follow each string, the low E included, at least as quickly and steadily as with the phone's own mic, and better in the noise; the reference tone plays in the earbuds; pulling the plug goes back to the phone's mic without a restart. If it passes, one line on the website's help page in English and Dutch ("Noisy room? Plug in wired earbuds and lay the mic on the guitar"), no hint in the app; if the low strings fail, the line names guitar and ukulele only
  - Phase 15, a bass (or a 7-string guitar or cello): the lowest strings read correctly (B0, E1, B1, C2), and the tuner shows no note while the amp only hums
- ☐ **Phases 63 and 65 on the tablet and the watch** (2026-11-01, no rush; both were checked only in forced window sizes, on the phone and on the Wear OS emulator):
  - On the Lenovo tablet, upright and on its side, and on the Galaxy Watch: the buttons, pickers and switches look as on the phone (Phase 63)
  - On the Lenovo tablet, upright and on its side, in a light and a dark style (Phase 65, *Contrast and edges*): the string buttons and the round buttons stand out clearly from the background, the style's background runs under the navigation buttons (no white or black strip), and Settings' rows fade out under the title as you scroll
  - The upright tablet store screenshots (Phase 65, *The store pictures*): with the tablet connected, Claude runs `scripts/store-screenshots.sh` for English and Dutch; the pictures show one scaled-up column with no empty middle, every word readable
  - On the Galaxy Watch, TalkBack on (Phase 65, *TalkBack on the watch*): the dial is spoken in words, e.g. "A 2, 12 cents flat, Tighten" (2.0: the word on screen; 1.1 says "tune up") or "E 2, in tune", calmly (not on every small change), and "Another app is using the microphone." is spoken when one has it (a voice recorder open on the watch)
- ☐ **TalkBack's own voice** (the launch review, 2026-11-01; ~5 min). **Failed on 2026-11-06** on the Xiaomi with 2.0.0 from Play, in its first step: the tuner showed notes while only TalkBack spoke, and its spoken readings ("x cents sharp/flat") set off the next ones in a loop; Listen and Play it back weren't tried. Phase 81 fixes it; then the whole check again: TalkBack on (volume keys held 3 s, or *Settings → Accessibility*), no headphones, phone on the table, the tuner open with the speaker loud. Play nothing and let it talk, then tune two strings: the tuner never shows a note while only TalkBack speaks, and the reading doesn't set off the next one in a loop. Then the metronome's Listen and Ear training's Play it back the same way. If TalkBack's voice shows up as a note, or a reading sets off the next one, tell Claude: it adds a roadmap item that sets the readings aside while TalkBack speaks, the way the reference tone's own sound is set aside (`ignoredFrames`)

## 2.0 — the app designed again
Decided on 2026-11-02, from the from-scratch design of 2026-11-01 ([IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md), *A fresh start*, with the first mock-up [docs/design/fresh-start-preview.html](docs/design/fresh-start-preview.html): the navigation, the metronome, the Practice cards, Settings, the watch) and the mock-ups of 2026-11-02: [docs/design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html](docs/design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html) (the tuner: six single designs, then the chosen combination with each of the three looks in the lens, and focus mode on its side) and [docs/design/v2-one-look-preview.html](docs/design/v2-one-look-preview.html) (four candidate looks, Ink chosen, and the picker sheet). Each page's top comment lists its decisions. **Listed first because it is what sessions work on now;** built on the `v2` branch until it landed on `main` on 2026-11-06. Phases 53, 56 and 58 below are the owner's launch and after-launch work on `main`.

**The design, settled (owner, 2026-11-02):**
- **One look, Ink:** blue-grey dark, near-white light, a plain sans, rounded corners; light, dark or the phone's choice (the switch stays in Settings); the phone's wallpaper colours as the one personalisation (on by default, Android 12 and up; off by default since the owner's review of 2026-11-03; amber and green stay the app's own). The six styles, the meter looks and the wave looks go; a saved one reads as the one look.
- **The tuner screen:** the top row with the gear (top left), the tab's name "Tune" in the middle and the focus mode button (top right), and the picker chip "Guitar · Standard ▾" on its own line right under it (owner, 2026-11-04, Phase 77: "each screen should just have the screen's name there"; until then the chip stood between the gear and focus mode; a phone on its side keeps it in the row's middle, where the rail names the tab); the ruler (the chromatic scale sliding under a fixed pointer: which note); the lens below it (how far: left flat, right sharp, the tuner's own in-tune zone, ±5 cents or ±2 with Precise, green in tune, quiet when the string dies); the whole screen softly tinted amber far off and green in tune (how close); one word at the bottom, Tighten · Loosen · In tune, with the cents small under it; the pegs as a plain row, the one heard ringed, tap to lock, hold to hear; the signal bars, behind their switch in Settings as today, beside the ruler's heard note. No mode switch, no big note (the ruler is the note), no cents pill, no hertz, no wave view, no strings backdrop.
- **Three meter looks, the one choice a player gets (Settings):** Classic (the default, 1.1's needle in Ink: a needle on a numbered half-circle dial), the Light arc (as today: 17 rays of 5 lights on the stretched scale), the strobe (three bands drifting flat or sharp, still in tune). The meter's box is the contract; only what is drawn inside it changes. Stop at three. The meter sits straight on the page, with no frame round it (owner, 2026-11-04, Phase 78; until then a rounded lens with a border and the pitch trail as the default look). The pitch trail is a Practice tool of its own, for singers and players without frets, behind its switch.
- **The word or the cents** (owner, 2026-11-05: Tighten / Loosen is a beginner's hint, and to an experienced player it repeats what the needle shows while the number they look at stays small): Settings' Look asks, under the Meter cards, what goes under the meter, *Words* (the default: Tighten · Loosen, the cents small under it) or *Cents* (the cents big in the word's place, nothing under it). A choice of what fills the place, not a switch that empties it, so the screen never changes shape. In tune and "Play a string" read the same in both, and TalkBack always says the direction. Done in Phase 78 (until then the word always, with no setting, 2026-11-02).
- **The string tick-off** (a tuned string's peg gets a check, "In tune. Play." once all have one) is a beginner helper: present, behind a toggle in Settings, off by default.
- **Focus mode:** one button, no switch in Settings; the same screen laid out wide on its side (the ruler across the full width, the lens wide, the word and the string small under it, the exit button in the same top-right corner), the pegs and tabs back with a tap; the turning of the whole app, Back and the tap stay as they are.
- **The one picker:** the chip (on its own line under the top row, on the tuner and every Practice tool, since Phase 77) opens "What are you tuning?": instrument pills, that instrument's tunings with their strings, Edit tunings, Any note as a switch. Instrument, tuning and chromatic in one place.
- **Navigation:** three tabs, Tune · Metronome · Practice; Settings and About behind the gear; Practice as rows (Chords, Scales, Capo, Ear training, and the pitch trail since Phase 78; big cards until the owner's review of 2026-11-04) under a "Last used" shortcut; no tour.
- **The 12th-fret check** is a tuner feature, not a Practice tool: it stays on the tuner, started from the last row of "What are you tuning?" (the owner's pick B of Phase 71's mock-up, 2026-11-03; no Settings switch), ended by the × on its panel or Back.
- **The strum check is a button above the pegs, and the chord name is Listen in Chords** (owner, 2026-11-03, after the guitar check: deciding by itself whether a sound was one string, a strum or a chord flickered). Until then: strum check and chord name happened by themselves, no mode to switch on.
- **Under the hood nothing changes:** `:core:dsp`, `:core:audio`, `:core:settings`' storage, the tunings, the reference tones, the drone, the metronome clock, the charts, Ear training's questions, no internet, no account, no ads. The watch and the widget follow the one look.
- **A new logo, Harmonics** (owner, 2026-11-04, Phase 79): one moment of a plucked string's first three harmonics, the whole in amber, the halves in blue, the thirds in green, on Ink dark ([docs/design/logo-v2-preview/](docs/design/logo-v2-preview/index.html), eight rounds). Until then 1.1's "twang to tune" on Retro's mustard.

## Phase 81 — The tuner doesn't hear TalkBack's own voice
Goal: with TalkBack on and the phone's speaker loud, nothing that listens takes TalkBack's speech for a played note. The owner's check *TalkBack's own voice* failed on 2026-11-06 (Xiaomi 12X, 2.0.0 from Play): with nothing played the tuner showed notes while TalkBack spoke, and each spoken reading ("12 cents flat") made a new reading that was spoken in turn, a loop. 1.1 likely did the same; the check had never run. Before production: for a player who relies on TalkBack the tuner is unusable as it is.
- ☐ **Readings set aside while TalkBack speaks:** the microphone's frames are dropped while TalkBack's voice plays, plus a short tail for the room's echo, the way the reference tone's own sound is set aside (`ignoredFrames` in `TunerViewModel` and `EarTrainingViewModel`). TalkBack plays with the usage `USAGE_ASSISTANCE_ACCESSIBILITY`, and `AudioManager.registerAudioPlaybackCallback` (API 26, the app's minSdk) reports when such a sound starts and stops, other apps' sounds included; checked first on the Xiaomi and the S26 that it does for TalkBack, before building on it. The same for every listener: the tuner (its tools too: strum check, 12th-fret check, pitch trail), the metronome's Listen, Chords' Listen and Ear training's Play it back; the watch app the same where Wear OS's TalkBack reports it. Only while TalkBack (or another screen reader) is on, so nothing changes for anyone else
- ☐ **A reading spoken once per note** (so a missed sound can't loop): TalkBack reads a reading when a played note settles, and again only when it changes enough to matter (in tune, or Tighten ↔ Loosen) or a new note is played, never on each small change; the watch's "calmly" rule is the model
- ☐ **Tests:** unit tests for setting frames aside (a fake playback source: speech starts, stops, the tail) in every listener; a UI test that with a screen reader on the tuner's spoken reading doesn't change on its own. Then the owner's check *TalkBack's own voice* again, all three parts
