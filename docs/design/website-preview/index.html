A calm, precise tuner. Free, with nothing to sign up for.

Play a string and Twangle shows the note and how far it is from being in tune, to a single cent. Then it helps you play: chord names, chord charts, scales, the capo and a metronome.

Tune any instrument

Guitar (6, 7, 8 or 12 strings), bass, ukulele, banjo, violin, viola, cello and mandolin, in the common tunings or your own. The chromatic tuner does any other instrument, or your voice.

The tools

Tuning

The tunerPlay a string, see how far it is from being in tune. Strum checkStrum all strings and see which ones are off. 12th-fret checkLearn whether a string's saddle should move.

Listening

Chord nameStrum a chord, see its name. Pitch trailSee how steady a note, a bend or your vibrato is.

Practice

MetronomeSteady time from 30 to 250 beats a minute. ChordsChord charts for your instrument and tuning. ScalesEvery note on the neck, with a drone to play over. CapoPick the song's key; see where the capo goes.
