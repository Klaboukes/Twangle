What stays the same

  • ♭ left, ♯ right, by position; the rays stand on the stretched scale, a few cents apart near the middle.
  • The wedge from the middle out to the reading, in the reading's tone (amber far off, green in tune), each light fading as the reading leaves it; the middle ray muted off the note and green in tune, its neighbours half lit; the in-tune glow behind it.
  • The ring of marks takes a little room past the lights, so with it the fan is about 6 % smaller.
  • High contrast would get the same shapes, an unlit light an empty outline in ink, as today.
  • It's one drawing file (LightArc.kt); nothing else on the screen moves.
