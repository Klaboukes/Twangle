What the watch could do next

Seven ideas beyond today's tuner, each drawn live on a Galaxy Watch 5-sized screen. Every one works on any Wear OS watch; the Watch 5 is where we check them, and its touch bezel (a swipe round the edge of the screen) and strong vibration make them shine there. Nothing here is built yet.

NOW

Today's watch tuner

One ring along the edge, the note large in the middle, the cents under it, a soft glow when in tune. The base for everything below.

Built in Phase 20
1soft tap

Feel it in tune

One soft tap on the wrist the moment the string comes in tune, so your eyes can stay on the peg.

Who: everyone, most of all on a dark stage or with the watch turned away.

Keep in mind: one tap per tuned note, never a buzz; it rides on the in-tune glow, so it is as calm as the glow.

Strings around the ring

The tuning's strings sit as beads along the bottom of the ring. The string you play lights up, each turns green once it is in tune, and when all six are, the whole ring glows once.

Who: anyone tuning a whole guitar: the phone's guitar mode, on the wrist. A swipe round the edge picks the tuning.

Keep in mind: the watch then needs a small tuning list (the phone's presets; its own tunings stay on the phone).

Phase 61Medium
3

A tail that shows the drift

The marker leaves a short tail that curls inward as it ages. A note that settles draws a curve that straightens; a steady note, a straight line to the middle; a slipping peg, a wobble.

Who: players chasing the last cent: the phone's pitch trail, folded onto the ring.

Keep in mind: a little more drawing each frame, so it gets a battery check on the watch.

Small
4beat

A silent metronome on the wrist

The beat as a tap on the wrist, the first beat of the bar stronger, with four beat lights round the ring. A swipe round the edge changes the tempo. Nobody else hears it: play along with a band, with headphones, or at night.

Who: anyone practising; a beat you feel doesn't fight the music the way a click does.

Keep in mind: a tool of its own, so it gets a switch; it must stay on time while the watch dims (a drift check like the phone's).

The stand-outLarge
5

A calm always-on look

When the wrist drops, the microphone stops (as now), the ring thins to a hairline and the time comes back in outline. One tap and you are tuning again.

Who: everyone: the watch stays a watch, and nearly every pixel stays dark.

Small
6

One swipe from the watch face

A Twangle tile next to the watch face: Tune, or the metronome at your last tempo. Plus a small Twangle shortcut to put on the watch face itself.

Who: anyone who opens it often; quicker than the app list.

Keep in mind: the tile never listens; the microphone starts only once the tuner is open.

Phase 61Small
7

The six styles on the watch

Ancient, Classic, Retro, Contemporary, Futuristic and Space Age, each with its own ring finish and colours on the watch's black.

Who: players who picked a style on the phone and want the watch to match.

Keep in mind: phone and watch don't talk to each other, so the watch needs its own small style setting.

Decided (2026-10-30)

  1. Phase 61: the tile with a watch-face shortcut (6), Feel it in tune (1) and the strings round the ring (2), with a tuning list on the watch: a watch tuner is only worth it if it opens faster than finding the phone, and a tap on the wrist is what only a watch can do.
  2. Waiting in IDEAS.md: the wrist metronome (4) once players use the watch app; the always-on look, the tail and the styles if players ask.

Left on the shelf

  • Phone and watch in step (style, tuning, your own tunings): needs Google's watch-link library and a line in the privacy policy, though still no internet. Only if players ask.
  • A reference note from the watch's speaker: it is too small for a low E (82 Hz); only higher strings would sound right. Only if ukulele or violin players ask.
  • Strum check and chord name on the watch: the watch's microphone and small screen make both weaker than on the phone, where they already are.
The edge swipe uses the Watch 5's touch bezel (on in the watch's settings); watches with a crown use the crown, and any watch can swipe up or down instead. Colours: the phone's Contemporary style on black, as the watch app today; card 7 the six styles' dark palettes.
