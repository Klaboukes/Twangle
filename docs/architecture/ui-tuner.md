## Tuner screen
`TunerScreen` wires the ViewModel; `TunerContent` is the stateless body used by previews and UI tests. Upright (`SingleColumnLayout`, as the approved mock-up `docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html`) the column fills the screen's height: the picker chip at the top, the meter, the note, and the guitar controls at the bottom; the free height goes 1 : 2 above the meter and above the strings (`TOP_GAP_WEIGHT`), each gap at least 24 dp. On a short screen (a narrow phone with navigation buttons: the Galaxy S26 overflowed, 2026-10-18) the gaps close and the **note** shrinks, never the meter (owner, 2026-10-21): the meter keeps its full width, and `FitColumn` in `:core:designsystem` gives the note block what the rest leaves (`NoteDisplay`'s `maxHeight`). Only the big note letter gets smaller (`FitNoteColumn`, down to 32 dp), to the size whose line fills the room under the wave or the pill, worked out from the room and the face's line, never from the note's name, so it doesn't change from one note to the next. The octave beside it grows with the text size but never past half the letter's size (set in dp, since large text grows non-linearly in sp): Polish at 2× text on the Xiaomi shrank the letter to 32 dp and left a 37 dp octave beside it (overnight run, 2026-10-24). Space Age's Michroma has the tallest line (1.42 em: 159 dp at the full 112 dp; a single line keeps its face's own height on the phone whatever `lineHeight` or `LineHeightStyle` asks, measured 2026-10-21), so there the note shrinks most. Before, the meter shrank instead and on the S26 it was at its 160 dp minimum in Space Age. With a tool on, its panel in the meter's place still shrinks (keeping its shape, down to 160 dp, `FitGauge`), so the tool's text under it keeps its size. Only with a very large font does it scroll. The meter spans the screen less 8 dp each side (`METER_MARGIN`); everything else keeps a 16 dp text margin (`COLUMN_PADDING`).
- **Upright tablet** (Phase 52): the column is at most 480 dp wide, so on a Lenovo Tab M11 upright (800 dp) the meter was 464 dp across and about 290 dp of screen lay empty under the note. There the whole column is now laid out as on a phone and drawn bigger (`columnScaleFor` and `ScaledUp` in `:core:designsystem`'s `ScaledUp.kt`, shared with the metronome's upright column, [Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md); `columnScaleFor` is JVM-tested in `ColumnScaleTest`; `ScaledUp` gives the column a `LocalDensity` with that many more pixels to the dp): it is laid out in the window divided by the scale, as on a phone of at least 560 × 760 dp, so the meter, the note, the string buttons, every text and gap grow together and keep the phone's proportions, and no rule above needs a tablet case. The scale is the smaller of width / 560 and height / 760, at least 1 and at most 1.5 (720 dp across on a large tablet): 1.43 on the Tab M11 (a 686 dp column, a 663 dp meter), 1 on every phone (at most about 480 dp wide, and on its side far too short), growing smoothly with the window so a resized window doesn't jump; the provider stays at 1, so crossing the point keeps the column's state. Text keeps Android's own non-linear font scaling (sp go to dp through the screen's density, then to pixels at the scaled one). Popups opened from the column (the more tools menu, tooltips) inherit it and grow too; the instrument and tuning panel, the two panes on its side and focus mode keep their own sizes. Tests measuring nodes inside the column read their bounds in the window's dp (`windowBounds` in `TunerContentTest`): `getBoundsInRoot()` uses the node's own, scaled, dp. `TunerContentTest.anUprightTabletShowsThePhonesColumnBigger` checks that every part on an 800 × 1240 dp tablet at 1.3× text sits where it sits on the phone-sized window, that much bigger, with no text cut off, and that a phone keeps its own size.

So a pluck's sharp attack, a wobble or the gap between plucks keeps it, nothing flickers, and a peg turned away shows within a second or so; back in tune, it is ticked again. Merely not held in tune (a new pluck settling, a reading inside the exit margin) keeps it. The pill fills, a soft glow lights behind the note and the note pops (grows 8 % and springs back). Each runs once per change, read in the draw phase.
- **Pill turns green gradually** (2026-10-15, owner: the jump from grey to green was abrupt): before the note is in tune the pill already takes some of the "in tune" colour as it comes close (`pillTint`, JVM-tested): none from 25 cents off, rising with a smoothstep to 40 % at the target, the same flat or sharp, so it says "getting close" without repeating the meter's direction. Only in tune fills it fully (with the check mark), so a full pill still means in tune. The fill glides on a very slow spring (`StiffnessVeryLow`), so it follows the note, not every jitter of the cents; past 40 % the text switches (150 ms fade) to whichever of `onSurface` and `onInTune` has more contrast on the fill at that moment, so it stays readable all the way (a blend of the two was grey on mid-green in the phone check); below 40 % it never changes colour.
- **Bloom and sheen** (2026-10-15, owner, as the mock-up shown in chat): the fill is drawn as a bloom (`drawPillBloom` in `PillTint.kt`), a soft oval in the pill's proportions that grows from the middle and deepens with the fill (`bloomReach`, `bloomCentre`, JVM-tested), the same both sides; past 70 % a flat layer fades in, so in tune the pill is even to its ends. As the note gets in tune one soft white band (32 %) crosses the pill once, 250 ms after, over 900 ms (`drawPillSheen`), then all is still. A real string drifts a few cents and drops in and out of tune while it rings, so the sheen follows `InTuneTickRule` (like the haptic tick) with a 6 s quiet time: the same note sheens again only after 6 s out of tune; another note at once. The sheen runs in its own coroutine, so the note leaving tune never freezes it half way; none in the wave view or with "Remove animations". Phone check (Xiaomi, Futuristic): a tone sliding into tune, wobbling ±3 cents, drifting 11 cents flat for a second and back sheened once.

## Strum check view
Strum once and every string of the tuning shows flat, in tune or sharp at once (the detector: [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md), *Strum check (polyphonic)*).
- **Entry point:** a switch above the strings, at the end of a row of its own, its outline ending where the last string button's face does (`StrumCheckToggle`: strings with a sweep across them, in the style's button outline and shape, filled with `primaryContainer` while on), so it lives with guitar mode's controls and leaves the top corners empty. The string row spreads its buttons evenly, so its edges are not the buttons': `GuitarControls` takes the faces' inset from the same fit rules the row uses (`stringFaceInset`, JVM-tested; the row is symmetric, so one inset serves both ends) and lines the switch's outline and the tuning's name up with them (at the row's edge the switch sat up to 22 dp past a bass's last string; reading the buttons' positions as alignment lines during layout left the row at stale positions when the tuning changed). Not saved: the app always opens on the tuner. Switching to chromatic turns it off; switching it on from chromatic goes to guitar mode. In the compact layout on its side it is reached like the other controls (tap the screen).
- **Audio** (hard rules 2–4 unchanged): one `AudioRecorder` collection serves both. `PitchPipeline.listen(range, strumStrings)` asks for every chunk whether strum check runs (`TunerOptions.strumChecking`, and no reference tone or its 0.3 s tail); while it does, chunks go to a `StrumListener` + `StrumDetector` (`StrumAnalysis`, made the first time and again only for another range; the frame follows the tuning's lowest string like the tuner's window) instead of YIN. It emits a `StrumReading` when strum check starts or starts over (other strings or A4), when a strum is heard or stops ringing, and with each reading (every 0.1 s). Back on the tuner, its window and smoothing start afresh. The switch never reopens the mic.
- **State:** `StrumCheckInterpreter` (pure, JVM-tested) makes `TunerUiState.StrumCheck`: verdicts from `StrumVerdict` at the tuner's own tolerance (5 cents Relaxed, 2 Precise; +2.5 for shadowed strings), the previous reading of the same strum holding "in tune" 1 cent longer.
