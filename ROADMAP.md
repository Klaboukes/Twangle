## Owner checks (live tests)
Done by the owner by hand, with a real guitar or headset. They are not roadmap increments: `/roadmap-step` skips them.

- ☐ **When you have the gear** (postponed 2026-10-16, no hurry):
  - Phase 5, a Bluetooth headset: with one connected, the tuner still listens through the phone's mic and reads a string correctly
  - Wired earbuds with a mic (3.5 mm plug, or USB-C), for a noisy room (2026-10-31): with them plugged in, Android listens through the earbud mic, which Twangle allows (only a Bluetooth headset is overruled), so the mic can lie close to the guitar. Lay the mic on the guitar's top a few centimetres from the soundhole (not in it), hands off the cable, and tune all six strings, first in a quiet room, then with loud music from the laptop: the note and cents follow each string, the low E included, at least as quickly and steadily as with the phone's own mic, and better in the noise; the reference tone plays in the earbuds; pulling the plug goes back to the phone's mic without a restart. If it passes, one line on the website's help page in English and Dutch ("Noisy room? Plug in wired earbuds and lay the mic on the guitar"), no hint in the app; if the low strings fail, the line names guitar and ukulele only
  - Phase 15, a bass (or a 7-string guitar or cello): the lowest strings read correctly (B0, E1, B1, C2), and the tuner shows no note while the amp only hums

## Phase 89 — App fixes from the code review
Goal: the bugs a player could hit, found by the full code review of 2026-11-10 (seven reviewers, one per part of the app and its tooling; the most serious checked by hand), fixed before more people get the app. Each fix comes with a test that fails without it, and the ones a player can see or hear get the usual check on the phone. They are fixes inside existing tools, so no new switch (CLAUDE.md, *Every tool has a switch*). In order of how much they matter:
- ☑ **Chords' Listen no longer closes the app when the microphone is busy:** `ChordsListen.kt`'s `LaunchedEffect` collects `ChordListener.chords()` with nothing to catch the recorder's `AudioRecorderException` (busy, failed to start, permission gone), so a phone call, or on Android 8–9 tapping Listen within 5 s of leaving the tuner, closes the app. Caught there as the metronome's Listen and Ear training already do: Listen turns off and says the microphone is busy, as the other listeners' busy state does. A test with a fake recorder that throws
- ☑ **Ear training stays quiet once left:** its ViewModel lives as long as the app, and a tuning change (in the tuner, or Chords' picker chip) after leaving Ear training on *Chord type* starts a new question and strums a chord on whatever screen is open, cutting off a reference tone; the next visit then doesn't start fresh. A "visit on" flag, set when the tool is shown and cleared by `endVisit()`, so a tuning change only asks again while the tool is on screen. Tests: a tuning change after leaving makes no sound, and the next visit starts fresh. **Done (2026-11-10):** `visiting` in `EarTrainingViewModel`; two JVM tests (Chord type's fails without the fix: no strum, the reference tone not cut off, the next visit afresh on the new tuning; Play it back and Intervals stay quiet too)
- ☑ **The tuner follows a mode set elsewhere:** the guitar / any-note pick made on the tuner's switch (`pickedMode` in `TunerViewModel`) wins over the saved mode for as long as the app is open, so picking an instrument or tuning in Practice, which switches the tuner back to guitar mode, shows only after a restart. The pick gives way once the saved mode has caught up with it, or when the saved mode changes from elsewhere. A test for a mode change saved from outside the tuner. **Done (2026-11-10):** the pick gives way at the first reading of the settings once none of the switch's saves is under way (`modeSavesUnderWay`); two JVM tests (a mode saved from outside after a pick shows at once; a pick holds while its slow save is under way), both failing without the fix; on the Xiaomi, Any note on the tuner, then Drop D picked in Chords: the tuner shows guitar mode with Drop D's strings at once
- ☑ **Play it back hears the answer after ▶ is tapped mid-question:** tapping ▶ while the question still sounds leaves Ear training waiting for a pause that is never reported (the "aside" reading is sent only when it changes), so it ignores every note played until ▶ is tapped again after the sound has ended. Each sounding of the question gets its own number, carried through what is heard, or a fresh "aside" is sent each time a question starts. A test tapping ▶ during the question, then playing the right notes. **Done (2026-11-10):** each sounding gets its own number (`sounding` in `EarTrainingViewModel`, carried by `Hearing.asideFor`), so the microphone sends a fresh aside for a ▶ tapped mid-question; two JVM tests (▶ once mid-question then the right note is found; ▶ twice then a wrong note says "Not that one"), both failing without the fix
- ☑ **The tuner and the pitch trail try the microphone again when they come back:** leaving with Home and coming back within the tuner's 5 s grace can end in "Microphone is busy" (Android silences a recording in the background), which stays until Try again; the watch already retries a failure when its screen comes back (`WatchTunerViewModel.shown()`, seen on the Galaxy Watch 5), the phone doesn't. The same on the phone's `TunerViewModel` and `PitchTrailViewModel`; and on Android 8–9, a tool that listens (the trail, Ear training, Chords' and the metronome's Listen) opened within 5 s of the tuner retries a busy start for a few seconds before it gives up. Tests with a fake recorder that is busy, then free; a phone check on the Xiaomi: Home and back within 5 s, the tuner listens again by itself. **Done (2026-11-10):** after a failure, `listenAgainOnReturn` (the tuner's and the trail's ViewModels, fed by `ReportShown`) listens again once the screen has gone and come back, as the watch does; before Android 10, `AndroidAudioRecorder` tries a busy start again every 250 ms for about 6 s (`retryingBusyStart`), which covers every tool that listens at once. JVM tests: the tuner and the trail busy while away, then free, listen again on return (and not while still away, nor while shown); a busy start free after 4 s records, one that stays busy gives up after 6 s, a busy after the first chunks and other failures pass on at once. On the Xiaomi (Android 14), Home and back within 2 to 4 s: the tuner listens on with no error; this phone silences a recording only about 6 s after Home, at the grace's end, so the busy itself shows there only in a moment's window (the watch showed it, and Android 8–9 had no device here: both checked with the JVM tests)
- ☑ **The tuning editor opens without an old error:** after a save that failed, the "couldn't be saved" line still shows the next time the editor opens, even for another tuning (`TuningEditorSaver` is shared by every opening and only resets after a save that worked). Reset each time the editor opens, with a test. **Done (2026-11-10):** `TuningEditorSaver.opened()`, called once per opening (a `rememberSaveable`, so turning the phone keeps the line), drops an earlier failed save; a JVM test, and a UI test (a failed save, the editor left and opened again: no line) that fails without the fix
- ☑ **Changes of several settings saved as one:** picking a tuning in Practice (guitar mode, instrument, tuning, lowered by), the drone's sound with its voicing, and the capo row's chord are each saved as two to four separate writes in `MainActivity`, in a scope that ends when the activity is rebuilt, so a turn of the phone in between can save half of it, and every listener (the widget too) sees the half-done state. One repository method per such change (one DataStore edit), run in a scope that lives as long as the app. Tests in `:core:settings` that the change lands whole. **Done (2026-11-10):** `setInstrument`, `setTuning` and `setLoweredBy` take `guitarMode`, `setDrone` replaces the drone's three setters, `openInChords` saves the capo row's chord with Chords as the tool used last, each one DataStore edit; `MainActivity`'s writes run in `AppContainer.save`, a scope as long as the app (on the main thread, so in order); JVM tests count one edit for each and read it back after a restart
- ☐ **Small fixes found by the same review**, one pull request:
  - focus mode doesn't hide its controls while TalkBack (touch exploration) is on, since a TalkBack user's swipes aren't touches and the controls vanished mid-way (`TunerScreen.kt`)
  - an unexpected error in the tuner or the trail is written to the log (and, if it is a code bug rather than a microphone failure, no longer shown as "Recording stopped" and hidden from Play's crash reports)
  - the metronome's Listen makes a new beat detector each time it starts, and drops a stale beat grid, so Stop and Listen tapped quickly can't share one detector between two threads
  - "Listening… 96 BPM" as one text with placeholders, so each language can order it (`ListenParts.kt`)
  - the watch's metronome player shares one beat thread instead of leaving one behind each time the watch app is reopened
  - in `:core:dsp`: `ChordSettler.reset()` forgets when the last chord was shown, `InTuneDetector` treats a missing reading (NaN) as not in tune, `InTuneTickRule` has no overflow on its first in-tune moment; each with a test (none can show today, but each would with the next caller)
  - Ear training's listener made once, not on every redraw (`AppContainer.earTrainingListener`, `by lazy` as Chords' is)
  - the home-screen widget redrawn only when the language or dark mode changes, not on every turn of the phone; its tuning chip grows with the text at large font sizes instead of a fixed 30 dp, with a test at font scale 2
  - two stale comments: `Tuning.centsFromString` names a function that no longer exists, `TuningWidgetTest` says wallpaper colours are on by default
- ☐ **The tuner redraws less:** while a note sounds, the tuner's header, chip and pegs are rebuilt on every whole cent, since the tuning and notation come from `:core:dsp` (not compiled for Compose, so counted as changeable) and new wrappers are made each time. A Compose stability file naming `core.dsp.music`'s types as stable (or the wrappers remembered), and the string rings' `Stroke`s made once instead of on every draw (`StringSelector.kt`). `scripts/meter-frames.sh` before and after: no frame times worse
- ☐ **A string change an octave or a twelfth away followed sooner:** in standard tuning, low E → high E (two octaves), low E → B and A → high E (a twelfth) are the jumps `PitchSmoother` holds for about a second as a likely detector slip, unless the new pluck is 10 dB louder than the quietest recent frame. A test with a high E plucked over a still-ringing low E only a little louder; if the needle lags, a jump that lands on another string of the tuning in guitar mode is followed after the short confirm instead of the long hold, the room-noise tests (a TV on) still passing. Checked on the phone with real playing (`scripts/record-plucks.sh`'s fixtures, then a guitar)

## Phase 90 — Tooling fixes from the code review
Goal: the scripts and skills say truthfully what passed and never get in each other's way, from the same review of 2026-11-10. Nothing here changes the app itself.
- ☐ **The full check's result can be trusted:** `scripts/full-check.sh` always ends with exit code 0 (its last line prints the summary), so only the summary text tells a failure; a watch that wasn't connected or dropped off still gives "everything passed" on the first line, the line the release skill reads, so a watch app whose tests never ran could be released; and a lane stopped because the phone was busy (exit 5) says "failures below" and lists nothing. It ends with its real exit code, the first line says "watch not tested" when so, the busy message is in the summary, and `scripts/release.sh bundle` refuses unless the last full check passed whole on the commit it releases (today only the release skill checks that)
- ☐ **The phone lock can't be taken from a live run:** in `scripts/ui-tests.sh`, when one `adb shell stat` fails (most likely with the watch over Wi-Fi) the age comes back empty and the script takes the lock over, stopping the other session's tests and resetting rotation; two takeovers at once can also race. An age that isn't a number counts as busy (exit 5), and the takeover is one step that only one run can win
- ☐ **Every script that uses the phone takes the lock:** `release-smoke.sh` and `meter-frames.sh` (both uninstall the app), `strum-check.sh`, and the skills' `installDebug` steps (roadmap-step, add-language) run outside it and can break another session's UI test run; `meter-frames.sh` and `strum-check.sh` also pick no device, so with two connected the Gradle task runs on both. One lock helper in `scripts/devices.sh`, used by each, and each picks its device the same way
- ☐ **The publish scripts publish only what is on `main`:** `publish-website.sh` builds the folder it runs in and `publish-privacy.sh` copies its `PRIVACY*.md`, without checking the branch, so run from a worktree they put unmerged text on the public site. Both build from `origin/main` (or refuse when the folder differs from it)
- ☐ **The full check keeps its pass notes when docs change mid-run:** each lane's `ui-tests.sh` checks for a clean tree afresh, so one doc edit during the run (which the full-check skill allows) stops every later module from being noted as passed, and the release's `--since-pass` runs them again. The full check hands its starting commit and clean state to every lane
- ☐ **Build and CI checks see every change:** the unit test that guards the saved settings' keep rules reads `app/proguard-rules.pro`, which isn't among the tests' inputs, so a broken keep rule can pass on a cached result (one line in `app/build.gradle.kts`); the nightly CI skips a change made only in `scripts/`, but `LocalizationTest` reads `scripts/ui-tests.sh`, so that one file keeps the build; the free F-Droid build is also built in release mode, so its shrinking is checked; and the five Gradle calls become one, to save the free plan's minutes
- ☐ **Small script fixes**, one pull request: `share-store-screenshots.sh` unpacks into a temporary folder before replacing the local set (a failed download no longer leaves nothing, ~50 min to make again); `meter-frames.sh` stops its keep-awake loop however it ends; `release.sh bump` says "no release tag" instead of stopping silently; `website/build.py` refuses to empty a folder that isn't its own build folder; the three `python` calls without `-I` (`ui-tests.sh`, `style-screens.sh`, `strum-check.sh`) get it

## Phase 91 — Getting found, after launch
Goal: the rest of the growth plan, once Twangle is public on Play. The plan, its message and its rules (no paid ads, no tracking, no bought reviews, no other app named) are in Phase 50's goal. In order:
- ☐ **Store listing experiments:** after launch, **the owner** starts Play Console experiments one at a time, about two weeks each, Claude preparing the variants in every language the experiment covers: first the short description (the chosen one against the TalkBack one, already in *Being found*), then the first screenshot, then the icon. The winner goes into `fastlane/metadata/` and the losing variants into the store-listing doc with their figures
- ☐ **Search, language by language:** after the first month, **the owner** shares Play Console's search-terms report per country; Claude tunes each language's short description and title subtitle to the words people really use there (in normal sentences, Google's rules in the store-listing doc), starting with the languages where the big English tuners are weakest. `StoreListingTest` still passes; sent with `scripts/release.sh listing` after the owner's yes
