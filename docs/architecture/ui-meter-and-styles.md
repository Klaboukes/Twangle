## Meter
The meter above the note is the first thing anyone sees, so players pick one of six looks (setting `meterLook`), in the owner's order: *Classic* (default), *Light arc*, *Pointers*, *Loupe*, *Arrows*, *Light bars*. Every look shows the note its own way (the approved mock-ups [docs/design/meter-looks-round2-preview.html](../design/meter-looks-round2-preview.html) and, for Phase 60's sharper looks, [docs/design/meter-precision-preview.html](../design/meter-precision-preview.html)); only Classic has a numbered scale. A saved look that no longer exists (*Rolling scale*, *Comet*, *Studio meter*) reads as Classic; a saved *Spirit level* opens as Pointers and a saved *Eclipse* as Loupe, which took their places (`OLD_METER_LOOKS` in `DataStoreSettingsRepository`).
- **One frame** (`:feature:tuner` `components/MeterFrame.kt`): every look sits in `MeterFrame`, so changing looks moves nothing else. It owns the 2:1 box (`METER_ASPECT_RATIO`; each look draws its ♭ and ♯ inside it, at its scale's ends), the ±50-cent range (beyond it the marker pins at the edge, `meterCents`; without a reading it rests in the centre at 0.3 opacity), the look's spring (`meterFeel(look)`), the in-tune amount `tuned` (smoothstep, 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out), the in-tune `glow`, the in-tune window, the reception fade and blur, and `clearAndSetSemantics {}` (`NoteDisplay` speaks the reading). It hands the look one `MeterReading`: cents, `velocity` (from the spring, without boxing), `presence`, `tuned`, `glow`, `inTuneWindow` and `applyMarkerLook(layer)`, read only in layer and draw phases (or in a `drawWithCache` block, which is made again when what it read changes). The window is the tuner's own, 5 cents each way or 2 with the Precise setting (`meterInTuneWindow(precision)`, the `InTuneDetector` windows): `TunerViewModel.inTunePrecision` → `TunerContent(inTunePrecision)` → `MeterFrame(precision)`; the settings cards get the chosen precision too (`MeterLookPicker(precision)`), as they get the in-tune effect.
- **Art in the design system** (`:core:designsystem` `meter/`): `MeterLookArt` draws each look, so the tuner and the settings cards draw exactly the same thing and `:feature:settings` needs no `:feature:tuner`. A part that only turns or slides gets its own `graphicsLayer` and only changes its transform; a part that changes shape is redrawn in its `Canvas` from paths, brushes and strokes made once. The art is not clipped: the calm field and the glow fade out past the box (up to ~110u). The settings cards clip their preview to its box so the names stay clean.
- **The stretched middle** (`meter/MeterStretch.kt`, Phase 60): `meterStretch(cents)` = sign · 50 · (1 + k)|x| / (1 + k|x|), x = cents / 50, k = 1.5 (`METER_STRETCH`): 2.5× the movement per cent at the centre, 0.4× at ±50, 50 stays 50, so 5 cents sits where 11 did and 10 where 19 did. A look multiplies it by its units per cent at the end of its range. `stretchedFraction(x, k)` is the same shape for another strength (the loupe's glass, k = 2, `loupeMagnified`). Never on Classic, whose numbers would go uneven.
- **Shared pieces** (`meter/MeterArt.kt`, named like the mock-up's helpers): `MeterRoles` (the colour roles and number face), `FieldArt` (`meterField`, the same calm field under every look, so switching looks changes nothing round the box), `ContactShadowArt`, `GlassArt`, `ScaleMarks` (with `numberFit`, which keeps neighbouring numbers 8u apart by setting them up to 30 % smaller), the style's finish (chosen in `meter/MeterFinish.kt`, drawn by `NeedleArt`, `HubArt` and `TrackArt`), `SignsArt` (♭ and ♯), `markerShadowPaint`, `GlowArt`, `StaticArt`, plus `mix` and `blurRadius`. Everything is built in `drawWithCache`, so only a size or colour change rebuilds it; drawing allocates nothing. Shadows need hardware-accelerated `BlurMaskFilter` (Android 9+); on Android 8 the meter has no shadows. `meter/MeterLooks.kt` holds Classic and its dial (`DialGeometry`, `DialUnder`, `DialCentre`).
- **The looks:**
  - *Classic:* the dial with the style's needle; needle and shadow drawn once and only rotated by their layers, the style's hub over the pivot in a layer that doesn't turn (it redraws only while it fades to "in tune"). Slightly underdamped, so it swings ~9 % past a new string and settles within a second, like a mechanical needle. The unit "CENTS" (`meter_cents_label`, translated) sits 42u above the pivot under the needle, 7.5u in the number face with 0.3 em letter spacing, muted at 90 % (`CentsLabelArt`).
  - *Light arc* (`LightArc.kt`, sharpened in Phase 60): a fan of little lights like an LED pedal's, no dial and no numbers. 17 rays, evenly spaced (±1.2 rad at the ends), one every 6.25 cents of the stretched scale (`lightArcRayPlace`), so the reading's place among them is `meterStretch(cents)` and the rays out from the middle are reached at about 2.7, 5.9, 9.7, 14.3, 20, 27.3, 36.8 and 50 cents. They rise from a pivot 6u under the box, each of 5 lights 78u to 148u out (3.4u to 5.6u across), unlit in the track colour (75 % dark, 70 % light) like dark LEDs. The rays from the centre out to the reading light as a wedge (`lightArcGoal`): the reading's ray all five lights at full, rays nearer the centre fewer (down to 1.6) and dimmer (to 40 %); the ray just past the reading lights part of the way, so the wedge grows smoothly. A lit light is the accent (a little whiter in dark), 8 % larger, with a halo 3.4× its radius. The middle ray is the centre mark in its own layer: its two inner lights at 45 % and the rest at 14 % off the note, all five in the centre colour as it's reached, and the rays beside it light half way. Tiny ink dots 12u past the outer lights mark the rays nearest 5 cents (1.8u, ink 55 %) and 10 cents (1.3u, 32 %) each side (`lightArcMarks`, on the rays as on the mock-up, so each says which ray it belongs to: the second and the third out); the in-tune window's edge gets a strong dot too, so with Precise the first ray (2.7 cents, the nearest to 2) is marked as well (the mock-up shows Relaxed). They are static, drawn over the lit layer so a halo never dims them, and made again only when the window changes. `LightArcHeat` (one preallocated array of 85) gives a 0.12 s fading trail (`TRAIL_SECONDS`); its frame loop (`withFrameNanos`) runs only while a light is still changing, then waits on `snapshotFlow`, so a steady reading moves nothing and UI tests settle. ♭ and ♯ in the box's lower corners.
  - *Pointers* (`Pointers.kt`, Phase 60, in Spirit level's place): a straight track (1.5u in the track colour, 52 % down the box) on the stretched scale, ±150u at ±50 cents (`pointerOffset`: ~7.5u per cent near the note, 1.2u at the ends; pinned at ±53). A fixed pointer over the middle (a triangle 18u wide and 24u tall, its tip 7u above the track, pointing down) in the centre colour, muted at rest; the note's pointer under it pointing up (the same size, its tip 7u under the track, in ink with the marker shadow), drawn once and only moved by its layer. In tune the two tips meet. A band of light along the track from the middle to the note's pointer (8u high, round ends, the accent from 8 % by the middle to 80 % dark / 65 % light at the pointer, however long it is: `bandAlpha`), redrawn each frame from a gradient made once along one unit, the canvas stretched to the pointer (mirrored when flat) and the ends' radius narrowed by as much, so the ends stay round and nothing is allocated; the track is drawn over it. Ticks without numbers above the track (from 8u over it, `pointerTick`): every cent from 1 to 4 (6u, ink 40 %), 5 and 10 (12u × 2u, ink 80 %), 20 to 50 (9u, ink 50 %), in the style's caps and by hand in Ancient; the in-tune window's edge is a tall one, so with Precise the 2-cent tick is too (the mock-up shows Relaxed). The in-tune glow (120u) round the middle of the track; ♭ and ♯ 4u in from the track's ends, 26u under it. Its spring hardly swings past the note (under a cent from 38 flat).
  - *Loupe* (`Loupe.kt`, Phase 60, in Eclipse's place): a straight scale half way down the box (1.5u in the track colour, ±150u at ±50 cents, 3u per cent, not stretched: `loupeOffset`, pinned at ±53) with a tick every 5 cents as on the open face but without numbers (13u × 2.1u in ink every 10, 7u × 1.3u at 50 % between, from 4u over the line, the style's caps, by hand in Ancient), and a slim marker on it (3u wide, round ends, 22u over and under the line, in ink with the marker shadow), drawn once and only moved by its layer. Over the scale's middle a round magnifying glass (54u radius) lies on the screen: its contact shadow (`ContactShadowArt`) and a face of the background 70 % of the way to the surface colour (`loupeFace`, a little lighter than the screen, as a lens lying on it, so without the recessed glass's inner shadow), and over everything the art language's sheen and 1u rim in ink at 14 % (`GlassArt.drawOver`). Through the glass the scale shows magnified (`loupeMagnified`: x' = R · h(x / R), h the stretch's shape with k = 2, `stretchedFraction`): 3× at its centre, easing to the same point at the rim (where the slope is a third), so the view meets the plain scale round it without a step and nothing hides under it; from 18 cents flat to 18 sharp the marker is seen through it, about 9u per cent near the note. Under the glass, clipped by one layer's round outline (`LoupeGlassShape`, so nothing is clipped per frame): the line 1.4× as wide; a tick for every cent where the glass sets them at least 4u apart (1 to 4 cents, ink 45 %), 5 and 15 (13u, ink 75 %) and 10 (17u, ink) (`loupeGlassTicks`), each grown by the glass's magnification there between 0.6× and 1.8× (`loupeGrowth`; widths at most 1.4×), from 5u (grown) over the line; the in-tune window as a quiet band (`LoupeWindowArt`: the track colour across the glass, 38 % dark / 45 % light by the line, fading to nothing toward the glass's top and bottom, its edges 1u hairlines in ink at 18 % on the ticks for 5 cents, or 2 with Precise: `loupeWindowHalfWidth`), which takes on 40 % of "in tune" as the note is reached (a second band made once, laid over at the `tuned` share; never green at rest); the centre mark (3.5u, from 8u to 36u over the line, in the centre colour, muted at rest), redrawn only while it fades; the in-tune glow seen through the glass (1.1× its radius at 80 % of the usual strength, its own layer, alpha from `glow`), whose face hides the 120u glow round it; and the marker seen through the glass (34u over and under the line), drawn once in the middle and moved by its layer to where the glass shows it and widened as it magnifies there (`scaleX` up to 1.5×), gone once the marker leaves the glass. In tune the thin marker stands on the centre mark. ♭ and ♯ 4u in from the scale's ends, 26u under it.
  - *Arrows* (`Arrows.kt`, after a pedal tuner's converging wedges, sharpened in Phase 60): four chevrons each side (tips 26u + 30u·k from the middle, 7 % smaller each, 7.5u round strokes) point at a 9u × 100u centre bar. On the side the note is off they light one after another, the inner one first, each over a step of its own (`arrowLight`, Phase 60): from 2 to 5 cents, 5 to 10, 10 to 20 and 20 to 40 (`arrowLightsFrom`, `arrowFullAt`), so one lit arrow means "almost there" (inside the 5-cent window), two "close", and all four 40 cents or more off; in the accent with a halo of themselves 2.2× as wide; the other side and unlit chevrons are the track colour. In tune the inner pair lights at 90 % in the centre colour and the bar (outlined in the centre colour, filled 18 %) fills in. No shimmer (the mock-up had one): a steady reading must draw nothing new, and UI tests must settle.
  - *Light bars* (`LightBars.kt`, after a pedal tuner's display, sharpened in Phase 60): 41 bars (±150u, 3.6u round) mirrored about the middle line, evenly spaced on the stretched scale (`lightBarPlace`, one every 2.5 of `meterStretch`), so out from the middle they stand for about 1.0, 2.1, 3.3, 4.5, 5.9 and 7.4 cents: one bar per cent near the note, wider apart toward the ends. The heights are the mock-up's at 91 % (`ROW_HEIGHT`): its dots sat 4u outside the 170u box, which the Settings cards clip. Dark, a bar reaches 5u each way in the track colour (95 % in light); the bars of the in-tune window (`lightBarInWindow`, placed on the stretched scale as the mock-up does: 4 each side with Relaxed's 5 cents, 1 with Precise's 2) reach 8u and are tinted 14 % toward ink, blending 45 % toward "in tune" as the note is reached. The centre bar stands out: 5.6u wide, 85 % of the way from the track colour to the centre colour (muted at rest), always taller than the peak ((16 + 64 · peak)u each way against the others' (5 or 8 + 62 · peak)u), with a 3.2u dot in the centre colour 89u above and below the middle. A narrow Gaussian peak round the reading on the stretched scale (σ 2.6, widening to 3.6 as the note is reached; `lightBarPeak`: about 0.6 one bar away, 0.16 two away, dark three away) grows the bars, each first redrawn to its full reach in its dark colour, then lit over it at 1.25× its share (the centre bar 1.6×, `lightBarLit`), fading to 30 % toward its tips over its own length, as the mock-up's gradient per bar: one brush per colour is made once along −1..1 and each bar scales the canvas to its reach (its round ends' corner height narrowed by as much), so nothing is allocated. Its light is the accent (muted without a note); reaching the note only the centre bar turns fully "in tune", the lit bars beside it at most 35 % (`lightBarInTune`), the colours laid one over the other at their shares (exact at the middle line). A soft accent halo (56u, 60 % of the old strength, none at rest) sits under the peak and gives way to the in-tune glow (80u, round the middle). Layers: the field, the plain bars and the ♭ and ♯ (static, made again only when the window changes); the in-tune glow; the middle (the window's bars, the centre bar and its dots), redrawn only as a note comes and goes and as it is reached; and the peak with the halo, redrawn from the reading. The peak is pinned at ±51.5 on the stretched scale, so the end bar stays fully lit past the range.
- **Feel per look** (`meter/MeterFeel.kt`, frequency / damping ratio, stiffness = (2π f)²): Classic 1.6 Hz / 0.6, Light arc 2.2 / 0.9, Pointers 1.9 / 0.8, Loupe 1.8 / 0.8, Arrows 2.0 / 0.9, Light bars 1.8 / 0.8.
- **The in-tune moment** (owner: smooth, not gimmicky): a soft glow eases in with the haptic tick, holds while the note rings and fades when it leaves.
  - *When:* `InTuneGlowRule` (pure) lights it on the update that raises `Detected.inTune`, never restarts it while the note holds, and bridges a dropout of the same note for 600 ms (shorter than the tick's 1.5 s, so a bridged dropout never ticks twice). Another note or drifting out of tune puts it out at once, and so does the same note coming back while the hint asks to tune it (a re-pluck often starts sharp), so the glow never lights with "Tune down" on screen. `rememberInTuneGlow` runs it in `TunerScreen` (saved over rotation) → `TunerContent(meterGlow)` → `MeterFrame(glow)`, eased 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out.
  - *Remove animations:* `rememberAnimationsOff()` (`:core:designsystem`, `AnimationsOff.kt`, shared with Settings' look cards) reads `ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE` and follows changes; at 0 `tuned` and `glow` snap.
  - *Drawing:* the shared `TuneGlow`, a `GlowArt` in the "in tune" colour drawn once in its own layer under the marker; `glow` only sets the layer's alpha (`CompositingStrategy.ModulateAlpha`), so easing it redraws and allocates nothing. Never clipped: it fades out past the box, behind the controls around the meter.
  - *Calm or Bold* (`InTuneEffect`): Bold is the same glow and timing at 1.7 × the alpha, capped at 52 % (`BOLD_GLOW_MAX_ALPHA`) so the scale stays readable, and 1.35 × the radius (`BOLD_GLOW_SIZE`). It travels into `meterRoles(effect)`, so changing it rebuilds each look's cached art once and is never read per frame.
- **Picker** (setting "Meter", above the display choice): `MeterLookPicker` draws each look's `MeterLookArt` in a 3 × 2 grid of cards (two to a row from 1.3× font, except on a large window: *Landscape and wide screens* in [UI basics](ui.md)). It shares `LookCardGrid` with `WaveLookPicker`: the ring on the picked card, names on one line shrinking to 6 sp (French "Barres lumineuses" in Classic at 2× text), one selectable radio group for TalkBack, test tags, and `LookDemo`, an infinite animation (UI tests skip it). With "Remove animations" the cards hold still on the loop's first second (`holdStill`: the meter 18 cents flat, the wave drawn, the style strips in place), or where they were when it is switched on, and move again when it is off. The cards play one loop (`MeterDemo`): 18 cents flat, tuned up slowing like a peg, in tune, drifting 14 sharp, tuned down. Each card previews the chosen in-tune effect.
- **Tests:** JVM tests for each look's maths (`MeterReadingTest` with the in-tune window, `MeterFeelTest`, `MeterStretchTest`, `LightArcHeatTest`, `PointersTest`, `LoupeTest`, `ArrowsTest`, `LightBarsTest`, `TuneGlowTest`, `InTuneGlowRuleTest`, `MeterDemoTest`, `NumberFitTest`, `MeterFinishTest`). UI: `MeterLooksTest` (every look × style × light/dark: clearly drawn in its box, fading out around it with no edge at the box and nothing left at 0.3 box widths, skipped by TalkBack, moves without recomposing, glows in Calm and more in Bold with Bold's glow reaching past the box, snaps with animations off), `CompactLandscapeStylesTest`, `TunerContentTest`, `SettingsContentTest`.
- **Pictures for the owner:** `scripts/meter-look-screens.sh` (~2 min) runs `MeterLookScreenshots` with `-a meterScreenshots=true` (without it the class has no cases): every look in every style, light above dark, 20 cents flat and in tune with Bold, as PNGs plus an `index.html` contact sheet in `build/owner-screens/meter-looks/`. `scripts/style-screens.sh` (~1 min) runs `StyleScreenshots` the same way (`-a styleScreenshots=true`): the whole tuner screen with the A string in tune in every style, light and dark, to `build/owner-screens/styles/`, for looking at a changed background or in-tune colour everywhere at once.

## Meter art language
The looks are drawn "as if by the same artist" (owner, after two rounds of moving mock-ups, October 2026). The approved mock-ups are the reference: [docs/design/meter-looks-preview.html](../design/meter-looks-preview.html) and, for the open face (no plate), [docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html](../design/theme-styles-preview.html) (their top comments list the same rules). *u* = box width / 340, about 1 dp on a phone.
- **Colours:** roles only, from the current style: background (stage), surface (plate), onSurface (ink), onSurfaceVariant (muted), outlineVariant (track), primary (accent) and the "in tune" colour. No ornaments beyond the style's finish (below). Minor ticks are ink at 50 % and numbers ink at 80 % (`MINOR_TICK_INK`, `SCALE_NUMBER_INK`), so they keep 3:1 and 4.5:1 on every style's background.
- **Font:** only the style's number face (`numberFontFor(style)`), with lining figures (`LINING_FIGURES`); wide faces (Michroma, Orbitron) at 82 %.
- **Plate:** none. No plate, frame or drop shadow: the meter sits straight on the screen's background. The dial (Classic) has a 146u radius around a pivot 17u above the box's bottom.
- **Field:** instead, a calm field: a soft oval wash of the background colour, 190u × 150u each way round a point 47u above the box's bottom middle, 75 % in its middle, 56 % at 62 % of the way out, 0 at its edge, no edge line. The same for every look.
- **Glass:** dial looks have none. A window that is an object lying on the screen (the loupe's glass, since Phase 60) has a 1u rim in ink at 14 %, one sheen from the top (white 28 % light, 7 % dark, gone by 45 % of its height) and a soft contact shadow under it (10u blur, 4u down, black 50 % dark, 16 % light). A recessed window (the spirit level's tube until Phase 60) was also sunk toward the background, with an inner shadow under its top edge (7u blur) and inner corners of 10u; the loupe lies on the screen, its face a little lighter than the screen and without the inner shadow.
- **Scale** (the open face's larger one): a tick every 5 cents, none at 0. Major (every 10) 13u × 2.1u in ink; minor 7u × 1.3u at 50 %; the style's caps. Numbers 10–50 without sign, 12.5u, ink at 80 %, centred 27u from the tick's base, at least 8u apart (`numberFit` sets close numbers up to 30 % smaller; only Classic has a scale). The dial's ticks start 4u inside its arc.
- **♭ and ♯:** inside the box, in muted, at the ends of each look's scale (`SignsArt`, 20u in the music-sign face): under the dial's end ticks (fitted between them and the box's bottom), under the ends of Pointers' track and of the loupe's scale, under the outer arrows, in the light arc's and the light bars' lower corners. No row of signs under the box, so the meter takes 2:1 and nothing more.
- **Centre:** one 3.5u rounded bar in the accent.
- **Marker** (needle, pointer, the loupe's marker, lights or bars): a small soft shadow (3u blur, 1.5u right, 2.5u down); the beam glows instead (a 6u halo of itself at 18 %).
- **Finish** (`MeterFinish.kt`, the mock-up's `fin`; one of each per style, same proportions, same art): needle (Ancient spear, Classic watch hand with a ring near its tip, Retro VU line with an accent tip, Contemporary plain bar, Futuristic beam with a chevron, Space Age ball tip), hub over the pivot (stone, cap in the centre colour, ink disc with a dot, ink dot, ring, ringed planet), arc (dots in the accent, railway of two ink hairlines, broad band in the track colour, line, dashed accent line, dots), tick caps (round, butt, butt, round, square, round) and, for Ancient only, ticks drawn by hand (±12 % length, ±20 % width, ±0.05 rad, from a fixed hash so they never change). Classic wears the needle and the arc; the scale and Pointers' track and ticks the caps. The arcs are decoration (the ticks carry the scale), so the dashed and dotted accent arcs (55–60 %) need no contrast minimum.
- **Light:** one glow only, radial, in the "in tune" colour (32 % dark, 16 % light at its middle, fading to 0), never clipped by the box. The loupe's glass hides it where it lies, so it shows the glow again through the glass (clipped to the glass, at 80 %).
- **At rest:** every look's centre (Classic's centre bar and hub, Light arc's middle ray, Pointers' fixed pointer, the loupe's centre mark, Arrows' bar, Light bars' centre bar, its dots and its peak's light) is the muted colour without a note and fades to the accent with `presence` (`MeterRoles.centre(tuned, presence)`, `RestingCentreTest`): with wallpaper colours the accent could be green (before Phase 43's guard; a teal just past 40° still can come near), and a resting meter must never look in tune.
- **In tune:** one eased amount (smoothstep, 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out) blends centre mark and marker to "in tune"; the glow eases the same way. Both follow the steady hint's decision; with "Remove animations" both switch at once. Nothing flashes, bursts, flies, pulses or repeats.
- **Motion:** springs with a damping ratio of 0.5 or more.
- **Reading to the cent** (Phase 60, [docs/design/meter-precision-preview.html](../design/meter-precision-preview.html)): Classic reads best because its needle tip moves ~4.3u per cent, it has a tick every 5 cents to compare against, and a thin needle meets a thin centre bar. So every other look uses the stretched middle (`meterStretch`), a few fixed marks without numbers, thin edges instead of soft blobs, and shows the in-tune window (`MeterReading.inTuneWindow`) where the mock-up shows it. Pointers and Loupe came first, then Light arc (stretched rays, marks at 5 and 10 cents, a shorter trail), Arrows (steps at 2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 cents) and Light bars (stretched bars, a narrow peak, the window's bars tinted, a centre bar with two dots).

## Wave view
Most people don't know what a cent is, so the wave display (`WaveView`, 240 × 40 dp, low and frameless so it doesn't compete with the meter) shows how far off the note is as a moving shape under the note. It borrows the beats you hear between two close notes: `WaveMotion` (`:core:dsp` `strobe/WaveMotion.kt`, plain Kotlin, shared with the watch; the ribbon's shape `ribbonAt` beside it in `RibbonShape.kt`) pinches the ribbon into pulses.
- **Drift follows the strobe rule exactly:** `WaveMotion` runs its own `StrobeMotion` ([Notes, tunings and audio](notes-and-audio.md), *Strobe model*), so it stands dead still within half a cent and drifts right when sharp, left when flat, faster the further off (up to 4 pulses/s from 10.5 cents). It uses the exact cents, not `inTune`, so the last few cents still show.
- **Shape shows distance from afar, up to 50 cents:** the pinch deepens (`depthFor`) and more pulses fit across (`beatsFor`, 1.5 to 4.5) the further off; within half a cent the band is perfectly even. Shape eases towards the reading at 5/s, so a new string never makes it snap.
- **Breathing:** off pitch the band breathes slowly (one breath every 2.5 s, 10 % of its height). On the note it fades out (`WaveMotion.stir` → 0 in about a second), so nothing moves at all, like a strobe disc. The owner picked this over breathing on gently (October 2026); an old saved `wave_breathes_in_tune` is ignored.
- **Colour:** off pitch a tint of `primary` at 80 % (60 % was too dim on dark backgrounds); in tune the "in tune" colour with a soft halo; without a reading a faint line.
- **No words for the distance:** during a note the pill stays empty and uncoloured (it keeps its place); in tune a small check mark shows in its middle in the "in tune" colour, or at its start while a tip or the tune hint fills it, and fades out where it was, so colour-blind players see in tune too; and the frequency line is left out; in tune shows in the note's colour, glow and pop. With the tune hint on, the pill shows "Tune up ↑" / "Tune down ↓". TalkBack still hears the cents.
- **Drawing:** the frame loop runs only while there is a reading, with `withInfiniteAnimationFrameNanos` (UI tests skip infinite animations), and redraws only the view's own layer, without recomposing. `WaveView` is `clearAndSetSemantics {}` (test tag `TunerTestTags.WAVE` only); `NoteDisplay`'s live region says the reading, and the sentence never changes between frames.
- **Layout:** 240 × 40 dp at any font size (30 dp in the compact note on a phone on its side). In two-pane on a short screen the note and wave get smaller so the readout fits.

## Strobe look
The first wave look: two rows of stripes that drift right when sharp, left when flat, and stand still within half a cent, for the last cent of fine tuning. `drawWave` passes `WaveMotion.phase` to `drawStrobe` (`dial/Strobe.kt`) in the current style's own marks (see *Visual styles*), the lower row at twice the speed (`phase · 2`), like the rings of a strobe disc. The marks fade over the outer fifth at each end (`strobeFade`, by position only, so the wrap stays seamless). Without a reading the rows shrink to half height in the faint resting colour. On the note it freezes, as every look does. Every look shares one view and one set of layout tests.

## Wave looks
Setting "Wave look", shown while the display is the wave: six looks (`WaveLook`: strobe, ribbon, dots, fireflies, loop, ripples, in that grid order), picked from `WaveLookPicker` cards with live previews (`LookCardGrid`, as the meter picker).
- **One cue, each in its own way:** off pitch something moves one step of the look per strobe period (`WaveMotion.phase`), right or outwards when sharp, left or inwards when flat; within half a cent everything stands still. `unrest(depth)` (0 on the note, ~0.4 a cent off, 1 at 50 cents) shows distance from afar and eases to nothing, so every look settles into one calm shape on the note.
- *Strobe:* see above. *Ribbon:* pulses drift and smooth out (`dial/SoundRibbon.kt`, in each style's marks). *Dots:* a dot-matrix strip scrolling one dot per period (`dotColumnAt`, `edgeFade`). *Fireflies:* eight specks per pulse riding the pulses, scattered by `unrest`, gliding into an even line on the note (`fireflyAt`). *Loop:* a hoop spinning like a coin at the beat rate, its brighter near half sweeping the way the note is off, settling face-on into a still ring (`loopAt`, `loopWidth`, `loopNearSide`). *Ripples:* five rings spreading out when sharp and drawing in when flat, swelling unevenly off pitch, smooth ovals on the note (`rippleAt`, `rippleWobble`).
- Strobe and ribbon use each style's own marks; the other four have one shape in the style's colours.
- Every position is a continuous periodic function of `phase`, so nothing jumps when the direction flips or the phase wraps. JVM tests check each function for direction, stillness on the note and no jumps.
- Drawing allocates nothing: one `Ribbon` refilled, the caller's `Path` reused, strokes from `roundStroke` / `buttStroke`, cached per quarter-pixel width. `drawWave` (`dial/WaveLooks.kt`) draws them; the settings previews use the same `drawWave` through one shared `WaveMotion`.
- **Tests:** `WaveViewTest` (every look in every style drawn inside its box, at most a 4 dp glow outside; motion without recomposing), `TunerContentTest` (TalkBack skips the wave and hears the reading once), `SettingsContentTest` (radio buttons, names on one line at 1.3× and 2× font). The wave's size is the same for every look, so the layout tests cover all six.

## Visual styles
Six styles, in the owner's order: Ancient, Classic, Retro, Contemporary (the default, and the only one that can follow the wallpaper), Futuristic and Space Age. A saved Prehistoric or Middle Ages (styles of older versions) opens as Ancient (see [Lifecycle](lifecycle.md), *Saved settings*). Colours, faces, shapes and backgrounds follow the approved mock-up [docs/design/theme-styles-preview.html](../design/theme-styles-preview.html) (its data block is the source). Each style owns one accent hue round the colour wheel (Ancient terracotta, Retro avocado, Futuristic cyan, Contemporary blue, Space Age violet, Classic burgundy); greens are kept for "in tune". Each style sets:
- **Palette** (`StylePalettes.kt`): a light and a dark `Palette` of the mock-up's roles (background, surface, ink, muted, track, accent + onAccent, inTune + onInTune, and `second`/`third` for background art only, never text), copied exactly, turned into a Material 3 `ColorScheme`. **Soft grounds** (October 2026, `docs/design/background-tones-preview.html`): light mode was too white and dark mode too black, so the backgrounds sit at CIELAB lightness ≈ 91.5 (light) and ≈ 11 (dark; Futuristic 8 for its neon) instead of 95–97 and 2–8, surfaces 4 (light) or 5 (dark) above, each in the style's own hue with its tint raised (never plain grey); Ancient's light stone was already there. Four colours went a shade darker to keep their contrast: Contemporary light's and Futuristic light's in tune and Futuristic light's accent (4.5:1), and Retro light's ink (its meter ticks, 3:1). **Matched greens** (October 2026, the "matched" set of `docs/design/calm-backgrounds-preview.html`): one fresh grass green for every style clashed with the backgrounds, above all Space Age by night, so each style's in-tune pair leans the style's way (Ancient olive, Classic and Space Age a cool mint, Retro and Contemporary sea green; Futuristic's neon green stays), every one still 4.5:1 on its grounds and still plainly green; the watch copies Contemporary's dark one (`WatchDialTest`). In the scheme, primary = accent; primary and secondary containers (selected segments, chips, the slider's track) = surface mixed 22 % (light) / 32 % (dark) with the accent; secondary/tertiary = second/third (the background reads them there, so Contemporary's wallpaper colours reach it); outline = muted, outlineVariant = track. `StylePaletteTest` holds every palette, light and dark, to WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for ink, muted, accent and inTune on the background, surface and surface container, for onAccent/onInTune on their fills and for text on every container; the meter's numbers (ink at 80 %) 4.5:1 and its minor ticks (ink at 50 %), centre mark and outlines 3:1. It also keeps the accents at least 30 degrees apart in hue and out of the greens. **Wallpaper colours keep the greens free too** (Phase 43): a green wallpaper gave Material You a green primary, so picked segments, the open tab, the metronome's Start and the strum check's marks looked "in tune". `colorSchemeFor` passes the wallpaper scheme through `keepingInTuneApart`: when its primary lies within 50° (`IN_TUNE_HUE_GUARD`; 40° until the matched greens of October 2026 moved the in-tune hue towards teal, which put a yellow-green wallpaper 43° away) of Contemporary's in-tune hue, measured as OKLCH hue (`hueOf`, so a yellowish and a bluish green are as far apart as they look; HSV put Material's usual green 43° away), primary, onPrimary, surfaceTint and inversePrimary become Contemporary's own, and the primary and secondary containers are the wallpaper's surface mixed with that blue as the palettes mix theirs, with the wallpaper's onSurface on them. Backgrounds, surfaces, text and secondary/tertiary (the background art) stay the wallpaper's; any other accent is left alone. Light and dark each compare with their own in-tune green; the widget follows because it draws from `colorSchemeFor`. `WallpaperColorsTest`: green, yellow-green, mint and teal wallpapers fall back, readable (4.5:1) on the wallpaper's grounds; blue, violet, red, orange and yellow keep theirs.

Ancient: Forum (antique Roman letters; the owner found Caesar Dressing too gimmicky and picked Forum from six calmer faces in `docs/design/ancient-font-preview.html`), numbers in Alegreya Sans Medium (kept from the approved readouts).
