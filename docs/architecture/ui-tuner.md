## Tuner screen
`TunerScreen` wires the ViewModel; `TunerContent` is the stateless body used by previews and UI tests. Upright (`SingleColumnLayout`, 2.0's combination in [docs/design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html](../design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html)) the column fills the screen's height: the top row (the gear, the picker chip, focus mode's button), the ruler, the lens, the word with the cents under it, and the pegs at the bottom; the free height goes 1 : 2 above the lens and above the pegs (`TOP_GAP_WEIGHT`), each gap at least 24 dp. The ruler names the note: since Phase 71's last step there is no big note, no octave beside it and no hertz under it ([history](history.md), *Tuner screen*). The lens takes what the rest leaves (`LensOverWord` in the `FitColumn`'s shrinking part): 0.85 times as high as wide where there is room (on the Xiaomi 12X, 353 × 300 dp), its height giving way on a short screen (the Galaxy S26's 570 dp) down to half its width, never its width (owner, 2026-10-21: shrink the note, never the meter; the note went, so now the lens gives way in height only). The word keeps its size. With a tool on, its panel in the lens's place shrinks instead (keeping its 2:1 shape, down to 160 dp, `FitGauge`), so the tool's text under it keeps its size. Only with a very large font does it scroll. The lens is the column less 20 dp each side (`LENS_INSET` past the 8 dp `METER_MARGIN`, which a tool's panel spans); everything else keeps a 16 dp text margin (`COLUMN_PADDING`). 1.1's column shrank the big note to fit (`FitNoteColumn`, down to 32 dp, the octave never past half the letter) before the meter; it went with the note.
- **Upright tablet** (Phase 52): the column is at most 480 dp wide, so on a Lenovo Tab M11 upright (800 dp) the meter was 464 dp across and about 290 dp of screen lay empty under the note. There the whole column is now laid out as on a phone and drawn bigger (`columnScaleFor` and `ScaledUp` in `:core:designsystem`'s `ScaledUp.kt`, shared with the metronome's upright column, [Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md); `columnScaleFor` is JVM-tested in `ColumnScaleTest`; `ScaledUp` gives the column a `LocalDensity` with that many more pixels to the dp): it is laid out in the window divided by the scale, as on a phone of at least 560 × 760 dp, so the meter, the note, the string buttons, every text and gap grow together and keep the phone's proportions, and no rule above needs a tablet case. The scale is the smaller of width / 560 and height / 760, at least 1 and at most 1.5 (720 dp across on a large tablet): 1.43 on the Tab M11 (a 686 dp column, a 663 dp meter), 1 on every phone (at most about 480 dp wide, and on its side far too short), growing smoothly with the window so a resized window doesn't jump; the provider stays at 1, so crossing the point keeps the column's state. Text keeps Android's own non-linear font scaling (sp go to dp through the screen's density, then to pixels at the scaled one). Popups opened from the column (the more tools menu, tooltips) inherit it and grow too; the instrument and tuning panel, the two panes on its side and focus mode keep their own sizes. Tests measuring nodes inside the column read their bounds in the window's dp (`windowBounds` in `TunerContentTest`): `getBoundsInRoot()` uses the node's own, scaled, dp. `TunerContentTest.anUprightTabletShowsThePhonesColumnBigger` checks that every part on an 800 × 1240 dp tablet at 1.3× text sits where it sits on the phone-sized window, that much bigger, with no text cut off, and that a phone keeps its own size.

From top to bottom:
- **Top row** (2.0): the gear that opens Settings in the start corner (Phase 69; on its side at the rail's top instead), the picker chip in the middle and focus mode's button in the end corner (*Picker chip*, below), as the tuner mock-up's top row ([docs/design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html](../design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html)); the signal bars, where they are on, right after the gear only while a tool's panel stands in the ruler's place (else beside the ruler's heard note, *Ruler*). The metronome and Practice are tabs ([UI basics](ui.md), *Navigation bar*).
- **Picker chip** (Phase 70, `PickerChip` in `:core:designsystem`, `TunerPickerChip` in `TunerScreen.kt`): what is being tuned in a pill, "Guitar · Drop D ▾" (`tuner_tuning_name`; the player's own tuning by its own name, a lowered one as "Drop C", `PickerNames.kt`) or "Any note ▾" in chromatic mode (`tuner_any_note`); a tap opens the picker sheet (*The picker sheet*, below). The pill is 40 dp high in a 48 dp touch target, the surface colour with a 1 dp `outlineVariant` edge, the name in `titleSmall` semibold and the ▾ in the muted colour; it sinks and lights when pressed, as every button (`PressFeedback.kt`). Centred at the top, 8 dp down, in a row of its own (`ChipRow`) with **focus mode's button** at its end, level with the chip, its 44 dp outline ending at the 16 dp text margin (owner, 2026-10-25, idea A1 of the Phase 43 rough mock-up: no button moves to another place when the mode changes). The chip stays centred between two equal 52 dp slots (the button's outline and 8 dp; 92 dp with the gear and the signal bars both at the start), so it never reaches the button; a long name (a large font, a long language, a 40-character own tuning) stays on one line and ends in "…". Its width follows its name, about its centre; nothing else moves when it changes. On its side the row heads the controls pane. Over an error there is no chip (the sheet can't open there); without the microphone `TunerScreen` shows it over the permission prompt, at the top centre beside the gear, from `TunerViewModel.panelTuning`. TalkBack hears the name as the button's ('Guitar, Drop D, button, double-tap to change instrument or tuning', `tuner_spoken_picker` and `instrument_button_action`). It took the mode switch's place, "Strings" / "Chromatic" (`ModeSwitch`, until Phase 70; [history](history.md)): the mode is the sheet's Any note switch now. The button itself: [UI basics](ui.md), *Landscape and wide screens*. No "A4 = … Hz" line.
- **Ruler** (2.0, Phase 71; `components/Ruler.kt`, the geometry in `RulerGeometry.kt`, JVM-tested in `RulerGeometryTest`; UI-tested in `RulerTest`): under the top row and over the meter in the plain tuner (a reading or waiting for one; none with a tool's panel), as the tuner mock-up's combination ([docs/design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html](../design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html)): which note. The chromatic scale slides under a fixed pointer in the middle, a mark and a name per semitone 58 dp apart on a line, 78 dp high. The reading sits at `rulerPosition(targetMidi, cents)`, the target note plus its cents (`TunerUiState.Detected.targetMidi`, the interpreter's target; in guitar mode a string tuned far off shows the note it sounds, three semitones sharp of E2 shows G). The heard note's name (`rulerHeardMidi`, the nearest) is 36 dp ExtraBold in ink, its neighbours 17 dp Medium in muted; each name grows and shrinks with its distance to the pointer (`rulerEmphasis`: full within a quarter semitone, small from 0.9, smoothstep), the bold name fading in over the plain one, so nothing jumps between two notes; names fade out over the last 32 dp at the ends. The names are the notation setting's (`rulerNames`: a string keeps its tuning's spelling, E♭ in Half step down, Any note the notation's own; Automatic is already resolved in the state), set with `musicText`, in dp, not sp: a drawing, like the meter's scale. The pointer, a small triangle under the line, takes the reading's tone (`readingToneAmount`, amber easing toward green, green while in tune: v2-plan.md, *The lens*); quiet (no note) the scale rests on the last note heard, a locked string, the tuning's first string or A4 (`rulerRestMidi`), every name and the pointer muted (fading over 300 ms). The position is read only while drawing, through a critically damped spring (2.4 Hz), so readings slide the scale without recomposing; with animations off it follows each reading at once; nothing is allocated per frame (the 24 names are measured once). TalkBack skips it (the note's live region speaks the reading until the word does); UI tests read `RulerNote` and `RulerQuiet` from its semantics (`TunerTestTags.RULER`). The **signal bars**, where they are on, stand beside the heard note: their 24 dp slot 42 dp right of the pointer and 4 dp down, above the neighbours' names (whose tops are about 33 dp down) and clear of the widest heard name, "Sol♯" (about 38 dp from the pointer); their 48 dp button reaches no further up than the gap under the chip. On its side the ruler spans the readout pane over the lens (since Phase 71's last step on a phone too: without the big note the pane has room for it, and the bars stand beside its heard note there as well); in focus mode it runs across the top between the two corners, clear of the exit button (72 dp in from each side, `FOCUS_RULER_CLEARANCE`), without the signal bars (they open a panel, a control), until Phase 73 lays focus mode out wide. Until then a phone on its side and focus mode showed no ruler, the big note naming the note.
- **Lens** (`Lens`, 2.0 Phase 71; *Lens* below) under the ruler, with `zIndex(-1)` so its glow, which reaches past its box, lies behind the picker chip and the word. The scroll areas fill the screen (`heightIn(min = …)`), so the glow fades out on the screen rather than at a scroll area's edge.
- **Word** (`TuneWordLine`, *Under the lens* below) under the lens, 24 dp below it: what to do and the cents, or what to play; the tuner's one item TalkBack speaks ("E 2, 12 cents flat, Tighten"). While listening it says "Play a string" in the same place, so nothing moves when a note arrives. In focus mode it stands under the lens. Until Phase 71's last step it stood under the big note and the hertz (`NoteDisplay`, with focus mode's small corner note `FocusNote`; [history](history.md)).
- **Guitar controls** (`GuitarControls`): a row of its own above the pegs (`StringSelector`, *Pegs* below) with the strum check's switch (or the more tools button) at its end, its outline ending where the last string's face does. Only in guitar mode, where the strings show; chromatic mode shows no tools button, which means nothing there (owner, 2026-10-25) (E₂ A₂ D₃ G₃ B₃ E₄, the target ringed and `selected`; each string a peg, "Lock to this string" / "Pick strings automatically"; a locked string carries a padlock, "Locked", and the word says "Play the A2 string").

A ticked string played clearly out of tune again loses its tick (owner, 2026-11-01), by the tuner's own out-of-tune rule: the hint line says "Tune up" or "Tune down" (`SteadyHint`: only past the in-tune window's exit margin, after settling) and the reading is past that margin (7 cents, Precise 3: `InTuneDetector.exitCentsFor`), without a break for `AllStringsInTune.UNTICK_SECONDS` (1 s) on that string. So a pluck's sharp attack, a wobble or the gap between plucks keeps it, nothing flickers, and a peg turned away shows within a second or so; back in tune, it is ticked again. Merely not held in tune (a new pluck settling, a reading inside the exit margin) keeps it. ## Strum check view
Strum once and every string of the tuning shows flat, in tune or sharp at once (the detector: [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md), *Strum check (polyphonic)*).
- **Entry point:** a switch above the strings, at the end of a row of its own, its outline ending where the last string button's face does (`StrumCheckToggle`: strings with a sweep across them, in the style's button outline and shape, filled with `primaryContainer` while on), so it lives with guitar mode's controls and leaves the top corners empty. The string row spreads its buttons evenly, so its edges are not the buttons': `GuitarControls` takes the faces' inset from the same fit rules the row uses (`stringFaceInset`, JVM-tested; the row is symmetric, so one inset serves both ends) and lines the switch's outline and the tuning's name up with them (at the row's edge the switch sat up to 22 dp past a bass's last string; reading the buttons' positions as alignment lines during layout left the row at stale positions when the tuning changed). Not saved: the app always opens on the tuner. Switching to chromatic turns it off; switching it on from chromatic goes to guitar mode. In the compact layout on its side it is reached like the other controls (tap the screen).
- **Audio** (hard rules 2–4 unchanged): one `AudioRecorder` collection serves both. `PitchPipeline.listen(range, strumStrings)` asks for every chunk whether strum check runs (`TunerOptions.strumChecking`, and no reference tone or its 0.3 s tail); while it does, chunks go to a `StrumListener` + `StrumDetector` (`StrumAnalysis`, made the first time and again only for another range; the frame follows the tuning's lowest string like the tuner's window) instead of YIN. It emits a `StrumReading` when strum check starts or starts over (other strings or A4), when a strum is heard or stops ringing, and with each reading (every 0.1 s). Back on the tuner, its window and smoothing start afresh. The switch never reopens the mic.
- **State:** `StrumCheckInterpreter` (pure, JVM-tested) makes `TunerUiState.StrumCheck`: verdicts from `StrumVerdict` at the tuner's own tolerance (5 cents Relaxed, 2 Precise; +2.5 for shadowed strings), the previous reading of the same strum holding "in tune" 1 cent longer. Each string shows **all readings of the strum so far together**, weighted by their frame's power (`StrumReading.levelDbfs`), so the loud start counts most and the noisy readings as the strings die away barely count (the phone check in [Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md)): heard when heard in more than half the weight, its cents the weighted median of the readings that heard it, less sure when more than half of those were (up to 100 readings, 10 s). A strum's first reading must hear at least half the strings, or it is taken for another sound (a voice, a knock) and what was shown stays. A new strum keeps the last strum's strings (faded) until its own first reading. A reference tone starts the strum check over.
- **Panel** (`StrumPanel`, in the meter's place at its 2:1 shape, so every layout places it like the meter): one column per string, in the string row's order: its name (octave low, as on the string buttons), a short upright scale (±50 cents, sharp up, flat down; ♯ and ♭ once at the panel's start; the in-tune band round the centre mark, faint and in the muted colour until the string reads in tune, then in the style's "in tune" colour, and the centre mark muted until the string is heard, so a panel waiting for its strum never looks like six strings in tune (green only ever means in tune)), a marker at the reading (the "in tune" colour, else the accent) and under it a check mark, the cents ("−12", "+8", up to ±99) or "–" for a string not heard (its scale faded). A **less sure** (shadowed) string has a hollow marker and paler figures. On a calm field like the meter's, in the style's colours, display face and number face, sized by the panel's width (u = width / 340, like the meter), not the font size. Markers glide for 250 ms.
- **Line under it** (`StrumStatus`, in the note's place): "Strum all the open strings", "Listening…", then "2 strings to tune", "Not every string was heard" or "All strings in tune" (in the "in tune" colour), with a smaller line: what the strings show, "Tap a string to tune it", "Strum again, or tap a string to tune it". Each line takes the room of its longest text (`TallestOf`), so nothing moves between phases. A polite live region.
- **Strings:** no target is highlighted. Tapping one, on the string row or its column in the panel, leaves strum check and locks the tuner to that string, to tune it (both call `onStringClick`, the ViewModel's `toggleString`; while a reference tone sounds, a tap stops it, as anywhere). TalkBack: a button with the action "Tune A 2" ("A 2 stemmen"; "Stop the tone" while one sounds), its spoken reading unchanged.

2.0, Phase 71 (docs/architecture/v2-plan.md, *The lens*; the mock-up docs/design/v2-tuner-screens-preview.html): the box under the ruler that says how far, in the old meter's place in the plain tuner. 1.1's pitch trail tool (Phase 31, *Tuner screen* in [history](history.md)) went as a tool of its own: its view, turned, is the lens's default look, so the "more tools" menu lists three tools; `ToolSwitches.pitchTrail` stays and still gates the trail's data (`PitchTrailFeed`), the brake that Phase 72 wires to the Light arc.
- **The box** (`Lens`, `components/Lens.kt`, in `MeterFrame`'s place; tag `TunerTestTags.GAUGE`): the surface colour with 22 dp corners; ♭ in the bottom start corner and ♯ in the bottom end one, 14 dp in, 12 dp, muted, in the music-sign font, drawn by the lens over whatever the look draws; the look clipped to the box. Its border is the line colour (`outlineVariant`) at 1.5 dp at rest and, with a reading, 2 dp in the reading's tone at 75 %, with a soft glow round the box in the tone (14 dp out, 28 in tune; drawn as rounded rects from the outside in under the face, so it needs no blur and works on every Android version), fading with the reading's presence. One silent item for TalkBack (`clearAndSetSemantics`, with `LensQuiet` for UI tests).
- **Size:** as wide as the column less 20 dp each side (`LENS_INSET` past the 8 dp margin; 353 dp on the Xiaomi, at most 440), 0.85 times as high as wide where the screen has room, its height giving way first, down to half its width (`LensOverWord` in the upright column; the word keeps its size). Since the big note, the pill and the hertz went (the phase's last step) the Xiaomi upright has room for the full 0.85 (353 × 300 dp); the S26's 570 dp gets less, its width the same. On its side (two panes, focus mode) it is half as high as wide until Phase 73 lays the wide screen out; the readout pane leaves 24 dp above and below for its glow (`LENS_GLOW_ROOM`), and focus mode puts the ruler above it and the word under it (the exit button in the top end corner, clear of the ruler).
- **The reading** (`LensReading`, `:core:designsystem` `lens/`): `MeterReading`'s fields (the marker on `meterFeel(look)`'s spring, presence, tuned, glow, the in-tune window of the Precise setting) plus the tone and the history. The tone (`readingToneAmount`, as the ruler's pointer): muted without a reading, amber eased toward green as the note nears the zone, green in tune; the last cents are held while a note fades so it doesn't flash amber. Read only while drawing. The presence, the in-tune easing and the tone are one `LiveTone` (`components/ReadingTone.kt`, `rememberLiveTone`), which `TunerContent` makes once and hands to the lens, the word and the screen's tint, so the three never disagree.
- **The looks** (`LensLookArt(look, reading, wide)`, `lens/LensLookArt.kt`): the trail (the default), the Light arc and the strobe (Phase 72), the saved `lensLook` (`TunerViewModel.lensLook`; Settings' "Meter" picks it from Phase 72's last step, a debug build and the frame-time build take `adb shell am start -S --activity-clear-task -n io.github.klaboukes.twangle/.app.MainActivity --es lensLook STROBE`: without a new task a stopped app gets its task's first launch again, with the old look). `wide` is a window wider than tall (`LocalLensWide` in `TunerContent`): the lens is then half as high as wide until Phase 73, and the Light arc opens less far.
- **The trail look** (`TrailLook`, `lens/TrailLook.kt`): the newest reading 28 dp under the top edge as a 7 dp dot in the tone with a soft glow (rings, stronger in tune), the older ones falling under it at 80 dp a second, fading out toward the bottom (to 28 % at 70 % of the way, through a layer faded by a gradient, so one stroke serves any tone); cents across on the stretched scale (`meterStretch`, 24 dp in from the sides, pinned at ±50); a tick every 10 cents on the top edge (12 dp at the ends and the middle, 5 dp between), a hairline down the middle, the in-tune zone as a band of the "in tune" colour at 12 % down the whole height (+8 % while the glow is lit). The parts of the line that were in tune stay green, the rest take the tone. Gaps and a change of note break the line. Geometry pure and JVM-tested (`TrailGeometry`).
- **Smooth and cheap,** as 1.1's trail: a frame loop (`runTrail`, `TrailClock`) runs only while a note is in view, bumping a counter the drawing reads, its moment pulled gently toward the newest reading; once the line has fallen out of the box, or no reading came for 0.5 s, it draws once and polls every 0.1 s. Paths, strokes, the fade and the layer's paint are made once per size; nothing is allocated per frame. Where infinite animations don't run (UI tests) the line is drawn still at the newest reading and redrawn only while a note is in view, so a quiet lens draws nothing new.
- **The Light arc look** (`LightArcLook`, `meter/LightArc.kt`, Phase 72; 1.1's Light arc meter, the mock-up's `lensArc`): 17 rays of 5 lights on the stretched scale, the wedge from the centre to the reading lit in the tone with a soft halo round each light (four rings in the tone), a light the reading leaves fading over 0.12 s (`LightArcHeat`, a frame loop only while one fades), the centre ray muted off the note and green in tune with its neighbours half lit, the in-tune glow behind it, ink dots past the outer lights at 5 and 10 cents and the window's edge (`lightArcMarks`), the unlit lights in the line colour. The fan is fitted to the box (`FanGeometry`, JVM-tested in `FanGeometryTest`): ±1.2 rad upright and ±0.8 rad wide, the largest that fits with 16 dp beside the outer lights and 14 dp above the marks and under the inner lights, centred in the height: 321 dp across on the Xiaomi's 353 × 300 dp lens. Quiet: dark but for the centre ray, muted.
- **The strobe look** (`StrobeLook`, `lens/StrobeLook.kt`, Phase 72; 1.1's strobe wave look, the mock-up's `lensStrobe`): three bands of Contemporary's rounded bars (`drawStrobeBand`, `dial/Strobe.kt`), 14 dp in from the sides and top, 10 dp apart, 20 dp left under them for ♭ ♯, each on the background at 55 % with 8 dp corners, a 2 dp centre line in the ink at 60 % across them, the stripes in the tone at 85 % fading out at the bands' ends (`strobeFade`); no halo in tune (1.1's drew a box round each bar on the phone; the lens's border glows instead). The top band's stripes are a fifth of the lens's width apart (24 to 64 dp: 64 on the Xiaomi), halved in each band below. They drift left when flat and right when sharp at the strobe's speed (`StrobeMotion.driftSpeed`: still within half a cent, 0.4 stripes a second more per cent beyond, at most 4), eased to the lens's reading: the top band an eighth of it in its own stripes, each band below twice as fast across the screen (`StrobeDrift`, its phase a whole multiple of the top band's so nothing jumps; at most 32, 64 and 128 dp a second). The zone isn't drawn: the bands coming to rest is the cue. The frame loop (an infinite animation) runs only while there is a reading and redraws only when the stripes moved; quiet, the stripes stop where they are, muted at 30 %. JVM-tested in `StrobeLookTest` (band periods and places, direction, speed, the wrap) and `StrobeTest`.
- **The quiet state:** when the string dies the presence eases to 0 (the border back to the line colour, the glow off, the tone and the line's green to muted) and the line falls out of the box; the Light arc goes dark but for its muted centre ray, the strobe's stripes stop, muted at 30 %.
- **TalkBack:** the lens says nothing; while the tuner has a history, the trail's motion joins the word's spoken reading after the word (`trailMotionWords`: `PitchTrailDescriber`, settled over two looks half a second apart): "E 2, 12 cents flat, Tighten, wavering", "…, bending up" / "bending down"; a steady note adds nothing.

## Under the lens
The word with the cents, always (until Phase 71's last step under the big note and the frequency; 1.1's "Below the note" setting, `TunerDisplay`, also offered the wave view in the cents' place, and was the wave by default; Phase 67 took the setting and the wave view out: [history](history.md), *Meter, wave and styles*).
- **The word** (2.0, Phase 71; `TuneWordLine`, `components/TuneWord.kt`; the choice `TuneWord` and `tuneWordFor` in `TunerHint.kt`, JVM-tested in `TuneWordTest`): one word under the lens that says what to do, the mock-up's 26 sp bold (22 compact) in the reading's tone: with strings **Tighten** (flat: raise the pitch) or **Loosen** (sharp), in Any note **Too low** or **Too high** (no string to turn), and **In tune**; "Play a string" ("Play a note", "Play the A2 string", "Playing D3") while nothing is heard, in a quieter 20 sp semibold, muted. Dutch: "Strakker", "Losser", "Te laag", "Te hoog", "Zuiver". Under it, small and muted, the cents ("12 cents flat"; in tune only how many, "2 cents"), or a tip about the sound ("Play a bit louder", in the accent colour) in their place, with a note and without. It follows the tuner's own cue (`TunerUiState.Detected.hint`: `TuningCue`'s margins and `SteadyHint`'s settle and least show times), so it never flickers between Tighten and Loosen near the note; a heard note whose cue hasn't settled (its first 0.2 s) keeps the word it had, so the line goes from "Play a string" straight to "Tighten". Each line keeps its height whatever it says and keeps to one line, set smaller where it doesn't fit (`TunerContentTest.theWordMovesNothing`). The colour is the `LiveTone`, read while drawing (`ColorProducer`), taken toward the ink on a light background just enough for the amber to read at 4.5 : 1 on the tinted screen (`wordInkShare`, 0 on dark), and the cents' muted colour likewise (`mutedInkShare`: 14 % of the green brings Ink light's muted to 3.9 : 1); `WordColourTest`. Tags: `TunerTestTags.WORD` (the spoken block), `WORD_TEXT`, `WORD_CENTS`.
- **TalkBack:** the word is the tuner's one speaking item (the lens and the ruler say nothing, the note and the cents are hidden): one polite live region, paced (`PacedSpeech`, the screen's one, so the pacing carries over from a note to "Play a string"), "E 2, 12 cents flat, Tighten", "E 2, in tune", "E 2, 8 cents sharp, Too high" in Any note, a tip in the word's place ("…, Play a bit louder"), then the trail's motion ("…, Tighten, wavering"). The word joined the spoken reading always: 1.1's "tune up" / "tune down" came only with *Show tuning help* on (`tuner_tune_up`, `tuner_tune_down` and their spoken twins went from every language). The strum check's strings say "Tighten" and "Loosen" too.
- **Show tuning help** (1.1's `showTuneHint`) went in Phase 71: the word is always there (the owner, 2026-11-02), and the ticks have their own toggle, *Tick off tuned strings*, off by default (*Strings ticked off*; v2-plan.md, *Settings from 1.1*). Its "In tune. Play." takes the word's quiet line once every string is ticked.
- **The screen's tint** (2.0, Phase 71; v2-plan.md, *The lens*, Colours): while a note is heard the whole screen is tinted 14 % toward the reading's tone (`SCREEN_TINT_SHARE`, `tintedBackground` in `:core:designsystem`'s `StyleBackdrop.kt`, JVM-tested in `ScreenTintTest`): amber far off, eased toward green, green in tune, never red; off in the quiet state, fading with the lens's presence (with "Remove animations" at once). Tool panels have no reading, so no tint. `TunerContentTest.theScreenIsTintedTowardTheReadingAndNotWhileQuiet`, `theTintGoesWithTheTuner`.
