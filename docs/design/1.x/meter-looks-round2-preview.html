Meter looks, round 2

Four of today's six looks share the same numbered half-circle, so they look alike. In this round each look shows the note its own way, without numbers, so you can tell them apart at a glance. Classic keeps the numbers.

Every meter plays the same story: 18 cents flat, tuned up slowly, in tune, drifting 14 sharp, tuned back. Drag the slider to set the note yourself.
