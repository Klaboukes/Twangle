Metronome click sounds

Each player plays one bar of four beats at 100 BPM, beat 1 higher and louder, then stops. Listen on the phone's speaker and in headphones, and pick the two (or three) that should join the wood block.

Wood block today's click

The click the metronome has now: a short, woody "tock". A good all-rounder.

Soft beep

A gentle electronic beep, like a classic digital metronome. Easy on the ears for long, quiet practice.

Sharp click

A bright, high tick that cuts through a loud amp or a band. For playing loud or with others.

Low knock

A low, round knock, like a soft drum. For players who hear high sounds poorly, or who find high clicks tiring.

All four are about equally loud (the sharp click measures a little quieter, but sits where the ear is most sensitive). Every click lasts 40 ms, like today's, so the timing, the beat lights and the vibration stay the same.
