The drone on Scales: where the button goes

Pick A, B or C. Each puts the ▶ / ■ button somewhere else and opens the drone panel another way. The button keeps one size whether it plays or not; the panel is the same in all three. Drawn on the Xiaomi in Contemporary, upright and on its side, dark and light.

The panel (the same in every option)

Three choices, all remembered: Sound (the tanpura first, as the default), Notes (+ fifth by default) and Lower (the whole drone an octave down, off by default). While the drone plays, a pick is heard at once, crossfading. Back or a tap beside the panel closes it. The flute organ, the pad and Lower are very quiet on the phone's speaker; the panel doesn't say so (no hint texts), so it is up to you whether that needs a word somewhere.

The four sound icons enlarged: tanpura, harmonium, flute organ, warm pad. Rough; drawn properly when the button is built.

Recommendation

A. The button sits beside the scale it drones (a new root or scale is one row up), the neck keeps all its height, and the sound shows on the button, so the panel can be found without a hint, unlike C's long press. B is the most familiar (the metronome's Sound · Start) but costs the neck about a seventh of its size upright, which Phase 47 just won back.
