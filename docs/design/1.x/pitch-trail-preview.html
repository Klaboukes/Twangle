How should the pitch trail look?

Owner picked A, 2026-10-17: the band in the meter's place.

The pitch trail is a new tool in the more-tools menu: a line that draws the note over the last few seconds. Each phone below plays the same sample on repeat: a held note, a slow bend up and back, a short pause, then vibrato. Green means in tune. Pick one.

All three keep the note name, the cents pill and the strings as they are; only where the line goes differs. Colours, sizes and the length of the line can still be tuned once you pick the idea.
