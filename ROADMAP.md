Status legend: ☐ todo · ◐ in progress · ☑ done

Each phase ends in a state that builds and can be demoed. Phases are worked in the order they are listed here, not by number. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) and its topic files for design details.

Every finished phase is in [ROADMAP-ARCHIVE.md](ROADMAP-ARCHIVE.md), with the overview of the music tools that went into v1.0 and the finished part of Phase 23; only the open phases are below. Move a phase there once all its items are ☑, its owner checks moved to *Owner checks* below, and finished owner items too. Ideas not planned yet live in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).

**Phase numbers:** a new phase takes the next free number and goes where it belongs in the order. If that leaves the open phases' numbers out of order, renumber them so they count up again (finished phases keep theirs) and add the mapping to the archive's opening note, which lists every renumbering so far; commits, PRs and branches from before a renumbering use the old numbers.

## Owner checks (live tests)
Done by the owner by hand, with a real guitar or headset. They are not roadmap increments: `/roadmap-step` skips them.

- ☐ **A string change over a ringing string** (Phase 89, 2026-11-10, the Open G round Phase 91; any time, a guitar in standard tuning and then Open G, guitar mode): pluck the low E hard and let it ring; a second later pluck the high E softly without muting the low E: the needle should move to the high E within about a quarter second. Then the same for low E → B and A → high E. Then once in Open G (D G D G B D, the tuning picked in the app): let the low D ring and pluck it again a little harder: the needle stays on the low D, never the D string; pluck the D string, then the low D under it still ringing: the needle moves to the low D within about a quarter second. If the needle stays on the low string while the new one rings, say how hard you plucked it: that is the detector hearing both strings as the lower one (a known limit), not the hold this fixed. Also once with a TV on: a single string left ringing out must still read as itself and then go blank, never jump to another string
- ☐ **When you have the gear** (postponed 2026-10-16, no hurry):
  - Phase 5, a Bluetooth headset: with one connected, the tuner still listens through the phone's mic and reads a string correctly
  - Wired earbuds with a mic (3.5 mm plug, or USB-C), for a noisy room (2026-10-31): with them plugged in, Android listens through the earbud mic, which Twangle allows (only a Bluetooth headset is overruled), so the mic can lie close to the guitar. Lay the mic on the guitar's top a few centimetres from the soundhole (not in it), hands off the cable, and tune all six strings, first in a quiet room, then with loud music from the laptop: the note and cents follow each string, the low E included, at least as quickly and steadily as with the phone's own mic, and better in the noise; the reference tone plays in the earbuds; pulling the plug goes back to the phone's mic without a restart. If it passes, one line on the website's help page in English and Dutch ("Noisy room? Plug in wired earbuds and lay the mic on the guitar"), no hint in the app; if the low strings fail, the line names guitar and ukulele only
  - Phase 15, a bass (or a 7-string guitar or cello): the lowest strings read correctly (B0, E1, B1, C2), and the tuner shows no note while the amp only hums

## Phase 88 — The watch app on Play, right after the phone
Goal: the Wear OS watch app on Google Play as soon as possible after the phone app's production launch, and never holding the phone up (owner, 2026-11-08: "My first prio is to get the phone app released"; the watch app is a notable distinction from other tuners, so as soon as possible after). Decision 3 stands in that the first production version is the phone app only. The one risk to the phone is two Google reviews at once: a change sent while another is in review restarts that review (as 2.0's listing did; managed publishing, on since 2026-11-08, holds back the publishing but not the review), and the phone has two reviews ahead, 2.1.0 on the closed track and the production release. So the steps Google doesn't review run now, beside Phase 53; the reviewed ones wait until the phone's production release is approved. Play's watch rules and commands are in [docs/release.md](docs/release.md), *The watch app*.

Now, with no review from Google:
- ☑ **The Wear OS screenshots:** square, at least 384 × 384, the watch's round screen only, no watch frame ([docs/store-listing.md](docs/store-listing.md), *Watch screenshots*): a note in tune (the dot and the word in green), a note flat, and the metronome page (Phase 80), drawn on the Galaxy Watch 5 or the Wear OS emulator by a script like `scripts/store-screenshots.sh`. In every language if they show words (`release.sh listing` refuses a half-finished set), one set for all if they don't; `listing` checks them against Play's rules and sends them. **Done (2026-11-10):** `scripts/watch-screenshots.sh` (the `WatchStoreScreenshots` test) draws the app's own pages for a fixed reading, 960 × 960 px, black round the circle, opaque: the A in tune with the arc's glow, the low E 12 cents flat ("Tighten"), the metronome at 120 playing with its first beat lit; one set a language, since the tuner's show words and the language's note names; all 38 store folders in one run of under 5 minutes, made on the Galaxy Watch 5 from `main` and shared on GitHub (the Wear OS emulator draws the same). Held back, git-ignored, in `fastlane/held-back/android/<locale>/images/wearScreenshots/` and shared between the PCs like the phone's; `listing` checks them (square, 384 to 3840 px, every language as many, no change to the watch app since they were made) once they move into `fastlane/metadata/` at the watch's turn
- ☑ **The watch line in every language's full description:** one sentence on the standalone watch tuner and metronome, in all 36 languages, `StoreListingTest` passing; kept out of `fastlane/metadata/` until the watch app goes to production, since every `listing` sends what is on `main` and the line would promise an app Play doesn't have yet. **Done (2026-11-10):** held back in `fastlane/held-back/android/<locale>/watch_line.txt`, which nothing that sends reads; it goes first under *Also*, and in the nine descriptions it would push past 4,000 the languages line names the number with its own language and English, as Greek's and Tamil's do (recorded in the same file); `StoreListingTest` checks every language with the line in place (docs/store-listing.md, *The watch line*)
- ☑ **The docs follow the plan:** decision 3 as it is now (the watch right after the phone, its own steps never during a phone review) in docs/release.md, docs/store-listing.md and the release skill, where they say the watch app "isn't part of a release yet", and docs/growth/featuring-nomination.md, whose watch nomination is timed 3 weeks before the watch goes out. **Done (2026-11-10):** docs/release.md's *The watch app* says which steps run while the phone waits and which wait for its approval, one at a time; the release skill, `scripts/release.sh`'s head and docs/store-listing.md (the watch line and screenshots) follow it; the nomination's watch answers go into Phase 92's one nomination
- ☐ **The Wear OS form factor (the owner, in the Play Console):** *Test and release → Advanced settings → Form factors → + Add form factor → Wear OS*, and *Use a dedicated release track for Wear OS* if Play offers the choice (as far as Claude knows, this needs no review; if the Console says it does, wait until the phone's production release is approved). Then tell Claude what the Console shows for the watch: whether it asks for a closed test of its own (12 testers for 14 days) or the phone's production access covers it; Google's help says the rule is for the app as a whole, but doesn't say so for the watch
- ☐ **The watch app on its internal track:** `scripts/release.sh upload watch` (after the owner's yes) puts the 2.0.0 watch bundle (in `release/` on the PC that built 2.0.0, or the next release's) on the watch's internal testing track, which Google doesn't review. ## Phase 92 — Getting found, after launch
Goal: the rest of the growth plan, once Twangle is public on Play. The plan, its message and its rules (no paid ads, no tracking, no bought reviews, no other app named) are in Phase 50's goal. In order:
- ☐ **Store listing experiments:** after launch, **the owner** starts Play Console experiments one at a time, about two weeks each, Claude preparing the variants in every language the experiment covers: first the short description (the chosen one against the TalkBack one, already in *Being found*), then the first screenshot, then the icon. The winner goes into `fastlane/metadata/` and the losing variants into the store-listing doc with their figures
- ☐ **Search, language by language:** after the first month, **the owner** shares Play Console's search-terms report per country; Claude tunes each language's short description and title subtitle to the words people really use there (in normal sentences, Google's rules in the store-listing doc), starting with the languages where the big English tuners are weakest. `StoreListingTest` still passes; sent with `scripts/release.sh listing` after the owner's yes
- ☐ **Ask Google to feature it, once it has a track record:** **the owner** sends Google's featuring nomination when Twangle is public and has its first few hundred ratings, not at launch (owner, 2026-10-29: a brand-new app with no ratings is a long shot, so not worth the stress of the Christmas deadline). Claude first checks the form and the answers against the app as it is then ([docs/growth/featuring-nomination.md](docs/growth/featuring-nomination.md))
- ☐ **Where players ask:** Claude drafts one honest post per community ("I made a free tuner, no ads, no internet permission; what it does; I'm the developer"), short, in that community's language and tone, for r/guitar, r/Bass, r/ukulele, r/androidapps, r/WearOS (with the watch app), r/privacy, and a few big guitar forums in other languages; and short answers for the frequent "which tuner app do you use?" threads. **The owner** posts them, one per community, and answers questions. Drafts in `docs/growth/`
- ☐ **People who recommend apps to others** (online only, owner 2026-10-28: no printed flyers): short personal emails to beginner YouTube channels, to guitar teachers who teach online and to Android sites (three angles: privacy, the watch app, accessibility); and a small press page on Phase 54's website (icon, feature graphic, screenshots, a short text). **The owner** sends and shares them
