Metronome click sounds, round 3

Clicks made to keep time by: short, exact, the same on every beat, with beat 1 a little higher and louder. Each sound plays alone (one bar) and under a strummed guitar (two bars), then stops. Listen on the phone's speaker and in headphones, and pick the two (or three) you'd like in the app.

Wood block today's click, for comparison

The click the metronome has now.

Woody click

A better wood block for everyday practice: a dry, woody tick with no note of its own.

Rim click

A drummer's dry cross-stick on the snare's rim: precise, like playing along with a drummer.

Cowbell tonk

A short, choked cowbell, the studio click that cuts through a band.

Bright tick

A high, crisp tick that sits above a loud or distorted guitar, without being harsh.

All five are about equally loud on their own. In the guitar versions the guitar is clearly louder than the click, as when practising. The guitar is synthetic (the app's own reference tones), so judge the click, not the guitar.
