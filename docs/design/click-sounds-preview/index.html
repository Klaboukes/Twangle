Metronome click sounds, round 2

Each player plays one bar of four beats at 100 BPM, beat 1 accented, then stops. Listen on the phone's speaker and in headphones, and pick the two (or three) you'd like in the app.

Wood block today's click, for comparison

The click the metronome has now.

Temple blocks

Two hollow wooden blocks, a high one for beat 1 and a lower one for the others. Warm and woody; a good all-rounder.

Claves

Two rosewood sticks struck together: a bright, ringing "tock" that cuts through a band without being harsh.

Side stick

A drummer's cross-stick on the snare's rim: dry and familiar, for playing along as if with a drummer.

Hi-hat

A closed hi-hat tapped with a stick: a short, crisp "tss" high above the guitar, so it never covers the notes.

Marimba

A low rosewood marimba note with a soft mallet: round and warm, for players who hear high sounds poorly or find them tiring.

All six are about equally loud. These ring a little longer than today's click (0.2 to 0.4 seconds), as real instruments do; the beats themselves stay exactly on time.
