Where does "lower by" go?

Any tuning can be lowered by ½ to 2½ steps (one to five semitones, lower only). Already decided: a lowered tuning with a well-known name shows that name (Drop D lowered 1 step is Drop C); any other shows its name with the shift (Open G, ½ step down). Below are three places for the control. They all show the same tuning, so a tap in one changes all three; under each panel is what the tuner then shows. The real panel closes when you pick a tuning; here it stays open.

Recommendations

Where: A, one row above the tuning list. Always in the same place, never under the fold, two buttons of one size, and the list right under it shows the result.

Stays or resets: keep it (answer 1). Players who tune down usually tune everything down, so "½ step down" set once then gives Drop C♯, Open G ½ step down and so on. The name on the tuner always says what you have, so it is never hidden.

Another instrument: picking another instrument starts it not lowered (a ukulele is rarely tuned down with the guitar).

The names with a common name: Standard lowered ½ to 3½ steps is Half step down, Full step down, C♯ standard, C standard, B standard, B♭ standard, A standard; Drop D lowered is Drop C♯, Drop C, Drop B, Drop B♭, Drop A. The rest (DADGAD, the open tunings, your own) say how far down.

Chord charts, scales and the capo helper follow the lowered tuning by themselves: the shapes don't change.
