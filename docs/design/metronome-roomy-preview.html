A roomier metronome

Two ways to give the tempo dial the screen. In both the dial fills the width (about 360 dp, today 288), Tap tempo, Listen and Vibrate become icon buttons (their names stay for TalkBack and on a long press), Start becomes a round play button, and the tool switch loses its check mark.

A · Everything about tempo inside the dial

Start sits in the dial's opening at the bottom, − and + either side of the number. One quiet row of three icons underneath. Vibrate lights up in the accent when it's on. Inside the dial gets fairly busy.

B · − and + end the scale, Start in a player row

The scale runs into − and + (slower at the slow end, faster at the fast end). Underneath, a row like a music player: Tap tempo, a big Start, Listen. Vibrate becomes a small switch beside the beat lights, since it's about the beat. The dial stays calm inside. My pick.
