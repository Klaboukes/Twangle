→

A moment later, Google's window slides up. It is Google's, in Google's look, not Twangle's.

  • At most once, right after the last string is in tune; never while tuning, never on the first day, and no "Do you like Twangle?" first (Google forbids filtering out unhappy players).
  • Google decides whether it shows: it limits how often, so it may not appear at all, and it never appears in an install from the PC. Twangle can't tell whether it showed or what was given.
  • The rating is given without leaving the app; it is public on Play under the player's Google name, as in A.
  • Before building: Google's library must add no permission (internet would break Twangle's no-internet promise). The privacy policy would say the window is Google's. The About row stays.

Either way, the closed testers are asked to rate Twangle once it is public, and "Rate Twangle" stays in About.
