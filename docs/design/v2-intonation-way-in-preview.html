The 12th-fret check: how you get there

2.0's tuner has no "more tools" menu, so the 12th-fret check needs a way in of its own. It shows only after you turn it on in Settings, so a player who never sets up a guitar never sees it. Three ways, each drawn on the plain tuner (left) and with the check open (where it goes for all three: the check's steps in the lens's place, its line in the word's, and the pegs to pick a string). The pink dashes mark what is new.

The three ways

The check open (the same for all three)

The lens's place shows the two steps (Open · 12th fret) and the ring that fills while the note is taken; the word's place says what to do. You pick the string by tapping its peg, which is ringed in the accent while it is checked; a checked string carries its result on its peg (✓ fine, → move the saddle back, ← forward). Once a string has a result, the lens's place shows every string as a row of small pegs with its cents.
