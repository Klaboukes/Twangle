Let the metronome listen

The metronome listens to music nearby and suggests the tempo it heard; nothing changes until you tap Use. While it listens, a thin line traces round the Listen button's edge over the 15 seconds. Every button lights up when touched; Tap tempo flashes crisply on each tap. This is the approved design the app will follow. No sound unless you turn it on.

What to look at

  1. 1Listening. The button reads Stop; a thin, soft line traces round its edge, clockwise from the top, over the 15 seconds, and fades where it is when the tempo is found.
  2. 2The suggestion. A balloon with the tempo it heard, ½× and 2×, Not now and Use. A stopped metronome stays stopped after Use.
  3. 3Buttons. Every button lights up the moment you touch it and fades after you let go; Tap tempo gives one crisp flash per tap.
