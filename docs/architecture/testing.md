## Test layers
| Layer | How |
|---|---|
| `:core:dsp` | JVM tests with `TestSignals` (`testFixtures`, shared via `testImplementation(testFixtures(project(":core:dsp")))`): sines, saw/square, weak fundamentals, white noise, mains hum, band-limited audio at any rate, decaying plucks. Assert ±1 cent. `EdgeCaseTest` runs whole recordings through the app's chain (`TunerChain`) and checks what would be shown ([Signal pipeline](signal-pipeline.md), *Edge cases*). Recorded WAV fixtures go in `src/test/resources/`. Allocation-free paths have byte-counting tests. `PluckSynthTest`: the reference tone's pitch within 1 cent by YIN, decay, end on silence, no clipping, no click at start, end or release. `ClickTrackTest` and `ClickSoundsTest`: every click on its exact sample, no drift over ten minutes, accents per meter, tempo clamped and changed while playing, clicks that start and end on silence and never clip. `StrumDetectorTest`, `StrumListenerTest` and `StrumVerdictTest` on synthetic strums from `StrumChords` (`PluckSynth` strings, or stiff strings with any harmonic levels); `PartialFitTest` on plain sinusoids; `StrumAccuracyReport` prints the strum check's accuracy tables (with the benchmarks, not in CI). `ChordDetectorTest` and `ChordNamerTest` on synthetic strums of 41 open and barre shapes from `ChordShapes` (chord-chart frets in standard tuning, played through `StrumChords`) and on sounds that are no chord (single notes, power chords, muddles, noise); `ChordAccuracyReport` prints how often chords are named right, not at all or wrongly. `OnsetTempoDetectorTest` on synthetic music from `BeatSignals` (click tracks, kick/snare/hi-hat grooves with swing and loose timing, a plucked bass line); `TempoAccuracyReport` and `TempoDetectorBenchmark` run with the benchmarks. |
| `:core:audio` | JVM tests for source, rate and Bluetooth selection and chunk sizes; `ChunkReaderTest`, `MicOpenerTest` and `BlockingTransferTest` with a fake mic: silenced mid-session, 2 s of zeros (and a quiet room or a slow start that are not), stuck and failing reads and writes, the next rate when the first won't open. `FakeAudioRecorder` can also open at another rate (`openedConfig`), for the pipelines' tests. Instrumented tests record from the real mic (don't play test tones during them). `FakeAudioRecorder` (`testFixtures`) plays a `FloatArray` as chunks paced like a mic (instant under `runTest`), can loop or fail, and reports `isRecording` / `startCount`, so everything downstream, mic lifetime included, runs without a device. `FakeTonePlayer` (`testFixtures`) records `play`/`stop` and drives `isPlaying` (`finish()` = the tone died away). Instrumented `AndroidTonePlayerTest` plays short tones through the speaker: it ends by itself after the whole tone, `stop` ends it quickly, a new note replaces the old one, losing audio focus stops it. `FakeMetronomePlayer` follows tempo and meter and emits beats on request; instrumented `AndroidMetronomePlayerTest` plays clicks: beats heard 250 ms apart at 240 BPM with the right accents and told on time, tempo and meter change while playing, `stop` ends it quickly, another app taking audio focus doesn't stop it. `CallModeTest` (JVM): only `MODE_NORMAL` lets it play. `MetronomeDriftMeasurement` (on demand, `scripts/metronome-drift.sh`) plays ten minutes and reports drift and jitter ([Strum check and metronome](strum-and-metronome.md), *Metronome*). |
| `:core:settings` | JVM tests on a real DataStore file: defaults, saving, restart, clamping, unknown or unreadable values, migrations. |
| `:core:designsystem` | JVM tests: WCAG contrast of every palette, distinct styles, `ThemeMode.useDarkTheme`, font credits, the meter and wave maths. |
| `:feature:tuner` | ViewModel and interpreter tests with `kotlinx-coroutines-test` and the fake recorder; Compose UI tests for each state, layout, TalkBack and every look. |
| `:feature:metronome` | `MetronomeViewModelTest` with `FakeMetronomePlayer` and `FakeSettingsRepository`, `MetronomeListenTest` with `FakeAudioRecorder` too; Compose UI tests `MetronomeScreenTest`, `MetronomeStylesTest` (with the Practice tab's tool switch over it and without) and `MetronomeRecreationTest` ([Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md), *Metronome screen*; [Lifecycle](lifecycle.md)). |
| `:feature:practice` | `PracticeToolsTest` (JVM: the tools offered with and without the switch, the tool to open on); Compose UI test `PracticeToolSwitchTest` (the tool switch over Chords: TalkBack, taps, where it stands, Dutch at 2× on a 320 dp phone, every language with `--languages`); `PracticeTabTest` in `:app` runs the tab in the real app ([Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md), *Practice tab*). |
| `:feature:settings` | `SettingsViewModel` tests with `FakeSettingsRepository`; `CreditsTest`; `AppLanguagesTest` (the Language setting's list, names and matching); Compose UI tests (`SettingsContentTest`) for each control, the Language dialog, About and the licence dialogs; `SettingsStylesTest` for Settings and About in every style. |
| `:app` | `LocalizationTest`, `StoreListingTest` (the Play Store texts), `NavigationTest` and `FirstStartTourTest` (JVM); instrumented `MicLifecycleTest` ([Lifecycle](lifecycle.md)), `VolumeKeysTest`, `NavigationBarTest` ([UI basics](ui.md), *Navigation bar*), `TourStylesTest` and `TourFlowTest` ([Tuner screen and tour](ui-tuner.md), *Tour*). `StoreScreenshots` makes the Play Store screenshots in one language (only with `-a storeScreenshots=<Play locale>`, so normal runs skip it): the real app with a fixed sound through a stand-in microphone (`AppContainer.testAudioRecorder`, `testMetronomePlayer`), drawn on the store canvas on the phone; `scripts/store-screenshots.sh` runs it per language ([store listing](../store-listing.md), *Phone screenshots*). `StoreGraphics` (only with `-a storeGraphics=true`) draws the store icon and feature graphic the same way, through `scripts/store-graphics.sh` (*Icon and feature graphic*); `StoreListingTest` checks their sizes and formats. |
| `:wear` | `WatchPitchPipelineTest`, `WatchTunerViewModelTest` and `WatchDialTest` (JVM) with the fake recorder and `TestSignals`; Compose UI tests `WatchTunerTest` (and `WatchTunerScreenshots`, only with `-e watchScreenshots true`) on the Wear OS emulator, not the phone: `scripts/ui-tests.sh` leaves `:wear` out, so install both debug APKs there and run `adb -s emulator-5554 shell am instrument -w io.github.klaboukes.twangle.test/androidx.test.runner.AndroidJUnitRunner` ([Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md), *Watch app*). |
| `:baselineprofile` | On demand, device needed ([Threading and performance](performance.md)). |
| Running UI tests | Always `scripts/ui-tests.sh` (the [ui-test skill](../../.claude/skills/ui-test/SKILL.md)), never Gradle's `connectedDebugAndroidTest`, which is 15–25 s slower per module and uninstalls the app. The script builds every test APK in one Gradle call, then installs and runs each module with `adb shell am instrument` under a time limit, prints one line per module and runs only the modules a branch touches. `-a key=value` passes an instrumentation argument. One run at a time holds the phone (a lock folder on it), its MIUI app-op loop ends with the run, and it wakes the screen before each module: a run beside another one, a screen that is off, or leftover loops (each about a third of a core) make the first test of a run time out, which in a `--thorough` run is always an Ancient one. `--cool` runs one test class at a time and waits while the phone is 37 °C or warmer; `--languages` adds the `@EveryLanguage` checks. `scripts/overnight-ui-tests.sh` is the overnight run (the [overnight-run skill](../../.claude/skills/overnight-run/SKILL.md)): every module in every style with `--cool`, then every language, one style at a time with cooling pauses, then the store screenshots in every language (`scripts/store-screenshots.sh`), and one summary. |
| Device | Manual checklist per release: real guitar, noisy room, Bluetooth headset, permission denial paths. |

## Fast UI tests
Compose UI tests use `createSharedActivityComposeRule()` (`:core:testing`): one activity for the whole run, a fresh composition (own recomposer, test clock and `ViewModelStore`) per test. `SharedActivityTestRunner` stops AndroidJUnitRunner closing every activity after each test, which took ~0.7 s per test on the Xiaomi 12X; together they cut the tuner suite from ~8½ to ~3 minutes. Pixel checks take one capture and cut it up (a capture costs a frame), and build assertion messages only on failure. A test that changes the activity itself (rotation, recreation, window flags) still uses `createComposeRule()` or `ActivityScenario`. How to write them: [writing-tests.md](../../.claude/skills/ui-test/writing-tests.md).

## Every screen in every style
`TunerStylesTest`, `SettingsStylesTest` and `MetronomeStylesTest` check each screen in each of the six styles, light and dark, because a style brings its own fonts and button shapes: the tuner upright (a short narrow phone at 1×: 360 × 570 dp, what a 360 × 720 dp phone with navigation buttons leaves under its bars and the app's; the owner's Xiaomi 12X at 1.25×, less 80 dp for the navigation bar) and in its full layout on its side (less the 80 dp rail) fits without scrolling, nothing overlaps; at 2× text every part, Settings row, card name and About text can be scrolled to and is then wholly shown (`assertWhollyShown` in `:core:testing`: nothing clipped, no line cut off or ellipsized), and the string buttons fit the longest names (solfège flats); TalkBack hears the reading once, nothing in the meter or wave, the mode switch, strings, style/meter/wave cards and light-or-dark choices by name with which one is picked. `CompactLandscapeStylesTest` covers the compact layout, `MeterLooksTest` and `WaveViewTest` every look.

## Every language
`@EveryLanguage` tests (`:core:testing`) show the tuner (a note with the meter, listening with the wave), Settings, About, the metronome (with each of Listen's status lines, under the Practice tab's tool switch), the Practice tab's Chords tool, the tour's balloons and the navigation bar (Metronome and Practice) in each of the languages in `twangle.languages` (`shippedLanguages`, from `BuildConfig` of `:core:testing`) at 2× text, upright on a 360 dp phone and on its side, and check every text TalkBack can find, scrolled to in turn, is wholly shown (`assertEveryTextWhollyShown`: no letter cut off, no line lost, no ellipsis); a text taller than its scrolling column must fill it. Texts that are only measured, never placed (a status line keeping the room of its longest wording, `TallestOf` and `Kept` in the tuner's tools), are not on screen and are skipped. The Practice checks also fail a word broken across two lines (`assertNoWordBroken`; Chinese and Japanese may break anywhere): Android breaks "majeur-pentatonisch" at its hyphen only with hyphenation on, so Practice puts a zero-width space after hyphens (`breakableAtHyphens`). A failure saves a picture of the screen to the run's extra output, which the script copies to the module's `build/outputs/connected_android_test_additional_output`. They take about 20 minutes per style, so they run only with `--languages`, in the overnight run (every style). Dialogs, menus and the tuning panel are windows of their own that take the phone's language and text size rather than a test's, so those are checked on the phone.

## Quick and thorough UI runs
A normal run is quick: it leaves out the double checks. Style test classes (`TunerStylesTest`, `SettingsStylesTest`, `MeterLooksTest`, `CompactLandscapeStylesTest`, `StringRowStylesTest`, `TourStylesTest`, two checks in `WaveViewTest`) run in the default style only (`allIfThorough(VisualStyle.entries, VisualStyle.DEFAULT)`), and `@Thorough` tests (variants of a quicker test in another font size, language, orientation or view) are skipped; `--thorough` runs everything. This cut the tuner suite from ~3 minutes to ~1. Each family keeps its hardest case in the quick run (e.g. Dutch at 2× font on a phone on its side), so most layout breaks still show at once. The thorough run belongs to the overnight run (`scripts/overnight-ui-tests.sh`, which passes `--thorough`), so daytime work never waits for it (owner, 2026-10-16: limited tests during normal work, the full suite and its fixes overnight). Thorough runs do find what quick runs miss: the first one (overnight 2026-10-15/16) found tests written while their runs were deferred, and the tour stuck on step 2.
