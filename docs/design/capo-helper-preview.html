How should the Capo helper look?

The third tool in Practice. It answers "the song is in E♭, where does the capo go?" and shows which chord each shape sounds with the capo on. Guitar in standard tuning; it follows the Instrument item. Tapping a chord opens it in Chords. Pick one letter.

All three work for minor songs too (Cm uses Am shapes at fret 3) and use your note names (letters, solfège, German B and H).
