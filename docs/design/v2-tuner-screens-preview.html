Six tuner screens

Rough on purpose: one look, the same phone, the same reading in every design. Only the middle of the screen, the meter itself, differs. Drag the slider or let the demo tune a string, and compare how each one tells you which way and how far, and what it looks like while you turn the peg.

  • Kept: the pitch trail is the meter, the default; the Light arc and the strobe are the two other looks, in the same lens
  • Gone: the strings as a backdrop behind the meter
  • Gone: the tick-off per string: a toggle, off by default, so not drawn here
  • Same in all: the picker chip on top, one word at the bottom, plain pegs, focus mode top right and the gear top left, three tabs
Every phone shows the same reading. The demo plucks flat, tunes up, overshoots, settles, then goes quiet and moves to the next string.

The combination: the ruler, the lens and the colour

The ruler on top says which note: the whole chromatic scale sliding under a fixed pointer. The lens says how far. The colour of the whole screen says how close: a soft amber far off, a soft green in tune. The lens is the contract: left flat, right sharp, green in tune, quiet when the string dies. Only what is drawn inside it changes, and that is the one choice a player gets.

On its side: focus mode

One button turns the app on its side, even with rotation locked, and the same screen is laid out wide with nothing else on it. The ruler across the full width shows more of the scale, the lens gains pixels per cent, the colour fills the screen. The word and the string are small under the lens, the exit button has the corner. The pegs and the tabs come back with a tap.

In the lens:

The six, for reference

The same in all

  • The note and its octave on top, with which string it is.
  • One word at the bottom: Tighten, Loosen, In tune. The cents under it, small.
  • Colour says how close: amber far off, green in tune. No colour is the meter on its own.
  • The six strings as plain pegs; the one heard is ringed. No tick-off: that is a toggle in Settings, off by default, and when on, a tuned string's peg gets a small check.
  • No mode switch. The chip at the top, Guitar · Standard, opens the one picker, "What are you tuning?": instrument, tuning and Any note. One string shows the string; a strum shows the chord.
  • No tour, no hints. The focus mode button top right, the same corner its exit button has on its side (one button, no switch in Settings); the gear top left; the three tabs below.
