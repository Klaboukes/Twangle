The metronome, and a bar to reach it

The app gets a third screen, the metronome, so the gear button in the corner gives way to a bar at the bottom: Tuner · Metronome · Settings. Below are three kinds of bar to choose from (A, B, C). All three keep the big frameless meter free of boxes and lines. The metronome runs by itself on this page so you can see its beat lights; tap the bar to switch screens. No sound unless you turn it on.

What to look at

  1. 1Which bar: A, B or C? Try each in a few styles, especially Futuristic and Retro in the dark, where the background reaches the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2The beat lights. Each style uses its own marks (dabs, engraved bands, glowing capsules, voice-memo bars, neon bars, stars). Beat 1 is the biggest; in 5/4, 6/8 and 7/8 the start of each group is middle sized, matching the accented clicks.
  3. 3The dial. A round dial you drag, without a frame, drawn like the meter. Is that right, or would you rather have a big number and a slider?
  4. 4Starting. In the app the metronome opens stopped and stops when you leave it. Here it starts by itself so you can see it move.
  5. 5Names. Dutch: Stemmer · Metronoom · Instellingen, and "Tik tempo", "Trillen op de tel". Switch the language above to see them fit.
