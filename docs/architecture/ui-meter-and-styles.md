## Meter
The meter is the first thing anyone sees. Since 2.0's Phase 71 it is the lens under the ruler, drawing the trail, the Light arc or the strobe (the last two since Phase 72: [2.0 plan](v2-plan.md), *The lens*; [Tuner screen](ui-tuner.md), *Lens*). From Phase 67 until then it was 1.1's *Classic* dial alone; Classic went in Phase 71's last step with the last thing that drew it, the store's feature graphic, which draws the lens now. 1.1's other meter looks, its wave looks and the Calm or Bold choice went with their settings and card grids ([history](history.md), *Meter, wave and styles*). The saved look is read as `LensLook` by the lens look's rule ([Lifecycle](lifecycle.md), *Saved settings*). What follows is what the lens keeps from the meter and the parts kept for Phase 72's looks and the metronome's tempo dial.
- **One frame** (1.1's `MeterFrame` until Phase 71; since then the lens, `components/Lens.kt`, [ui-tuner.md](ui-tuner.md) *Lens*, which keeps these rules): it owned the 2:1 box (`METER_ASPECT_RATIO`; the dial draws its ♭ and ♯ inside it, at its scale's ends), the ±50-cent range (beyond it the needle pins at the edge, `meterCents`; without a reading it rests in the centre at 0.3 opacity), the look's spring, the in-tune amount `tuned` (smoothstep, 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out), the in-tune `glow`, the in-tune window, the reception fade and blur, and `clearAndSetSemantics {}` (the word speaks the reading since Phase 71, `NoteDisplay` before). It hands the art one `MeterReading`: cents, `velocity` (from the spring, without boxing), `presence`, `tuned`, `glow`, `inTuneWindow` and `applyMarkerLook(layer)`, read only in layer and draw phases (or in a `drawWithCache` block, which is made again when what it read changes). The window is the tuner's own, 5 cents each way or 2 with the Precise setting (`meterInTuneWindow(precision)`, the `InTuneDetector` windows): `TunerViewModel.inTunePrecision` → `TunerContent(inTunePrecision)` → `Lens(precision)`; Classic didn't show it, the lens's trail shows it as a band.
- **Art in the design system** (`:core:designsystem` `meter/` and `lens/`): `LensLookArt` draws the lens's look, so `:feature:settings` draws the same art without `:feature:tuner` (the lens's Meter cards, Phase 72), as it draws the reading's tone (`lens/LensTone.kt`). A part that only turns gets its own `graphicsLayer` and only changes its transform; a part that changes shape is redrawn from paths, brushes and strokes made once. The art is not clipped: the calm field and the glow fade out past the box (up to ~110u).
- **The stretched middle** (`meter/MeterStretch.kt`, Phase 60): `meterStretch(cents)` = sign · 50 · (1 + k)|x| / (1 + k|x|), x = cents / 50, k = 1.5 (`METER_STRETCH`): 2.5× the movement per cent at the centre, 0.4× at ±50, 50 stays 50, so 5 cents sits where 11 did and 10 where 19 did. The Light arc and the lens's trail place the reading on it (1.1's Classic never did: its numbers would have gone uneven).
- **Shared pieces** (`meter/MeterArt.kt`, named like the mock-up's helpers): `MeterRoles` (the colour roles), `GlowArt`, `TuneGlow`, `StaticArt`, plus `mix`. `FieldArt` (the calm field), `NeedleArt` (1.1's Contemporary needle), `markerShadowPaint` and `blurRadius` went in Phase 75 with the metronome's tempo dial, their last user, as did `MeterRoles`' number face, digits and background. Classic's own pieces (`ScaleMarks` with `numberFit`, `HubArt`, `TrackArt`) went with it in Phase 71. Everything is built in `drawWithCache`, so only a size or colour change rebuilds it; drawing allocates nothing. Shadows need hardware-accelerated `BlurMaskFilter` (Android 9+); on Android 8 the meter has no shadows.
- **Classic** (1.1's default; the one meter from Phase 67 until the lens, gone in Phase 71's last step with `meter/MeterLooks.kt`, `MeterFeels.CLASSIC` and the text `meter_cents_label`, "CENTS", in every language): the dial with the needle, slightly underdamped (1.6 Hz, damping 0.6), so it swung ~9 % past a new string like a mechanical needle ([history](history.md), *Meter, wave and styles*).
- **The Light arc** (`LightArc.kt`, `LightArcHeat.kt`; nothing drew it from Phase 67 until the lens's Light arc, `LightArcLook`, reused it in Phase 72 with its fan fitted to the lens and its lights in the reading's tone: [Tuner screen](ui-tuner.md), *Lens*; what follows is 1.1's meter, its sizes in the 2:1 box's units): a fan of little lights like an LED pedal's, no dial and no numbers. 17 rays, evenly spaced (±1.2 rad at the ends), one every 6.25 cents of the stretched scale (`lightArcRayPlace`), so the rays out from the middle are reached at about 2.7, 5.9, 9.7, 14.3, 20, 27.3, 36.8 and 50 cents. They rise from a pivot 6u under the box, each of 5 lights 78u to 148u out (3.4u to 5.6u across), unlit in the track colour (75 % dark, 70 % light) like dark LEDs. The rays from the centre out to the reading light as a wedge (`lightArcGoal`): the reading's ray all five lights at full, rays nearer the centre fewer (down to 1.6) and dimmer (to 40 %); the ray just past the reading lights part of the way, so the wedge grows smoothly. A lit light is the accent (a little whiter in dark), 8 % larger, with a halo 3.4× its radius. The middle ray is the centre mark in its own layer: its two inner lights at 45 % and the rest at 14 % off the note, all five in the centre colour as it's reached, and the rays beside it light half way. Tiny ink dots 12u past the outer lights mark the rays nearest 5 cents (1.8u, ink 55 %) and 10 cents (1.3u, 32 %) each side (`lightArcMarks`); the in-tune window's edge gets a strong dot too, so with Precise the first ray (2.7 cents) is marked as well. They are static, drawn over the lit layer so a halo never dims them, and made again only when the window changes. `LightArcHeat` (one preallocated array of 85) gives a 0.12 s fading trail (`TRAIL_SECONDS`); its frame loop (`withFrameNanos`) runs only while a light is still changing, then waits on `snapshotFlow`, so a steady reading moves nothing and UI tests settle. Its spring (`MeterFeels.LIGHT_ARC`) follows almost without swinging (2.2 Hz, damping 0.9). `LightArcHeatTest` and `MeterFeelTest` keep it tested.
- **The in-tune moment** (owner: smooth, not gimmicky): a soft glow eases in with the haptic tick, holds while the note rings and fades when it leaves. One glow (owner, 2026-11-02): 1.1's Calm; Bold went in Phase 67 and a saved `in_tune_effect` is left unread.
  - *When:* `InTuneGlowRule` (pure) lights it on the update that raises `Detected.inTune`, never restarts it while the note holds, and bridges a dropout of the same note for 600 ms (shorter than the tick's 1.5 s, so a bridged dropout never ticks twice). Another note or drifting out of tune puts it out at once, and so does the same note coming back while the hint asks to tune it (a re-pluck often starts sharp), so the glow never lights with "Tune down" on screen. `rememberInTuneGlow` runs it in `TunerScreen` (saved over rotation) → `TunerContent(meterGlow)` → `MeterFrame(glow)`, eased 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out.
  - *Remove animations:* `rememberAnimationsOff()` (`:core:designsystem`, `AnimationsOff.kt`, shared with Settings' look cards) reads `ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE` and follows changes; at 0 `tuned` and `glow` snap.
  - *Drawing:* the shared `TuneGlow`, a `GlowArt` in the "in tune" colour (32 % dark, 16 % light at its middle, `tuneGlowAlpha`) drawn once in its own layer under the needle; `glow` only sets the layer's alpha (`CompositingStrategy.ModulateAlpha`), so easing it redraws and allocates nothing. Never clipped: it fades out past the box, behind the controls around the meter.
- **Meter in Settings** (2.0, Phase 72; Settings offered no meter, glow, display or wave look from Phase 67 until then): in the *Look* group under light or dark (since Phase 75; the *Tuner screen* group's only row until then), *Meter*, three cards (`MeterPicker`, `components/MeterPicker.kt`, on `LookCardGrid`): *Trail · Light arc · Strobe* (Dutch *Toonlijn · Lichtboog · Stroboscoop*), the saved `lensLook` ringed, a tap saving it (`SettingsViewModel.setLensLook`, which also ends the 1.1 rule, *Settings from 1.1*). Each card is the tuner's lens made smaller: its face, its border in the reading's tone and the look drawn by the tuner's own `LensLookArt`, laid out 240 dp wide at the lens's 0.85 and drawn scaled to the card (so the lights, bands and line keep the tuner's proportions; no ♭ ♯, no glow round it), from one demo `LensReading` (`DemoLensReading`) on 1.1's `MeterDemo` loop, back from Phase 67 (flat, tuned up, in tune, sharp, tuned down, in tune), which also feeds a `PitchHistory` at the tuner's frame rate so the trail falls as in the tuner; the tone is the tuner's (`readingToneShare`) with the in-tune window of the precision setting. Calm: the loop runs only while a card is on screen, the demo has no reading while none is (so the strobe's own loop rests too, `LookDemo.onScreenChanged`), and with "Remove animations" (and in UI tests) the cards hold still on the in-tune moment (`LookDemo.stillSeconds`, 4.4 s in), where the strobe stands still and asks for no frames, the trail rests and the Light arc settles. The trail's brake (`ToolSwitches.pitchTrail`, `SettingsScreen(trailLook)`): off, only the Light arc and the strobe, a saved trail ringed as the Light arc (`LensLook.offered`, `LensLook.shown`). The grid keeps 1.1's: the ring on the picked card, names on one line shrinking to 6 sp, one selectable radio group for TalkBack, test tags (`lensLookCardTag`), the still picture (`holdStill`, `HoldStillTest`) and the cards per row (`lookCardsPerRow`, `LookCardsPerRowTest`). Tests: JVM `MeterDemoTest`, `ToolSwitchesTest`, `SettingsViewModelTest`; UI `SettingsContentTest` (the three cards as radio buttons under Meter, the saved one picked, a tap reporting its look, the brake), `SettingsStylesTest` (their names wholly shown at 2× text, every language in the full check). Phone check (Xiaomi 12X, 2026-11-03, the synthetic-tone build): the cards moving in light and dark, each look picked and the tuner following.
- **Tests:** JVM: `MeterReadingTest` (with the in-tune window), `MeterFeelTest`, `MeterStretchTest`, `LightArcHeatTest`, `FanGeometryTest`, `TuneGlowTest`, `RestingCentreTest`, `StrobeTest`, `StrobeLookTest`, `InTuneGlowRuleTest`, `NumberFitTest`. UI: `CompactLandscapeStylesTest` (the meter covers its box, clearly drawn, skipped by TalkBack, on its side, also at 2× font), `TunerContentTest` (TalkBack skips the meter and hears the reading once), `TunerStylesTest` (the meter keeps its full width). `MeterLooksTest` and `MeterLookScreenshots` went with the looks; the lens's looks get `LensLooksTest` (Phases 71, 72).
- **Pictures for the owner:** `scripts/style-screens.sh` (~1 min) runs `StyleScreenshots` (`-a styleScreenshots=true`): the whole tuner screen with the A string in tune, light and dark, to `build/owner-screens/styles/` (1.1's `scripts/meter-look-screens.sh` went with the looks).

## Meter art language
The meter is drawn "as if by the same artist" as the rest (owner, after two rounds of moving mock-ups, October 2026). The approved mock-ups are the reference: [docs/design/1.x/meter-looks-preview.html](../design/1.x/meter-looks-preview.html) and, for the open face (no plate), [docs/design/1.x/theme-styles-preview.html](../design/1.x/theme-styles-preview.html); the lens's own rules are in the [2.0 plan](v2-plan.md), *The lens*. *u* = box width / 340, about 1 dp on a phone.
- **Colours:** roles only, from the theme: background (stage), onSurface (ink), onSurfaceVariant (muted), outlineVariant (track), primary (accent) and the "in tune" colour. No ornaments. Minor ticks are ink at 50 % and numbers ink at 80 % (`MINOR_TICK_INK`, `SCALE_NUMBER_INK`), so they keep 3:1 and 4.5:1 on the background.
- **Font:** the number face (`NumberFont`, Nunito since Phase 79; the phone's plain sans from Phase 67), with lining figures (`LINING_FIGURES`).
- **Plate:** none. No plate, frame or drop shadow: the meter sits straight on the screen's background. The dial (Classic) has a 146u radius around a pivot 17u above the box's bottom.
- **Field:** instead, a calm field: a soft oval wash of the background colour, 190u × 150u each way round a point 47u above the box's bottom middle, 75 % in its middle, 56 % at 62 % of the way out, 0 at its edge, no edge line.
- **Scale** (the open face's larger one): a tick every 5 cents, none at 0. Major (every 10) 13u × 2.1u in ink; minor 7u × 1.3u at 50 %; round caps. Numbers 10–50 without sign, 12.5u, ink at 80 %, centred 27u from the tick's base, at least 8u apart (`numberFit`). The dial's ticks start 4u inside its arc.
- **♭ and ♯:** inside the box, in muted, at the ends of the scale (`SignsArt`, 20u in the music-sign face): under the dial's end ticks (fitted between them and the box's bottom), in the Light arc's lower corners. No row of signs under the box, so the meter takes 2:1 and nothing more.
- **Centre:** one 3.5u rounded bar in the accent.
- **Marker** (the needle, the Light arc's lights): a small soft shadow (3u blur, 1.5u right, 2.5u down).
- **Light:** one glow only, radial, in the "in tune" colour (32 % dark, 16 % light at its middle, fading to 0), never clipped by the box.
- **At rest:** the centre (Classic's centre bar, the Light arc's middle ray) is the muted colour without a note and fades to the accent with `presence` (`MeterRoles.centre(tuned, presence)`, `RestingCentreTest`): with wallpaper colours the accent could be green (before Phase 43's guard; a teal just past 40° still can come near), and a resting meter must never look in tune.
- **In tune:** one eased amount (smoothstep, 0.4 s in, 0.6 s out) blends centre mark and needle to "in tune"; the glow eases the same way. Both follow the steady hint's decision; with "Remove animations" both switch at once. Nothing flashes, bursts, flies, pulses or repeats.
- **Motion:** springs with a damping ratio of 0.5 or more.
- **Reading to the cent** (Phase 60, [docs/design/1.x/meter-precision-preview.html](../design/1.x/meter-precision-preview.html)): Classic reads best because its needle tip moves ~4.3u per cent, it has a tick every 5 cents to compare against, and a thin needle meets a thin centre bar. So a look without a numbered scale uses the stretched middle (`meterStretch`), a few fixed marks without numbers, thin edges instead of soft blobs, and shows the in-tune window (`MeterReading.inTuneWindow`): the Light arc's stretched rays and its marks at 5 and 10 cents; the lens's trail and strobe follow the same rule.

## Strobe
1.1's strobe wave look, kept for the lens's strobe (Phase 72): nothing draws it since Phase 67 took the wave view out of the tuner, and `dial/Strobe.kt` with `StrobeTest` stays. `drawStrobe(phase, color, glow)` draws `STROBE_ROWS` rows of even stripes (`STRIPE_PERIOD`, 9 dp) in rounded bars, each row twice as fast as the one above (`phase · 2`), like the rings of a strobe disc; the marks fade over the outer fifth at each end (`strobeFade`, by position only), and every stripe looks the same, so the pattern wraps from 1 back to 0 without a jump (`stripeOffset`). The phase comes from `WaveMotion` (`:core:dsp` `strobe/`, which runs `StrobeMotion`: still within half a cent, drifting right when sharp and left when flat, faster further off; [Notes, tunings and audio](notes-and-audio.md), *Strobe model*), and the watch's ribbon uses the same motion. Phase 72 gives it three bands sized from the lens and its own frame loop ([2.0 plan](v2-plan.md), *The lens*). The wave view, the ribbon and the other wave looks are in [history](history.md).

## Visual styles
**One look, Ink** (2.0, Phase 67; [2.0 plan](v2-plan.md), *The lens*): 1.1's six styles (Ancient, Classic, Retro, Contemporary, Futuristic, Space Age; [history](history.md), *Meter, wave and styles*) went, with the style setting and its card grid in Settings. A 1.1 install's saved `visual_style` is left in the file unread, so every style opens as Ink (see [Lifecycle](lifecycle.md), *Saved settings*). Light or dark (`ThemeMode`: the phone's, light, dark) and the wallpaper colours (`dynamicColor`, Android 12+) stay as Settings' Look group.
- **Palette** (`StylePalettes.kt`): Ink's light and dark `Palette` from the mock-ups (`docs/design/v2-one-look-preview.html`, `v2-tuner-screens-preview.html`; the plan's colour table): background, surface (the lens's face), ink, muted, track (the mock-ups' "line": the lens's rest border, the scale's arc), the blue accent + onAccent, inTune + onInTune (green), `farOff` (amber, a note far off: the lens's tone eases from it to green in Phase 71; never text) and `second`/`third` (drawings only), turned into a Material 3 `ColorScheme` as before: primary = accent; primary and secondary containers (selected segments, chips, the slider's track) = surface mixed 22 % (light) / 32 % (dark) with the accent; outline = muted, outlineVariant = track. Dark is the mock-ups' as drawn. **Light darkened to 4.5:1** (Phase 67): the mock-ups' light muted `#6B7482`, accent `#1E7FE0` and green `#15A35A` came to only 4.2, 3.6 and 2.9 to 1 as text on the surface container, so each is darkened in its own hue just enough (`#666F7C`, `#1A6FC4`, `#107E46`). **Muted at 7:1** (the owner's review, 2026-11-03: "Play a string" and Settings' small lines were hard to read on a dimmed screen): muted is `#A8B0BC` in dark (8.7:1 on the background, was `#8D96A3`, 6.3) and `#4D5560` in light (7.0:1, was `#666F7C`), `MUTED_TEXT`; the widget's and the watch's copies follow. `StylePaletteTest` holds both to WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for ink, muted, accent and inTune on the background, surface and surface container, for onAccent/onInTune on their fills and for text on every container; the meter's numbers (ink at 80 %) 4.5:1 and its minor ticks (ink at 50 %), centre mark and outlines 3:1; the accent never green, the in-tune colour green, the far-off colour amber and nothing red. The see-through controls' outline is the muted colour at 80 % in light (60 % in dark; 1.1's 75 % gave Ink 2.97:1), `controlOutline`. The watch copies the dark palette (`WatchColors`, `WatchDialTest`).
- **Wallpaper colours** (`dynamicColor`, Android 12+ for every install; off by default since the owner's review of 2026-11-03, on before; in 1.1 Contemporary's only, so a 1.1 player who switched them on keeps them): their muted text is taken toward their ink until it reads at 7:1 on the background and surface (`withReadableMuted`, as Ink's own muted); `colorSchemeFor` takes Material You's scheme for the background, surfaces, text and accent; the in-tune green and the far-off amber are never the wallpaper's, they come from `StyleTokens`, so a resting meter never looks in tune. **The greens stay free** (Phase 43): a green wallpaper gave Material You a green primary, so picked segments, the open tab, the metronome's Start and the strum check's marks looked "in tune". `colorSchemeFor` passes the wallpaper scheme through `keepingInTuneApart`: when its primary lies within 50° (`IN_TUNE_HUE_GUARD`) of Ink's in-tune hue, measured as OKLCH hue (`hueOf`, so a yellowish and a bluish green are as far apart as they look), primary, onPrimary, surfaceTint and inversePrimary become Ink's own blue, and the primary and secondary containers are the wallpaper's surface mixed with that blue as the palettes mix theirs, with the wallpaper's onSurface on them. Backgrounds, surfaces and text stay the wallpaper's; any other accent is left alone. Light and dark each compare with their own green; the widget follows because it draws from `colorSchemeFor`. `WallpaperColorsTest`: green, yellow-green, mint and teal wallpapers fall back, readable (4.5:1) on the wallpaper's grounds; blue, violet, red, orange and yellow keep theirs.
- **Lettering** (`StyleTypography.kt`): since Phase 79 (2026-11-05, the owner's pick after a branding review) Twangle's own face, **Nunito** (`TwangleFont`), for everything, display and headline styles light (`inkTypography`); readouts of numbers in the same face (`NumberFont`), and the drawings that measure their own text (the tuner's ruler) name it too, as a text measurer's default is the phone's font. Nunito's rounded ends go with the name's Comfortaa and the icon's harmonics while staying sturdy and open at small sizes, and the app now looks the same on every brand of phone (Phase 67 to 79 it took the phone's own plain sans, `FontFamily.Default`: MiSans on the Xiaomi, One UI Sans on the Galaxy). One variable file, `res/font/nunito.ttf` (about 277 KB, unchanged from google/fonts `ofl/nunito`, SIL OFL 1.1 with its licence in `assets/licenses/OFL-nunito.txt`, credited on the About page), one `Font` per weight from 200 to 900 through its weight axis. It covers the Latin, Cyrillic and Vietnamese letters (17 of the 25 languages); Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali and Thai letters come from the phone's own font glyph by glyph, as Android does for any face, and so do the few signs Nunito lacks (→, ⌄). Its figures are lining and all one width (600 units), so a changing number never shifts. The candidates weighed in the review: Manrope (crisp, same coverage, but no echo of the round name), Figtree, Lexend and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (no Cyrillic). The watch app and the home-screen widget keep the phone's font. Every text style asks for lining figures (`fontFeatureSettings = "lnum"`, `LINING_FIGURES`). With the six styles' faces gone (Forum, Alegreya Sans, Playfair Display, Bodoni Moda, Righteous, Orbitron, Michroma, about 1.15 MB), so went the per-language face rules (`facesFor`) and `StyleFontCoverageTest`; the one bundled font is `music_signs.ttf`, credited on the About page (`bundledFontCredits`).
- **Music signs** (`MusicSigns.kt`): the phone's font has no ♯ or ♭ of a steady size (1.1's six faces each drew them differently), so every ♯ ♭ ♮ ♩ ♪ comes from `music_signs.ttf` (Noto Sans Symbols cut to those glyphs, 2.5 KB, `scripts/subset-font.py`). The cut is renamed "Twangle Music Signs" inside the file, without Noto's trademark line: a changed font is a Modified Version under the OFL and "Noto" is Google's trademark. Copyright, licence and designer records stay, and About still credits Noto Sans Symbols. `musicText()` sets a sign after a letter like a chord symbol (0.7 em and raised; ♭ 0.8 em). The text itself is unchanged, so UI tests see "C♯"; TalkBack hears words via `NoteNameSpeech`.
- **Shapes** (`InkShapes`): `small` 18 dp (the cents pill), `medium` 12 dp (look cards and tips), `large` 22 dp (the lens and the sheets' top corners, the mock-ups'); Material's own for the rest. The buttons (`StyleButton`, `ButtonLook`) and the string buttons (`StringChipShape`, 40 dp round, `StringChipSize`) are circles, the segmented switch a capsule; in the tuner each string button sits in a touch target of at least 48 dp each way (`minimumInteractiveComponentSize`), and the compact row's small ones shrink to fit 32 dp. Rows of 7 or 8 strings that don't fit shrink every button alike (see [Tuner screen](ui-tuner.md), *String row*).
- **Background** (`StyleBackdrop`): drawn by `MainActivity` behind every see-through screen (`Scaffold(containerColor = Transparent, contentColor = onBackground)`): Ink's plain background colour, which fades with the rest of the scheme; no picture. Phase 71 lays the tuner's tint toward the reading's tone over it.
- **The meter and the strobe** draw 1.1's Contemporary finish without the styles' own (the bar needle, the dot hub, the thin arc; rounded bars for the strobe): *Meter* and *Strobe* above.
- **Navigation mark** (`NavMark`): a rounded 20 × 4 dp bar in the accent under the open tab's name (and in Listen's balloon).
- **Press effect everywhere** (owner's request 2026-10-15: "all buttons should have the feedback effect when tapped"): every button, row and item of the phone app lights up on touch-down (the accent at 20 %; 1.1's styles each had their own light), stays lit while held and fades over 280 ms, the metronome's `pressFeedback` ([Metronome, widget and watch](ui-other-screens.md), *Metronome screen*), instead of Material's ripple. `TwangleTheme` provides `LocalIndication = PressIndication.Row` and `LocalRippleConfiguration = null`, so:
  - **Rows, list and menu items** (Settings' switch, choice and link rows, About's credits, the language list, the tuning panel's tuning rows and New tuning, the strum panel, the ⋮ menu's items through `TwangleMenuItem`) take the theme's light from a plain `clickable`/`selectable`/`toggleable`: `PressIndication`, an `IndicationNodeFactory` that reuses `pressFeedback`'s node with no sink (only the tint), across the row's bounds and clipped by the row's own clip. The navigation bar's and rail's items take it in the theme's `medium` shape, no sink.
  - **Material's buttons** draw no ripple once `LocalRippleConfiguration` is null, and give no other way in, so the screens use `TwangleTextButton`, `TwangleButton`, `TwangleIconButton`, `TwangleFilledTonalIconButton` (`PressButtons.kt`): Material's own button, look and TalkBack, with `pressFeedback` on the same interaction source (and `pressFlash()` in its click, for TalkBack's double tap). Material sets its 48 dp touch area *outside* the modifier it is given, which would light the touch area rather than the 40 dp button, so these turn Material's off (`OwnTouchArea`: `LocalMinimumInteractiveComponentSize` 0 inside) and set the same area round the light themselves (`Modifier.touchArea`, Compose still widens touches to it); nothing moves. A text or icon button's light lies under its text; a filled button (Next, Allow microphone, Retry, the editor's − / +) draws its face as content, so its light lies over it (`above`) in a flat tone that never hides the text (`ACCENT_FILL`: on-accent at 20 %; Listen's balloon: Skip `IN_ACCENT`, Use `IN_ACCENT_FILLED`). The tuner's mode switch (Material's `SegmentedButton`) does the same in place.
  - **Custom faces with a shape** (the string buttons, the tuning panel's instrument chips, the strum check switch, the look cards, unused until Phase 72; and the tuning's name above the strings, which as a quiet label lights with the tint only) sink as well: `Modifier.pressSink(source)` before the face (the whole button, fill and outline included, drawn smaller about its middle) and `PressIndication(shape)` on its clickable or after its last border, so the light lies over the fill and under the name (over the preview for the look cards, `above`).
  - Not changed: the A4 slider (a drag control; its thumb has Material's own pressed look, without the ripple), the switches inside rows (the row takes the touch; the radio buttons went in Phase 63, the choice rows have the press effect), the watch app (`:wear`, Wear Compose with its own press look, no shared theme) and the home-screen widget (Android views, no Compose). `PressFeedbackTest` (JVM) checks the touch area and the lights' equality; `PressEffectTest` (`:feature:settings`, UI, light and dark) holds each kind of button down, checks it lights and fades back, clicks once by touch and by TalkBack, keeps 48 dp, and a text button's light stays on its pill.

`TwangleTheme(darkTheme, dynamicColor)` provides the `ColorScheme`, `Typography`, `Shapes` and `LocalStyleTokens` (`StyleTokens`: dark or not, the "in tune" pair, the far-off amber, the unfaded scheme), remembered per brightness. A brightness change (or the wallpaper's) fades every colour over 450 ms (one `Animatable`).
