The ruler as the tuning needle

The tone sweeps slowly from 40 cents flat to in tune and back. Each phone moves the lens down into the empty band. A only makes the ruler bigger. B also zooms in near the note: the last 20 cents get three times the room, and small marks every 5 cents fade in around the pointer.
