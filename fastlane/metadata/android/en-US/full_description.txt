Twangle is a guitar tuner you can use without looking. Play a string and the meter shows the note and how far off it is, to a single cent. Turn on the vibration guide and your phone taps once to tighten and twice to loosen, quicker as you get close, then gives one long buzz when the string is in tune. On a Wear OS watch the taps come on your wrist.

Everything in Twangle is free: no ads, no account, no in-app purchases and no tracking. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet, so the sound from your microphone is analysed live on your phone and never recorded or sent anywhere.

It is a bass tuner, ukulele tuner, violin tuner and chromatic tuner too, and once you're in tune it helps you play: a metronome, chord charts, scales, a capo helper and ear training.

TUNE ANY INSTRUMENT
• Guitar (6, 7, 8 or 12 strings), bass (4 or 5 strings, down to the low B), ukulele (low G and baritone too), violin, viola, cello, mandolin and 5-string banjo
• Guitar tunings: Standard, Drop D, half step down, full step down, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, Open E and Open C
• Make your own tunings for any instrument
• Chromatic tuner for any other instrument or your voice
• Hold a string to hear its note and tune by ear
• Set A4 from 415 to 466 Hz, for example 442 Hz to play with an orchestra

TUNE WITHOUT LOOKING
• Vibration guide: one tap to tighten, two to loosen, a long buzz when in tune
• Sound guide: short sounds while you tune, up when too high, down when too low, a bell when in tune
• TalkBack says what to do first ("Tighten, E 2, 12 cents flat"), reads chord charts and scales string by string, and never talks over your string
• Every screen works with large text, up to twice the normal size, and a high-contrast look

ON YOUR WATCH
• A Wear OS watch app with a tile and a watch-face complication: tune with the watch's own microphone, no phone needed, feel the vibration guide on your wrist, and swipe left for a metronome that taps the beat

LISTENING TOOLS
• 12th-fret check: play a string open and at the 12th fret, and learn whether its saddle should move, which way, and on a guitar about how far
• Pitch trail: see how steady a held note, a bend or your vibrato is, for singers too
• Chord name: strum a chord on guitar, ukulele or mandolin and see its name
• Strum check: strum all strings at once and see which ones are flat, sharp or in tune

PRACTICE
• Metronome: 30 to 250 beats per minute on a tempo ruler you slide, with tap tempo
• 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8 and 7/8, with an accent on the first beat, lights to follow along, and a vibration on the beat if you like
• Between the beats: extra clicks in eighths, triplets or sixteenths
• Speed trainer: the tempo climbs step by step to your goal
• Chord charts for your instrument and tuning, your own tunings included
• Scales on the fretboard: major, minor, pentatonic and blues, every note named
• Capo helper: pick the song's key and the shapes you like, and see where the capo goes
• Ear training: Play it back (play the notes you hear on your instrument), Intervals and Chord type

MAKE IT YOURS
• In 36 languages, with note names the way you learned them: C D E, with H for B, or Do Re Mi
• Light, dark, or following your phone, with your wallpaper's colours on Android 12 and newer
• Three meters to choose from: the classic needle, the light arc or the strobe
• Focus mode: the tuner full screen on a phone on its side, easy to read from a distance
• A home-screen widget that opens the tuner with one tap, and the screen stays on while you tune

Twangle will stay free and offline, for good.
