Phase 55 · rough mock-up

Ear training

One Practice tool with three exercises: Play it back, Intervals and Chord type. A tool, not a game: no scores kept, only a quiet tally for this visit. Where there is a real choice there are options A and B, the one I'd pick marked. Tap a ▶ to hear it (a short tone that stops by itself); the answer buttons in section 3 work.

1. Finding it and picking an exercise

2. One question of each

3. Right and wrong

4. The tally, and a missed question coming back

5. Choosing what is asked

Questions for you

  1. Picking the exercise: A, three icons in a row; or B, a second drop-down button with the name.
  2. Play it back, while you play: A, the note the tuner hears is shown as you play; or B, only "listening" until you hold a note, then right or not yet.
  3. After an answer: A, the next question comes by itself after about 1½ seconds; or B, a Next button.
  4. A missed question coming back: A, no mark, it is simply asked again; or B, a small "again" over the ▶.
  5. Choosing what is asked: A, questions start at once and a button opens the choices any time; or B, a short start page with the choices and a Start button each visit.

Not shown: every language and 2× text, the other five styles, the phone on its side. Those come when it is built. The roadmap says sixteen chord types; the Chords tool has twenty since Phase 49, so the choice offers all twenty.
