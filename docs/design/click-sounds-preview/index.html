Metronome click sounds

Four instruments for the metronome's Sound button, each in its own register. Beat 1 is louder and higher, as on the wood block. Each plays alone (one bar) and under a strummed guitar (two bars), then stops.

Wood block today's click, the default

A short, woody "tock" in the middle register.

Cowbell

A metal bell, lower than the wood block, that clangs and rings on a little: the classic studio cue.

Claves

Two rosewood sticks struck together: a high, clear, glassy "tick" that rings briefly and sits above the guitar.

Rim click

A drummer's stick cracking on the snare's rim: dry and noisy, with a small thump of the drum under it.

All four are about equally loud on their own. In the guitar versions the guitar is clearly louder than the click, as when practising. The guitar is synthetic (the app's own reference tones), so judge the click, not the guitar.
