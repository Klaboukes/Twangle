Metronome click sounds

The four sounds for the metronome's Sound button. Each one has a note of its own, and beat 1 jumps an octave and is louder, as on today's wood block. Each plays alone (one bar) and under a strummed guitar (two bars), then stops.

Wood block today's click, the default

The everyday click: a short, woody "tock".

Rim click

A drummer's dry cross-stick on the snare's rim, the stick ringing briefly: precise, like playing along with a drummer.

Cowbell

A small, choked cowbell with a clear note, beat 1 on its smaller twin: the studio click that cuts through a band.

Bright tick

A high, crisp metal tick with a small glint of a note, for loud or distorted guitar.

All four are about equally loud on their own. In the guitar versions the guitar is clearly louder than the click, as when practising. The guitar is synthetic (the app's own reference tones), so judge the click, not the guitar.
